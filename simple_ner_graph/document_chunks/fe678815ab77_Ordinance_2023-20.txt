--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2023-20 - 06_13_2023.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:16:35 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## ORDINANCE NO. 2023-20 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA, AMENDING CHAPTER 34 - ' NUISANCES,' SECTION 34-105 ' ABANDONED OR JUNK MOTOR VEHICLES ON PUBLIC OR PRIVATE PROPERTY PROHIBITED; EXCEPTIONS,' TO PROHIBIT THE PARKING, STORING, OR LEAVING OF VEHICLES UNDER CAR COVERS ON CITY SWALE AREAS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY, REPEALER, CODIFICATION, AND AN EFFECTIVE DATE. WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Coral Gables wishes to minimize traffic hazards and public nuisances within the City and to ensure the safety of its residents and visitors; and WHEREAS, the City Code of the City of Coral Gables regulates where vehicles may be parked and how they may be stored, specifically regulating the number of vehicles that shall be allowed on private property under form-fitting car covers with clips or drawstrings; and WHEREAS, the City Code does not currently contemplate the use of form-fitting car covers within the City' s rights- of-way such as the swale areas; and WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that the use of form-fitting car covers in the City rights-of-way poses multiple issues for the City, including but not limited to: detracting from the overall appearance of the City, making the identification of a vehicle' s license plate, its make and model, and its condition more difficult for Code Enforcement and Police, and car covers can collect water and debris, which can create a breeding ground for mosquitoes and other pest; and WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that this Ordinance serves a public purpose by preventing neighborhood blight and the deterioration of neighborhood character and is necessary for the preservation of the quality of life and the public health, safety, and welfare of City residents and visitors; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That Chapter 34 'Nuisance,' Article IV. 'Lost, stolen, junked, and abandoned property,' Division 2 ' Vehicles,' Section 34-105 ' Abandoned or junk motor vehicles on public or private property prohibited; exceptions' of the Code of the City of Coral Gables, Florida is amended as follows1: ## CHAPTER 34 ## ARTICLE IV. - LOST, STOLEN, JUNKED, AND ABANDONED PROPERTY ## DIVISION 2. VEHICLES Sec. 34-105. - Abandoned or junk motor vehicles on public or private property prohibited; exceptions. - a)It shall be unlawful for any person to park, store, or leave any motor or other vehicle maintained on cement blocks, with flat tires, partially dismantled, not properly registered or insured, or in a wrecked, junked, dilapidated or abandoned condition on public or private property in the city: - 1)Unless it is in connection with a purpose or business enterprise lawfully situated and licensed. - 2)Or if the vehicle is kept on private property, the vehicle shall be kept under a formfitting car cover, with clips or drawstrings, that does not retain water, and is free of tears, holes, dirt, discoloration, fading or cracking. - 3)A maximum of two vehicles shall be allowed under car covers on or within the property lines on any private property within the city and must be parked on an approved parking surface as provided in the city zoning code. - b) It shall be unlawful for any person to park, store, or leave any motor or other vehicle under a car cover in the swale area. - 1) A ten ( 10) day grace period shall be granted for any permitted construction, tree pruning, or similar work on the abutting residential property which prevents the use of the approved parking surfaces on the property. - 2) This section does not apply to vehicles parked wholly within the improved surfaces of a driveway approach if the car cover is form fitting and does not retain water. - 3) No blocking of the sidewalk is permitted. 1 Deletions indicated in strikethrough and additions indicated in underline.
