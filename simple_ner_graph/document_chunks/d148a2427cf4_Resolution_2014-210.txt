That $ 350,000.00 from the Capital Improvement Program, that has already been approved for the " Coral Gables Merrick House," be allocated for the City to apply for a Special Category Matching Grant from Florida Department of State Division of Historical Resources for the restoration and rehabilitation ofthe " Coral Gables Merrick House" located at 907 Coral Way. SECTION 2. That this resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FOURTEENTH DAY OF OCTOBER A.D., 2014.
