City of Coral Gables Election, which was held on April 11, 2023, wherein voters elected Ariel Fernandez, to hold office as City Commissioner, Group V, for a term of four years; said term to begin at noon on the third day after the election, until noon on the third day after the General Biennial Election scheduled for April 13, 2027.
