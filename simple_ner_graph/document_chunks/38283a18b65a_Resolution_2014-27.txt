--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-27 - 02_11_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 00:35:14 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 27 RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING ENCUMBRANCE IN THE AMOUNT OF 8,748.15 OR 15 PERCENT OF THE STATE FORFEITED ASSET FUND F.A.F.) MONEYS TO PROVIDE FOR THE SUPPORT AND OPERATION OF CRIME PREVENTION, DRUG ABUSE PREVENTION, SAFE NEIGHBORHOOD, OR SCHOOL RESOURCE OFFICER PROGRAMS, AS REQUIRED UNDER THE FLORIDA CONTRABAND FORFEITURE ACT. WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Police Department's investigations of criminal activity produce forfeited funds, both related and unrelated to its participation in the South Florida Money Laundering Strike Force operations; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Police Department may lawfully retain at the conclusion of the legal proceedings the forfeited funds as authorized under Florida State Statute Section 932.7055; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Chief of Police, Dennis L. Weiner and the Coral Gables Finance Director, Diana Gomez, attest to have deposited the sum of $ 8,748.15 in said forfeited funds into the Coral Gables Forfeited Asset Fund; and WHEREAS, the F.A.F. Committee met on January 29, 2014, and followed the F.A.F . Committee operating procedures adopted on July 23,2002; and WHEREAS, the F.A.F. Committee approved the Coral Gables Chief ofPolice's request to encumber the available funds on the specific items listed below on this resolution; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The forgoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption herein; and SECTION 2. That the City Commission does hereby direct the City Manager to authorize the Finance Director to encumber $ 8,748.15 from the State Forfeited Asset Fund moneys to provide for the support and operation of crime prevention, drug abuse education and prevention, safe neighborhood, or youth resource officer programs as required by FSS 932.7055 ofthe Florida Contraband Forfeiture Act. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS ELEVENTH DAY OF FEBRUARY A.D., 2014 .
