( LEGAL DESCRIPTION ON FILE AT THE CITY) WHEREAS, an application was submitted in connection with a project known as Paseo de la Riviera" (" Project') requesting a change from Commercial Low - Rise Intensity to Commercial High - Rise Intensity land use designations on a property generally bounded by US 1 to the north, Madruga Avenue to the south, the Gables One Tower to the east, and Caballero Boulevard to the west, generally known as 1350 South Dixie Highway, Coral Gables, Florida, as legally described on Exhibit " A;" ( the " Subject Property") and WHEREAS, the application qualified for review as a small scale map amendment in accordance with Section 163. 3187, Florida Statutes; and WHEREAS, companion applications for Paseo de la Riviera were filed seeking approval of a Planned Area Development (" PAD"), a text amendment to the City of Coral Gables Official Zoning Code by Amending Appendix A, " Site Specific Zoning Regulations," Section A- 83, " Riviera Section Part 8," and a Mixed -Use Conditional Use site plan; and WHEREAS, the Subject Property is within 1, 000 feet of the University Metrorail Station, part of a regional commuter rail system that connects to Brickell, Downtown Miami, and the Miami International Airport; and WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables Comprehensive Plan establishes a goal of growth in proximity to transit stations; and WHEREAS, the Subject Property is located on South Dixie Highway - a major arterial roadway; and WHEREAS, low-rise apartment buildings, duplexes, a park, and single- family homes are located to the south and west of the subject property; and WHEREAS, through the public hearing process, it was determined that a change oflanduse from "Commercial Low-Rise Intensity" to " Mixed- Use" would be more appropriate and less intense for the subject property, rather than a change to " Commercial High - Rise Intensity," and would be a more effective category forachieving the City' s vision for a mixed› use, transit- oriented destination that is in harmony with the low-rise apartment buildings, duplexes andsingle- family homes to the south and west; and WHEREAS, according to Table FLU-4 " Mixed-Use Land Use" of the Comprehensive Plan, maximum density, intensity and height for the Mixed- Use Land Use Classification are provided in the Zoning Code, and in this case are implemented by Zoning Code Appendix A, "Site Specific Zoning Regulations," Section A-83, " Riviera Section Part 8," as well as by Zoning Code Article 3, " Development Review," Division 5 " Planned Area Development ( PAD)," andthe Planned Area Development site plan; and WHEREAS, the Project consists ofa mix ofuses including hotel, residential, retail, commercial, and restaurant is proposed to be constructed on the subject property, and the project requires new land use classifications; and WHEREAS, the Applicant has also submitted an application for Zoning Code text amendments to Appendix A, "Site Specific Zoning Regulations," Section A-83, " Riviera Section Part 8," by modifying provisions restricting FAR, height, and setbacks for the property, which the applicant seeks to utilize in the design and development ofthe Paseo de la Riviera project;
