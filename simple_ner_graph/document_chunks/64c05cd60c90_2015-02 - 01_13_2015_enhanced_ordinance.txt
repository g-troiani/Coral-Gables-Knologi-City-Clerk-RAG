WHEREAS, the City's scholarship fund, now known as the Donald R. Hopkins and William A. Cooper Scholarship Program, was founded in 1989 to assist student residents in meeting the financial demands of higher education; and WHEREAS, the scholarship program has thus far been administered by an informal committee comprised of scholarship founder, Commissioner Jim Barker, William Cooper ( deceased), Leona Cooper, Edwina Prime and Leona Cooper Baker; and WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to formalize the administration of the Donald R. Hopkins and William A. Cooper Scholarship Program; and WHEREAS, the scholarship is based on several factors, including residency in the MacFarlane Homestead or Golden Gate neighborhoods, financial need, academic performance, enrollment in Coral Gables Senior High School and a minimum 2. 5 GPA; and WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables has a unique opportunity to assist scholarship recipients in attaining their educational and career goals; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. Incorporation. That the foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2. Creation and Purpose. That the Hopkins/ Cooper Scholarship Committee is hereby created for the purpose of administering the Donald R. Hopkins and William A. Cooper Scholarship Program. The Committee shall make non -binding recommendations for award recipients, in addition to making recommendations with regard to ( i) improving the administration of the scholarship program, ( ii) developing a plan for increasing the awareness of the program among eligible students, ( iii) resolving questions that might arise about the program and ( iii) modifying and updating the scholarship criteria as appropriate. SECTION 3. Qualifications. That Hopkins/ Cooper Scholarship Committee Members shall have their primary place of residence within the boundaries of the City. SECTION 4. Membership. The Committee shall consist of ( 5) Members, each appointed by a Member of the City Commission, and ratified by the City Commission as a whole. At least one member of the Committee should be a resident of the MacFarlane Homestead or Golden Gate neighborhood. SECTION 5. Grandfather Clause. The informal committee that has administered the Hopkins/ Cooper scholarship thus far shall be permitted to continue their service for the Committee' s first term. Each existing member shall be grandfathered in to the Committee for a term of office that shall commence September 1, 2014, for two ( 2) consecutive years expiring on August 30, 2016, or until a successor has been selected and qualified. SECTION 6. Appointment and Removal.
