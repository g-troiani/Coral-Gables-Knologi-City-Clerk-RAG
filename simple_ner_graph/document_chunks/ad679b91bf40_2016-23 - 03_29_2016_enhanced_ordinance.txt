Valet plan. If valet services are proposed, the study must reference and the project must propose to provide adequately staffed valet services during the hours of operation of all uses , including an appropriate time following closing to accommodate the departure of valet parked cars. Projects shall submit an operational plan for the valet service, specifying details, including but not limited to maximum wait times, distance from valet drop-off points to valet parking areas, operational modifications to the functioning ofany required parking areas such as stacking, and the number ofoperators at peak and non-peak hours .
