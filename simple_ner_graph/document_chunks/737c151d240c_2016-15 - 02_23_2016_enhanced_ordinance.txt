The remaining Board Member shall be a City resident. SECTION 5. APPOINTMENT AND RENEWAL. That the Waterway Advisory Board members' terms of office shall commence March 1,2016 and run for an initial term until May, 2017, or until a successor has been selected and qualified, and thereafter terms shall commence June 1st of odd numbered years and expire two ( 2) years thereafter on May 31 st, or until a successor has been selected and qualified. All appointments for replacement of any member shall be for the member' s unexpired term. SECTION 6. CHAIRPERSON. The Board shall select among its members a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson who shall serve for one ( 1) year terms. The Chair and Vice Chair may be named to successive terms. SECTION 7. ADMINISTRATIVE SUPPORT STAFF. The Public Works Director shall appoint a staffmember from his or her Department to serve as a liaison with, and Secretary for, the Board. Said support shall include, but will not be limited to, preparation of summary minutes, preparation ofmeeting agendas. SECTION 8. WRITTEN REPORTS. That the Waterway Advisory Board shall submit an annual report to the City Commission in accordance with City Code, Article III, Division 1, Section 2-66. SECTION 9. REPEALER. All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith, are hereby repealed. SECTION 10. SEVERABILITY. The provisions of this Ordinance are declared to be severable and if any section, sentence, clause, or phrase ofthis Ordinance shall for any reason be held to be invalid or unconstitutional such decision shall not affect the validity ofthe remaining sections, sentences, clauses and phrases ofthis Ordinance, but they shall remain in effect it being the legislative intent that this Ordinance shall stand notwithstanding the invalidity of any part. SECTION 11. CODIFICATION. It is the intention of the Commission of the City of Coral Gables, Florida, that the provisions of this Ordinance shall become and be made a part of the City of Coral Gables Code of Ordinances; and that the sections of this ordinance may be renumbered or relettered to accomplish such intention, and the word " ordinance" may be changed to " section", " article", or such appropriate work or phrase in order to accomplish such intentions. SECTION 12. EFFECTIVE DATE. This Ordinance shall become effective upon passage and adoption herein. 2016.
