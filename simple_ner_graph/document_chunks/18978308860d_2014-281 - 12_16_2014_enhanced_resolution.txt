In accordance with the provisions of Sections 8 and 9 of the City Charter ( Chapter 10847, Laws of Florida, 1925, as amended,) a General Biennial Election is directed to be held in the City of Coral Gables, Florida, at the polling places designated in Exhibit No. 1, attached and incorporated, on Tuesday, April 14, 2015, from 7:00 a.m. until 7:00 p.m., for the purpose of electing candidates for the Offices of Mayor and City Commissioners for Groups I and IV and V of the City of Coral Gables, each to be voted upon at the General Biennial Election, which is to be held, as provided in Article" ( Mayor and City Commissioners); Section 8 of the City Charter, wherein the candidates for each group ( Mayor and City Commissioners), who receives the greatest number of votes for the prospective office for which he or she is a candidate, in which case he or she shall be elected to said office. SECTION 2. A General Biennial Election is directed to be held in the City of Coral Gables, Florida at the polling places designated in said Exhibit No. 1, from 7:00 a.m. until 7:00 p.nl., on Tuesday, April 14,2015, for the purpose of electing a Mayor and two Commissioners of the City of Coral Gables. SECTION 3. The candidate who shall receive the greatest number of votes for Group One ( I), for Mayor, shall serve as Mayor of the City of Coral Gables for a period of two ( 2) years from 12:00 noon, after the canvass of the vote and the declaration of the result of said General Biennial Election. The candidates who shall receive the greatest nunlber of votes for Group Numbers Four ( IV) and Five ( V) s.hall serve as Commissioner of the City of Coral Gables for a period of four ( 4) years from 12:00 noon, after the canvass of the vote and the declaration of the result of said General Biennial Election. SECTION 4. The Precinct Election Clerks and Inspectors to serve at said polling places on said date shall be designated by the Supervisor of Elections of Miami- Dade County, Florida, for such purposes in accordance with the General Laws of the State.
