Vacant property does not mean property that is temporarily unoccupied while the residents are away on vacation, personal matters or business, or is not intended by the owner to be left vacant, so long as the period does not exceed thirty (30) days. ## Sec. 34- 199. - Purpose and intent. It is the purpose and intent ofthe city to establish a process to address the deterioration and blight of city neighborhoods caused by an increasing amount of abandoned, foreclosed or distressed real property located within the city, and to identify, regulate, limit and reduce the number of abandoned properties located within the city. It is the city' s further intent to establish a registration program as a mechanism to protect neighborhoods from becoming blighted due to the lack of adequate maintenance and security of abandoned vacant and foreclosed properties. ## Sec. 34- 200. - ApplieabiliProvisions supplemental.
