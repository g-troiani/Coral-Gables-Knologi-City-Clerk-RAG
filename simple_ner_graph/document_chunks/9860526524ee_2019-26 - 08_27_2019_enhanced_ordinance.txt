Guard District, as created by Miami Dade County pursuant to County Ordinance 95- 214, is hereby amended to increase the hours of security services from 16 hours per day to 24 hours per day. SECTION 3. If any section, sentence, clause, or phrase of this Ordinance is held to be invalid or unconstitutional by any court of competent jurisdiction, then said holding shall in no way affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Ordinance. SECTION 4. All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith, are hereby repealed. SECTION 5. This Ordinance shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption hereof.
