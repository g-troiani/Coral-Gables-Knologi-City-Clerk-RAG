--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Meeting Date: 02.27.2024 - Source File: 2024-41 - 02_27_2024.pdf **ENTITIES IN THIS DOCUMENT:** - AGENDA_ITEM: F-9 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2024- 41 ## A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION ADOPTING A MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE FOR THE CITY' S WHITE WAY LIGHTS. WHEREAS, the City Commission has a strong interest in preserving the rich history of the City of Coral Gables; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has undertaken the restoration and installation of the historic White Way lights to be installed along University Drive between Ponce de Leon Boulevard and Bird Road, and along Riviera Drive, from University Drive to Anastasia Avenue; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to ensure that these City owned White Way lights are maintained for years to come; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to adopt a maintenance schedule in order to ensure the longevity of the White Way lights; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the White Way lights installed on University Drive between Ponce de Leon Boulevard and Bird Road, and those lights installed along Riviera Drive, from University Drive to Anastasia Avenue, will undergo inspection twice yearly, and further, will be washed, cleaned, and generally touched up as needed in accordance with recommendations from conservators and City staff, and an annual report will be sent to the City Commission detailing the same. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. ## PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY-SEVENTH DAY OF FEBRUARY, A.D., 2024.
