--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-213 - 10_14_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:26:31 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 213 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE PLACEMENT OF TEMPORARY EVENT BANNERS AND DIRECTIONAL SIGNS FOR THE " MIAMI DADE FLORIDA NURSERY, GROWERS AND LANDSCAPE ASSOCIATION PALM SHOW AND SALE," TO BE HELD ON OCTOBER 25 AND OCTOBER 26,2014 AT THE MONTGOMERY BOTANICAL CENTER, 11901 OLD CUTLER ROAD, CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA, AND AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO CONSIDER AND RENEW APPROVAL FOR FUTURE TEMPORARY SIGNAGE REQUESTS RELATED TO THIS EVENT. WHEREAS, the " Miami Dade Florida Nursery, Growers and Landscape Association Palm Show and Sale" event is scheduled for October 25 and October 26,2014; and WHEREAS, the President of the Miami Dade Florida Nursery, Growers and Landscape Association has requested permission to temporarily place three ( 3) event banners on the existing fences adjacent to the Montgomery Botanical Center and Gulliver Academy ( where parking will be provided for the event) and six ( 6) directional signs to assist in directing traffic for the event; and WHEREAS, approval of this request is subject to the removal of the banners and signs following the closing ofthe event on October 26,2014; and WHEREAS, the City Manager has requested authorization to consider and renew approval for future temporary signage requests related to this annual event; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission does hereby authorize the City Manager to allow for the temporary placement of three ( 3) event banner signs ( 3'x8') on the existing fences, adjacent to the Montgomery Botanical Center and Gulliver Academy ( where parking will be provided for the event), and the placement of six ( 6) directional signs ( 16" x24") to assist in directing traffic for the event, subject to the removal of the event signage following the closing of the event on October 26,2014. SECTION 3. That the City Commission does hereby authorize the City Manager to consider and renew approval for future temporary signage requests related to this event. SECTION 4. That said resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FOURTEENTH DAY OF OCTOBER; A 2014.
