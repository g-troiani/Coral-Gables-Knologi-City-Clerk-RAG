--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-197 - 09_23_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:24:10 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014-197 RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE SALE OF ALCOHOL AND THE WAIVING OF THE COST OF PARKING RENTALS AFTER 5:30 P.M. ON THE 100 BLOCK OF GIRALDA AVENUE ON THE FIRST FRIDAY OF EACH MONTH FROM NOVEMBER 2014 THROUGH MARCH 2015, FOR A TOTAL OF FIVE EVENTS, FOR THE GIRALDA UNDER THE STARS STREET DINING SERIES, SUBJECT TO FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF PROFESSIONAL REGULATION REQUIREMENTS. WHEREAS, during the last six years the Business Improvement District of Coral Gables implemented the Giralda Under the Stars program for the 100 block of Giralda Avenue; and WHEREAS, the program has been very well received by the public and business community; and WHEREAS, this program helps revitalize the restaurants on Giralda Avenue and increases foot traffic throughout the Central Business District; and WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to support this effort; and WHEREAS, section 74-140 ofthe Code ofthe City of Coral Gables allows for the City Commission to waive the cost ofmetered parking; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission does hereby authorize the City Manager to approve the sale of alcohol in the public rights- of-way and waive the cost of parking meter rentals after 5 : 30 p.m. on the 100 block of Giralda Avenue on the first Friday of each month from November 2014 through March 2015, for a total of five events, subject to Florida Department ofProfessional Regulation requirements. SECTION 3. That this resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY-THIRD DAY OF SEPTEMBER, A.D., 2014.
