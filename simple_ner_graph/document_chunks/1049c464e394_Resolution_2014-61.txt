WHEREAS, seven ( 7) qualifications statements were submitted in response to the RFQ by the following: Reynolds, Smith &amp; Hills ( RS&amp; H), CB&amp; I Environmental &amp; Infrastructure, Inc., formerly known as Shaw Environmental Inc.), LEVEL Agency for Infrastructure, LLC, Carbon Solutions America, LLC ( CSA), CDM Smith, Inc., AECOM Technical Services, Inc., and Natural Capitalism Solutions; and WHEREAS, the Evaluation Committee met to evaluate the qualification statements, which resulted in five ( 5) firms being shortlisted as follows: Carbon Solutions America, LLC CSA), CDM Smith, Inc., Reynolds, Smith &amp; Hills ( RS&amp; H), AECOM Technical Services, Inc., and CB&amp; I Environmental &amp; Infrastructure, Inc., with this shortlisted group then asked to make a presentation to the Evaluation Committee; and WHEREAS, after the presentations, the Evaluation Committee selected three firms to recommend to the Commission in the following order of preference: Reynolds, Smith &amp; Hills RS&amp; H), Miami, FL, CDM Smith, Inc., Miami, FL, and AECOM Technical Services, Inc., Coral Gables, FL; and WHEREAS, the Chief Procurement Officer recommends negotiations be authorized with the recommended list of firms in order of preference pursuant to Florida Statute 287.055 known as the " Consultants Competitive Negotiation Act" and Request for Qualifications ( RFQ) 2013.11.27; and WHEREAS, Section 2-650 ofthe Procurement Code entitled " Authority" authorizes the Chief Procurement Officer to determine the lowest or highest responsive and responsible offeror and make recommendations; and WHEREAS, Section 2-650 of the Procurement Code authorizes the City Manager to delegate authority to the Chief Procurement Officer to administer and make recommendations on city contracts; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission accepts the recommendation of the Chief Procurement Officer to authorize contract negotiations with Reynolds, Smith &amp; Hills, Inc. RS&amp; H), and if necessary, with other short- listed firms for the development of a Sustainability Master Plan pursuant to Florida Statute 287 .055, known as the " Consultants Competitive Negotiation Act" and Request for Qualifications ( RFQ) 2013. 11.27. herein . SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS EIGHTH DAY OF APRIL, A.D., 2014.
