WHEREAS, City Manager Patrick G. Salerno tendered his resignation effective April 18,2014 at the City Commission meeting on April 8,2014; and WHEREAS, the City Commission accepted his resignation with provision for compensation and transition; and WHEREAS, Mr. Salerno has three resolutions that comprise his employment contract; WHEREAS, the City Attorney has authority to interpret and provide opinions on behalf of the City regarding resolutions and contracts; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to ensure a smooth transition with cooperation from all parties; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION. 1. That the foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the resignation by City Manager Patrick G. Salerno is accepted effective April 18, 2014, with provision being made for Mr. Salerno to receive an amount equivalent of severance as stated in his employment resolutions. SECTION 3. That Assistant City Manager Carmen Olazabal will serve as Acting City Manager until an Interim City Manager is appointed. SECTION 4. That the City Attorney is authorized to interpret and implement this resolution to provide for the cooperation of the prior City Manager and to effect the orderly transition to a new City Manager. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS EIGHTH DAY OF APRIL, A.D., 2014.
