--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-98 - 05_27_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:14:16 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014-98 RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE EXECUTION OF A JOINT PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT ( JPA) BETWEEN MIAMIDADE COUNTY AND THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES FOR DISBURSEMENT OF ROAD IMPACT FEE FUNDS IN THE AMOUNT OF $ 1,490, 000. 00 TO THE CITY, FOR THE DESIGN AND CONSTRUCTION OF ROADWA Y CAPACITY IMPROVEMENTS AND A RAISED LANDSCAPE MEDIAN ON PONCE DE LEON BOULEVARD FROM SALAMANCA AVENUE TO ANTIQUERA AVENUE. WHEREAS, a Joint Participation Agreement has been prepared by Miami- Dade County to disburse Road Impact Fee funds in the amount of $ 1,490,000.00 to the City of Coral Gables for the construction ofroadway capacity improvements and a raised landscape median on Ponce de Leon Boulevard between Salamanca Avenue and Antiquera Avenue, in the City of Coral Gables, Florida; and WHEREAS, in 2007 a raised landscape median for a seven block segment was completed under the Ponce de Leon Boulevard Phase I Improvement Project; and WHEREAS, in 2011 the Phase II segment of the Ponce de Leon Boulevard Median Improvement was completed which included construction of roadway capacity improvements and a raised median from Alcazar Avenue to Salamanca Avenue; and WHEREAS, Road Impact Fee funds of $1,490, 000. 00 will provide the funding to move forward with the Phase III segment of the Ponce de Leon Boulevard Median Improvement Project; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The recitals and " Whereas" clauses contained in the preamble to this Resolution are adopted by reference and incorporated herein as if fully set forth in this Section. SECTION 2. The execution of a Joint Participation Agreement ( Exhibit A) between the City of Coral Gables and Miami-Dade County is hereby authorized for the disbursement ofRoad Impact Fee Funds in the amount of $1,490, 000. 00 to the City for the design and construction of the Phase III segment of the Ponce de Leon Boulevard Median Improvement Project from Salamanca Avenue to Antiquera Avenue which include raised landscaped median with addition of left turn bays along the entire corridor, and conversion of angle parking to parallel parking, with such modifications to the form attached hereto as Exhibit " A" as may be approved by the City Manager and City Attorney that are necessary to implement the intent of this resolution. SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective upon the date ofits adoption herein . PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- SEVENTH DAY OF MAY, A.D., 2014.
