WHEREAS, Resolution No. 2006-187, passed and adopted by the City Commission on October 10, 2006 and subsequently amended by Resolution 2006211 on December 12, 2006; Resolution 2007- 153 on June 26, 2007; Resolution 2007245 on October 23,2007; Resolution 2008- 201 ( As Amended) on November 18, 2008; Resolution 2009- 195 on July 7, 2009; Resolution 2009- 218 on July 29, 2009; Resolution 2009- 232 on August 25, 2009; Resolution 2010- 132 on July 13, 2010; Resolution 2010-184 on August 24, 2010; Resolution 2011-142 on June 7, 2011; Resolution 2011-170 on July 19, 2011; Resolution 2011-203 on August 23, 2011; Resolution 2012- 128 on July 24, 2012; Resolution 2013- 89 on May 28, 2013; Resolution 2013-178 on August 27, 2013; Resolution 2014-143 on July 22, 2014; Resolution 2014-172 on August 26, 2014; Resolution 2014-251 on November 18, 2014; and Resolution 2014- 256, on November 18, 2014, established fees for various licenses, permits, services, and penalties by the City of Coral Gables; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has determined that fees for various licenses, permits, and services are necessary to recover the City' s cost of providing these services; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has adopted numerous fees in the past pursuant to enabling ordinances, State Law, and applicable law; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has determined that it is in the best interest of the City to establish certain new fees and restate existing fees by this ordinance; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has determined that this ordinance is consistent with Fla. Stat. § 166. 201 that requires such charges or fees to be authorized by ordinance; and WHEREAS, any subsequent adjustments to the fees contained In this ordinance and the City Code shall be adopted by resolution; and WHEREAS, the establishment of any subsequent new fees shall be adopted by ordinance pursuant to Fla. Stat. § 166. 201, unless specifically authorized by separate local law, State Law, or federal statutes; and WHEREAS, it is the desire of the City Commission to incorporate certain fees within the City Code such as the impact fees and sewer capacity fees in the Fee Schedule; and ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. The fees for the various licenses, permits and services furnished by the City of Coral Gables are hereby adopted and attached as " Exhibit I." ## SECTION 3. IMPACT FEES The Fee Schedule in Exhibit I incorporates the impact fees from Section 22109( b). Section 2-2109( b) of the Code of the City of Coral Gables Article IX "Impact Fees" is hereby replealed: Sections 2-2106( c) and 2-2109( a) are hereby amended in part as specifically set forth as follow: ## Sec. 2-2106. The city shall track the use and allocation of such demolition credits. ## Sec. 2-2109. - Calculation ofimpact fee and impact fee schedules.
