CITY COMMISSION OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA, AMENDING THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES CODE, BY CREATING SECTION 38-12, TITLED " PENALTY ENHANCEMENTS FOR BIAS MOTIVATED VIOLATIONS OF THE CITY CODE OR ZONING CODE"; PROVIDING FOR A REPEALER PROVISION, SEVERABILITY CLAUSE, CODIFICATION, AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. WHEREAS, Florida Statute Section 775.085(1)(a) provides that " The penalty for any felony or misdemeanor shall be reclassified . . . if the commission of such felony or misdemeanor evidences prejudice based on the race, color, ancestry, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, national origin, homeless status, mental or physical disability, or advanced age of the victim"; and WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables will continue to enforce Florida Statute Section 775.085 to the full extent ofthe law; and WHEREAS, the City Commission ofCoral Gables requests a City Code text amendment to increase penalties for any bias motivated violations ofthe City Code or Zoning Code that are not already enhanced by Florida Statute Section 775.085; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. The foregoing " WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. Section 38-12 ofthe Code ofthe City of Coral Gables, Florida, is hereby created to read as follows: ## CHAPTER 38 OFFENSES AND MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS ARTICLE I. -IN GENERAL * Sec. 38- 12. Penalty enhancements for bias motivated violations of the City Code or Zoning Code. - a) A "bias motivated violation ofthe City Code or Zoning Code" means a violation ofthe City Code or Zoning Code in which the commission of such violation evidences prejudice based on the race, color, ancestry, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, national origin, homeless status, mental or physical disability, or advanced age ofthe victim. - b) A person who commits a " bias motivated violation of the City Code or Zoning Code" shall be subject to an additional penalty ofa fine not to exceed $ 500.00, by imprisonment in the county jail for a period not to exceed 60 days, or by both such fine and imprisonment, in the discretion of the court. Each bias motivated violation of the City Code or Zoning Code shall constitute a separate offense. - c) Violations ofthe City Code or Zoning Code which are continuous with respect to time and for which the penalty has been enhanced based on Section 38-12(b) above are a public nuisance and may be abated , on an expedited basis, by injunctive or other equitable relief, in addition to the relief set forth in Section 38-12(b). The city shall also be entitled to the recovery of reasonable attorneys' fees and costs it incurred in obtaining a court order for such injunctive or other equitable reliefbased on the violation . - d) This section shall only enhance penalties for bias motivated violations of the City Code or Zoning Code not already enhanced by Florida Statute Section 775.085. SECTION 3. All ordinances or parts of ordinances inconsistent or in conflict with the provisions ofthis Ordinance are hereby repealed. SECTION 4.
