--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-147 - 07_22_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:18:20 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014-147 RESOLUTION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA RATIFYING, CONFIRMING AND ADDING CERTAIN PROJECTS IN THE NEIGHBORHOOD RENAISSANCE PROGRAM; PROVIDING CERTAIN OTHER MATTERS IN CONNECTION THEREWITH; AUTHORIZING OTHER REQUIRED ACTIONS; AND PROVIDING AN EFFECTIVE DATE. WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Coral Gables, Florida (the " Borrower") authorized a loan from the Sunshine State Governmental Financing Commission ( the Commission"), evidenced by a loan agreement, for the purpose of financing the Borrower's capital improvements known as the " Neighborhood Renaissance Program", as described in such loan agreement (the " 2011 Loan") pursuant to Resolution No. 2011-139 adopted on June 7, 2011 and in Resolution No. 2011- 70 adopted on April 26, 2011; and WHEREAS, on May 22, 2012 the Borrower authorized a loan from the Commission, evidenced by a loan agreement, for the purpose of refinancing the Borrower' s capital improvements known as the " Neighborhood Renaissance Program", pursuant to Resolution No. 2012-101; and WHEREAS, the Borrower desires to ratify and confirm the capital improvements described in the Neighborhood Renaissance Program; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. That the foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this resolution upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2. The capital improvements, listed in Resolution No. 2011- 70 adopted on April 26, 2011 describing the Neighborhood Renaissance Program ( the " Renaissance Resolution"), specifically that project identified as " Lovers' Court" which is a civic plaza to be constructed on Biltmore Way and the immediate surrounding area instead of Miracle Mile, including the developing, constructing and equipping of such project, are hereby ratified and confirmed as a project within the Neighborhood Renaissance Program. The City Commission hereby authorizes and approves loan proceeds derived from the 2011 Loan be used to pay for such project. herein. SECTION 3. This Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- SECOND DAY OF JULY , A.D., 2014.
