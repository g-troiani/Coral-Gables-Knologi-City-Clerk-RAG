For small wireless facilities installed before the City adopts requirements that public utility lines must be placed underground, the City shall either: ( a) allow a wireless provider to maintain the small wireless facility in place subject to any applicable pole attachment agreement with the pole owner; or (b) allow the wireless provider to replace the associated pole within 50 feet of the prior location in accordance with the objective design standards contained within this Ordinance.
