Sec. 34-60. - Statement of costs; filing; publication of work; cost and lien. As soon as practicable after completion of the work, if such work is done by the city, the city manager shall execute, or cause to be executed, and file with the city clerk, a statement of costs and completion of work, which shall certify the completion thereof. The city clerk shall thereafter cause to be published in a newspaper of general circulation in the county or city, a notice giving the description of the property, the amount of the cost of the work, the date of completion of the work and the fact that the cost thereof is a lien against the property.
