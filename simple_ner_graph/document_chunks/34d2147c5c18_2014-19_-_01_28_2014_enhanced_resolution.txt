--- - Meeting_Date: 01.28.2014 - Document_Type: LEGAL_DOCUMENT - Source_File_Name: 2014-19 - 01_28_2014.pdf - Source_File_Path: /Users/gianmariatroiani/Documents/knologi/graph_database/city_clerk_documents/global/City Comissions 2024/resolutions/2014-19 - 01_28_2014.pdf --- **FULL TEXT:** CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 19 RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING SATCHMO BLUES BAR AND GRILL, INC. TO SELL ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES FROM 12:00 P.M. TO MIDNIGH AT THE PLAZA LOCATED AT 60 MERRICK WAY FROM FRIDAY, MAY 23, 2014 THROUGH SUNDAY, MAY 25, 2014 DURING THE 10 TH ANNUAL RIB AND BEER FEST TO BENEFIT BRANCHES, SUBJECT TO FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF PROFESSIONAL REGULATION REQUIREMENTS. BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That Satchmo Blues Bar and Grill, Inc. be pennitted to sell alcoholic beverages at the Plaza located at 60 Merrick Way from 12:00 p.m. to midnight on Friday, May 23,2014, through Sunday, May 25,2014, during the 10th Annual Rib and Beer Fest to benefit BRANCHES, subject to Florida Department of Professional Regulation Requirements. SECTION 2. The 10 th Annual Rib and Beer Fest will take place from 12:00 p.m. to midnight on Friday, May 23, 2014, through Sunday, May 25, 2014. There will be live music. Music played outside will end at 11:00 p.m. on Friday through Saturday, and will end at 8:00 p.m. on Sunday. SECTION 3. That this resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. 2014.
