The new single-family residences constructed on the two ( 2) building sites shall meet all applicable requirements of the Zoning Code, and no variances shall be required or requested. - 2. Restrictive covenant. Within thirty (30) days of approval ofthe Conditional Use Review for a Building Site Detennination, the property owner, its successors or assigns shall file for a release of the restrictive covenant currently running with the land and submit a new restrictive covenant for City Attorney review and approval that includes all conditions of approval as approved by the City Commission for both Lots 30 and 31. Failure to submit the covenant within the specified time frame shall render the approval void unless said time frame for submittal of the covenant is extended by the City Attorney after good cause as to why the time frame should be extended. - 4. Within 120 days of approval of the Conditional Use Review for a Building Site Detennination, the property owner shall amend the concrete dock and wood deck to meet the setback requirements pursuant to the Zoning Code, which can be extended at the discretion of the Development Services Director if the property owner can provide documentation that good faith efforts were made,to meet this condition. No Certificate of Occupancy will be issued on Lots 30 or 31 until the respective structures or encroachments confonn with the City ofCoral Gables Code ofOrdinances. - 5. The total square footage of the two ( 2) residences on Lot 30 and 31 shall be equal to or less than 34,974 square feet, which would be the maximum size of a residence pennitted by the Zoning Code that could be constructed on the property if developed as a single building site.
