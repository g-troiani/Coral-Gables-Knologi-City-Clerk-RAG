Hartnett Ponce Circle Park pursuant to Florida Statute 287.055, known as the “Consultants Competitive Negotiations Act” and Request for Qualifications (RFQ) 2012.05.17. Mayor Cason: Item C-6. Commissioner Lago: Thank you. Just want to make brief comments in regards to Item C-6 on the Consent Agenda. I was reviewing this item and I noticed that it is for an RFQ that went out for design of Fred B. Hartnett Ponce Circle Park. What I’d like to see if our Manager and maybe staff could give us a little further information in reference to the process how AECOM was selected as the short-listed consulting firm to perform the design, and also just to clarify a few technical questions that I may have in reference to the RFQ. City Commission Meeting January 28, 2014 Agenda Item C-6 – Resolution accepting recommendation CPO to Authorize contract with AECOM to renovate Fred B. Hartnett Ponce Circle Park Page 2 City Manager Salerno: Certainly. Mike would you come forward and address the Commission. Mayor Cason: Good morning. Commissioner Lago: Good morning. Mr. Pounds: The RFQ process that we operate under is dictated to a great extent by the Consultants Competitive Negotiation Act in Florida Statute. We advertise the RFQ and received a number of qualification statements, a total of nine (9). That group of 9 qualification statements was reviewed by our evaluation committee. They looked at all the information that was provided and short-listed down to six (6) firms. Those 6 firms were brought in for presentations and for a brief Q&A (Question & Answers) period. After those firms gave their presentations the evaluation committee came down with three (3) recommended firms and they ranked them and AECOM is the rated number one. They ranked first of that group of three, and we have submitted the three firms to you so that you can authorize if you agree, to authorize negotiations with the first firm and if fact, if we are not successful then we move on to the second firm and so on and so forth to the third firm. Mayor Cason: Who is on the evaluation committee? Mr. Pounds: The evaluation committee consisted of Cindy Birdsill, our Economic Sustainability Director, Carmen Olazabal, our Assistant City Manager, and Ernesto Pino, the Assistant Public Works Director, who is also the Interim Public Works Director. City Manager Salerno: I’d add one thing; I think Mike brushed over it a little too quickly. So there were 9 firms that submitted, the committee meets, this is standard, this doesn’t change for this process or anything, the committee meets. After they have reviewed all the 9 proposals, their first is to make a first cut and that’s where I think it was missed. The first cut narrowed from 9 to 6, then the 6 are the ones that are invited in for a presentation and Q&A. So it’s a two-step process, you don’t go from the one to interviews. First cut based on review of presentation that got to the 6, the 6 then presentations, Q&A, that got you to the 3 in the rank order. Mr. Pounds: There are two separate meetings. Vice Mayor Kerdyk: And I see the three are AECOM, Curtis and Rogers, and Kimley-Horn, those three; and just curiously, if you were – I assume we are going to give you approval to negotiate with the first one. If that does not do you go to a second one?- do you have a second one ranked, is that how it goes?- and a third one?- or do you start negotiating with the other two?
