--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-134 - 07_22_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:18:06 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014-134 RESOLUTION ACCEPTING THE RECOMMENDATION OF THE CHIEF PROCUREMENT OFFICER TO " PIGGYBACK" A CONTRACT FROM THE SCHOOL DISTRICT OF PALM BEACH COUNTY, FL WITH LEADEX CORPORATION, FOR PLAYGROUND IMPROVEMENTS FOR PIERCE PARK PURSUANT TO SECTION 2-978 OF THE PROCUREMENT CODE ENTITLED " USE OF OTHER GOVERNMENTAL UNIT CONTRACTS ( PIGGYBACK)". WHEREAS, on May 3,2010, The School District of Palm Beach County, FL awarded a term contract to LEADEX Corporation to furnish and install playground equipment, surfacing, shade structures, outdoor fitness equipment and fitness trails; and WHEREAS, under Section 2-978, of the Procurement Code, entitled " Use of Other Governmental Unit Contracts ( Piggyback)", contracts for services from another city or governmental entity, may be piggybacked if the contract resulted from a formal competitive bid process; and WHEREAS, the Parks and Recreation Division have requested the use of the School District of Palm Beach County Contract to make timely improvements to the playground at Pierce Park; and WHEREAS, the Pierce Park Playground Improvement Project scope of work consists of the demolition and removal of the existing playground, recapping of the existing poured- in-place playground safety surface, and the installation of new playground equipment; and WHEREAS, the Chief Procurement Officer recommends contracting with LEADEX Corporation under The School District of Palm Beach County Contract, for this playground improvement project in the amount of $ 38,339. 27; and WHEREAS, Section 2-650 of the Procurement Code authorizes the City Manager to delegate authority to the Chief Procurement Officer to administer and make recommendations on City contracts; and NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. Section 2. That the City Commission accepts the recommendation of the Chief Procurement Officer to " piggyback" a contract from The School District of Palm Beach County, FL with LEADEX Corporation, for playground improvements for Pierce Park pursuant to Section 2-978 of the Procurement Code entitled " Use of Other Governmental Unit Contracts ( Piggyback)", subject to final approval and execution ofa contract by City staff. Section 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- SECOND DAY OF JULY, A.D., 2014.
