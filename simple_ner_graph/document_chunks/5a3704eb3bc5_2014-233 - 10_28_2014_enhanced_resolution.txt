MONIES, TO ACQUIRE THE " POLICE OFFICER SCHEDUING SYSTEM ( POSS) AND PERSONNEL AUDIO RESPONSE SYSTEM (PARS)" SOFTWARE. WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Police Department' s investigations of criminal activity produce forfeited funds, both related and unrelated to its participation in the South Florida Money Laundering Strike Force operations; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Police Department may lawfully retain at the conclusion ofthe legal proceedings the forfeited funds, as authorized under Florida State Statute 932.7055; and WHEREAS, pursuant to the provisions of Florida State Statute 932.7055(5)(a), the proceeds and interest from assets forfeited as a result of criminal investigations shall be used for other law enforcement purposes, which include providing additional equipment or expertise; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Police Department has identified that POSS and PARS would provide additional expertise by enhancing the Department's ability to automate the staffing, scheduling and timekeeping functions carried out by staff, as well as the off-duty and extra duty assignments; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Interim Chief of Police, Edward J. Hudak, Jr., and the Coral Gables Finance Director, Diana Gomez, attest to having deposited the sum of $ 8,719 in said forfeited funds into the Coral Gables Forfeited Asset Fund; and WHEREAS, the F.A.F. Committee met on October 15, 2014, and followed the F.A.F. Committee operating procedures adopted on July 23, 2002; and WHEREAS, the F . A.F. Committee approved the Coral Gables Interim Chief ofPolice's request to expend these funds for use by the Department on the specific items listed below in this resolution; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon f SECTION 2. That the City Manager is hereby directed to authorize the Finance Director to expend $ 8,719 to the Coral Gables Police Department from State Forfeited Asset Fund monies to acquire the Police Officer Scheduling System ( POSS) and Personnel Audio Response System (PARS) software. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date ofits adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY-EIGHTH DAY OF OCTOBER A.D., 2014.
