WHEREAS, the City Commission of the City of Coral Gables wishes to minimize traffic hazards and public nuisances within the City and to ensure the safety of its residents and visitors; and WHEREAS, the City Code of the City of Coral Gables regulates where vehicles may be parked and how they may be stored, specifically regulating the number of vehicles that shall be allowed on private property under form-fitting car covers with clips or drawstrings; and WHEREAS, the City Code does not currently contemplate the use of form-fitting car covers within the City' s rights- of-way such as the swale areas; and WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that the use of form-fitting car covers in the City rights-of-way poses multiple issues for the City, including but not limited to: detracting from the overall appearance of the City, making the identification of a vehicle' s license plate, its make and model, and its condition more difficult for Code Enforcement and Police, and car covers can collect water and debris, which can create a breeding ground for mosquitoes and other pest; and WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that this Ordinance serves a public purpose by preventing neighborhood blight and the deterioration of neighborhood character and is necessary for the preservation of the quality of life and the public health, safety, and welfare of City residents and visitors; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That Chapter 34 'Nuisance,' Article IV. 'Lost, stolen, junked, and abandoned property,' Division 2 ' Vehicles,' Section 34-105 ' Abandoned or junk motor vehicles on public or private property prohibited; exceptions' of the Code of the City of Coral Gables, Florida is amended as follows1: ## CHAPTER 34 ## ARTICLE IV. - LOST, STOLEN, JUNKED, AND ABANDONED PROPERTY ## DIVISION 2. VEHICLES Sec. 34-105. - Abandoned or junk motor vehicles on public or private property prohibited; exceptions. - a)It shall be unlawful for any person to park, store, or leave any motor or other vehicle maintained on cement blocks, with flat tires, partially dismantled, not properly registered or insured, or in a wrecked, junked, dilapidated or abandoned condition on public or private property in the city: - 1)Unless it is in connection with a purpose or business enterprise lawfully situated and licensed.
