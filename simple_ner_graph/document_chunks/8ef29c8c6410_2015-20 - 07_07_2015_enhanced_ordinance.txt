SECTION 3. Qualifications. That Advisory Board members shall have their primary place of residence within the boundaries of the City. Waivers may be requested for non- resident members. SECTION 4. Membership. That the Advisory Board shall be comprised of a total of seven ( 7) members, five ( 5) of which shall be appointed by the Commission. Each of the Commissioners shall appoint one ( 1) member each for a total of five ( 5) and the Board as a whole is required to appoint one ( 1) member at large. The City Manager shall appoint the one ( 1) remaining member. Members are required to have basic knowledge of sustainability and environmental issues and most of all are motivated and have a high level of interest in helping move the city in a more sustainable and environmentally focused direction. SECTION 5. Appointment and Removal. That the Advisory Board members' terms of office shall commence be retroactive to June 1,2015 That the term of each Member shall be for a two- year period commencing on June 1st of odd years. Appointments for the replacement ofany Member or to fill a vacancy shall be for the unexpired term. SECTION 6. Chairperson. That the Advisory Board shall elect from among its membership, a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson who shall serve for a period of one ( 1) year, and may be named to successive terms if the Advisory Board so chooses. SECTION 7. Administrative Staff Support. That the City Manager shall appoint a staff member to serve as a liaison with and Secretary for the Advisory Board, and provide services as required by City Code, Article III, Division I, Section 2-63, including, but not limited to, preparation of summary minutes. SECTION 8. Meetings. That the Advisory Board shall hold one regular meeting each month and special meetings at such times as the Advisory Board may determine, at the call of the Chairman or the Secretary. If there is no business to conduct, a meeting need not be held, however, the Advisory Board must meet at least semi- annually to remain active. SECTION 9. Quorum. That meetings shall be called to order when a quorum is present, which shall be at least four ( 4) members, and a decision ofa majority ofthe members present and voting at which a quorum is present shall be the decision of the Advisory Board. SECTION 10. Compensation. That all members of the Advisory Board shall serve without compensation. ( Sec. 93 City Charter) SECTION 11. Public Meetings. That all meetings of the Advisory Board shall be open to the public, and a written summary of the proceedings shall be kept showing its action on each question considered, and such record shall be filed in the office of the Secretary and submitted to the City Commission. SECTION 12. Advisors. That the City may provide qualified professional consultants to serve in an advisory capacity to the Advisory Board. SECTION 13. Written Reports. That the Sustainability Advisory Board shall submit an annual report to the City Commission in accordance with City Code, Article III, Division I, Section 2-66. SECTION 14. Compliance with Board and Committee Rules. That the Advisory Board shall be governed by any and all legislation enacted by the City Commission, as well as any applicable administrative policies that govern the functions of City boards and committees, including, but not limited the provisions of the City Code. SECTION 15. Costs. That all costs incurred for notifications, mailings, printings, legal and/ or courtesy advertisements, and other items necessary to conduct the business of the Advisory Board shall be borne by the City. SECTION 16. Repealer. All ordinances or parts of ordinances in conflict herewith, be and the same, are hereby repealed. SECTION 17. Severability.
