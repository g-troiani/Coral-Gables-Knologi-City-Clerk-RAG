WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Fire Department has been mentoring EMT/Paramedic students by allowing them to " ride along" on their rescue units for over twenty years; and WHEREAS, students enrolled in the Emergency Medical Services curriculum are able to acquire clinical experience by riding with our Rescue personnel; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Fire Department has been using this standardized agreement for approximately 11 years; and WHEREAS, there is no cost to the City for this program; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City of Coral Gables is hereby authorized to enter into an agreement with Urgent Response Training Institute to allow EMT/Paramedic students to ride on the Coral Gables Fire Department's rescue units; and SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. Page 1 of2 - Resolution No. 2014- 21 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY-EIGHTH DAY OF JANUARY, A.D., 2014.
