Acceptable maintenance of yards and/or landscape shall not include weeds, gravel, broken concrete; or asphalt,. or similar material. - e) Maintenance shall include, but not be limited to, watering, irrigation, cutting and mowing of required ground cover or landscape and removal of all trimmings and dead vegetation. - f) Pools and spas shall be maintained so the water remains free and clear of pollutants and debris and shall comply with the regulations set forth in the applicable codes. If a pool is not kept full of water and filtered with a functioning pump, it must be covered to prevent the accumulation ofwater and the breeding of mosquitoes. - g) Failure of the mortgagee and/ or owner to properly maintain the property may result in a violation of the applicable codes and issuance of a citation or notice of violation/ notice of hearing in accordance with chapter 101. Pursuant to a finding and determination by the city's code enforcement board, hearing officer/special magistrate or a court of competent jurisdiction, the city may take the necessary action to ensure compliance with this section. - h) In addition to the above, the property is required to be maintained in accordance with the applicable codes. ## Sec. 34- 204. Security requirements. - a) Properties subject to these sections this article shall be maintained in a secure manner so as not to be accessible to unauthorized persons. - b) The term " secure manner" shall include, but not be limited to, the closure and locking of windows, doors, gates and other openings of such size that may allow a child to access the interior of the property or structure.
