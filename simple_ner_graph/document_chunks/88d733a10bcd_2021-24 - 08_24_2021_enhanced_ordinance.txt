WHEREAS, the Miami- Dade County Ethics Code sets the minimum standard for ethics rules applying to elected officials, appointed officials, and county/city employees and, applies throughout Miami- Dade County and all municipalities within Miami- Dade County; and WHEREAS, specifically, the Miami- Dade County Ethics Code sets forth the minimum standard for rules relating to and regulating lobbying activity; and WHEREAS, in Section 2-305 of the City Code, the City sets forth its lobbying regulations; and WHEREAS, Section 2-305 of the City Code has not been substantially revised since 2006 and only partially revised in 2017; and WHEREAS, Miami-Dade County is currently making changes to the lobbying regulations in the Miami- Dade County Ethics Code and an ordinance has been adopted on first reading by the Board of County Commissioners; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to amend its lobbying regulations to be consistent with the Miami- Dade County amendments and to ensure that all City regulations that differ, are more stringent than those imposed by Miami-Dade County; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to add a ' duty to announce' requirement to Section 2-305 of the City Code; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to add a ' duty to list' requirement to Section 2-305 of the City Code; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to require that all lobbyist complete ethics training; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OFCORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. Section 2-305 ' Lobbying,' of Article V, 'Conflict of Interest and Code of Ethics,' Chapter 2 of the Official Code of the City of Coral Gables is hereby amended as shown in Attachment A, with deletions indicated by strikethroughs and insertions indicated by underline. SECTION 3. All Ordinances or parts of Ordinances inconsistent or in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. SECTION 4. If any section, part of section, paragraph, clause, phrase or word of this Ordinance is declared invalid, the remaining provisions of this Ordinance shall not be affected. SECTION 5.
