Protect, strengthen, and enhance the City of Coral Gables as a vibrant community ensuring that its neighborhoods, business opportunities, shopping, employment centers, cultural activities, historic value, desirable housing, open spaces, and natural resources make the City a very desirable place to work, live and play. Objective FLU-1.1. Preserve Coral Gables as a ' placemaker' where the balance of existing and future uses is maintained to achieve a high quality living environment by encouraging compatible land uses, restoring and protecting the natural environment, and providing facilities and services which meet or exceed the minimum Level of Service ( LOS) standards and meet the social and economic needs of the community through the Comprehensive Plan and Future Land Use Classifications and Map ( see FLU-1: Future Land Use Map). Policy FLU-1.1.1. The City' s Future Land Use Classifications and Map shall describe, assign, and depict the future land uses found to be in the public interest and to be the basis for regulations, programs, actions and rules of the City and other affected agencies.
