--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2019-22 - 07_09_2019.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:17:22 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## ORDINANCE NO. 2019 -22 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION AMENDING CHAPTER 46 OF THE "CODE OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES," ENTITLED "PENSIONS;" AMENDING SECTION 46-25, ENTITLED DEFINITIONS", TO REVISE THE DEFINITIONS OF AVERAGE FINAL COMPENSATION AND NORMAL RETIREMENT DATE FOR FIREFIGHTERS; AMENDING SECTION 46-253, ENTITLED NORMAL RETIREMENT INCOME", TO REVISE THE NORMAL RETIREMENT INCOME FOR CERTAIN FIREFIGHTERS; AND PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY, REPEALER, CODIFICATION, AND AN EFFECTIVE DATE ( THE SOLE PURPOSE OF THIS ORDINANCE IS TO INCORPORATE THE PENSION CHANGES AGREED UPON IN THE 2017- 2020 COLLECTIVE BARGAINING AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY AND IAFF). WHEREAS, the City recently entered into a collective bargaining agreement with the International Association of Firefighters, Local 1210; and WHEREAS, the collective bargaining agreement contains certain changes to retirement benefits of firefighters under the City of Coral Gables Retirement System Retirement System"); and WHEREAS, to implement the Retirement System changes, an ordinance amending the City Code provisions governing the Retirement System is required; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing recitals are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a part of this Ordinance upon adoption. SECTION 2. That Section 46- 25 of the " Code of the City of Coral Gables" pertaining to " Pensions" is hereby amended to read as followsl: ## Sec. 46- 25. Definitions. The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this article, shall have the meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates a different meaning: Words and figures underscored are additions to existing law; words and figures struck through are deletions. Average final compensation means: - 3) Effective September 30, 2013, the term " average final compensation" for firefighters means: - a. For participants who retire on or after October 1, 2012, and on or before September 30, 2013, the highest three-year average total earnings as ofthe date of retirement or the highest four- year average earnings, whichever is greater; - b. For participants who retire on or after October 1, 2013, and on or before September 30, 2014, the highest four- year average earnings as of the date of retirement September 30, 2013, or the highest five-year average earnings, whichever is greater; and - c. For participants who retire on or after October 1, 2014, the highest five- year average earnings. Effective September 30, 2013, retirement benefit calculations and employee contributions for firefighters shall be based on pensionable earnings and not total earnings. For firefighters employed by the city and not participating in the DROP on September 30, 2013, total earnings shall be applied for any period of credited service prior to October 1, 2013, and pensionable earnings shall be applied for any period of credited service on or after October 1, 2013. Notwithstanding the provisions of this subsection ( 3), for firefighters who are employed on September 30, 2013, and have attained normal retirement eligibility as of that date, the highest three- year average total earnings shall continue to apply to retirement benefit calculations and employee contributions for all periods of credited service. Normal retirement date means: - 5) Notwithstanding the preceding definition of normal retirement date, effective September 30, 2013, the normal retirement date for firefighters with less than ten years of credited service on September 30, 2013, and firefighters hired on or after that date means the first day of the month coincident with or next following the date on which the participant attains 51 years of age with 25 years of credited service regardless of age, or " Rule of 76" ( age plus years of credited service equal 76); provided, any such member whose normal retirement date under the " Rule of 76" would be after age 59 and who is within 12 months of completing ten years of credited service on October 15, 2013, shall retain the " Rule of 70" normal retirement date. Firefighters who have ten or more years of credited service as of September 30, 2013, shall retain eligibility to retire at the normal retirement date specified in subsections ( 1) through ( 3) above, as applicable.
