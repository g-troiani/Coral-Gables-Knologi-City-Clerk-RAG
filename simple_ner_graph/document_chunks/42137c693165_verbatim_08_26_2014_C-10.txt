So if you have any statements you'd like to make in regards to what we are going to be seeing for the next six months, when the plan will be completed, what our target dates are. Mr. McCarthy: Yes. We'll be starting, we have a plan that works on government operations first, get our own house in order. It starts with the process of quantitatively and qualitatively evaluating where we stand, baseline operations. We will then benchmark against other communities and identify approaches that can be used with a goal of moving toward a project driven sustainability plan. We will at five months have a solutions memo together, which has not yet included the collaboration with all of governmental staff in a collaboration medium. We will have had discussions, but we will have a solutions memo identifying generally what our approach is going to be. From five (5) months to eight (8) months is the engagement phase, so these first five months will be working on developing solutions. The next three months will be delving engagement and the importance of this is that when we develop solutions and sustainability we've got to come up with a recurring funding mechanism to maintain the life of the Sustainability Plan. This engagement phase reaches out to individuals and senior management, mid management, and on the front lines that have ideas that can be brought to bear that will conserve resources, energy, fuel, waste minimization, water conservation, and all the other focus areas that we've identified. After that collaboration period where each of the department leaders and their key representatives will identify projects that would advance particular sustainability goals through development, will provide a project driven sustainability plan with returns on investment on the basis of where we are putting our money, where we are getting our money back. We expect the funding sources that we will be able to identify will be in energy conservation, fuel conservation, and potentially in waste minimization. We expect gains in water conservation as well.
