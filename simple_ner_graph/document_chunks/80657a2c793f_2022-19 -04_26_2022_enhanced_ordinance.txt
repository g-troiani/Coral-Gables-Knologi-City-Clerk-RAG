WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 2010-11 which was passed and adopted by the City Commission on July 13, 2010 established a Green Task Force to make recommendations to the City Commission regarding environmental sustainability; and WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 2015- 20 was passed and adopted by the City Commission on July 7, 2015, renaming the Green Task Force as the Sustainability Advisory Board; and WHEREAS, Ordinance 2019- 02 expanded the Board' s membership to include representation from the Coral Gables Chamber of Commerce, Florida International University, and the University of Miami; and WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables has become a leader in environmental initiatives and in developing sustainable communities based on environmental, cultural and economic policies that protect our natural resources and quality of life; and WHEREAS, the Sustainability Advisory Board has allowed for study and application of new technologies and philosophies promoting environmental sustainability measures; and WHEREAS, the Sustainability Advisory Board has attracted numerous young people to its recent meetings and events and the Sustainability Advisory Board members and City Commission wish to increase the Board' s membership to provide representation for the youth of Coral Gables; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OFCORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That Chapter 2, Article III- Boards and Committees of the City Code of Coral Gables, Florida, Section 2-147 be amended as follows: ## Sec. 2-147. Membership. The advisory board shall be comprised of a total of ten eleven members, five of which shall be appointed by the commission. Each of the commissioners shall appoint one member for a total of five, and the advisory board as a whole is required to appoint one member at large and one youth appointee. The city manager shall appoint the one member. The University of Miami shall appoint one representative as a member. Florida International University shall appoint one representative as a member, and the Coral Gables Chamber of Commerce shall appoint one representative as a member. The members appointed by the University of Miami. Florida International University, and the Coral Gables Chamber of Commerce shall be exempt from the residency requirements of section 2-146. The youth member shall be a freshman, sophomore, or junior in high school at the time of their appointment. The youth member shall attend a high school located in Coral Gables and/or be a resident of Coral Gables. The youth member shall provide a letter of recommendation from a teacher or mentor prior to appointment and ideally will belong to a school or community organization that addresses sustainability.
