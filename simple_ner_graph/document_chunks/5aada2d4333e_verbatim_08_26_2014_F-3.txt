Commissioner Frank Quesada Commissioner Quesada: I have one more item I want to discuss. I just have one item. Mayor Cason: Alright. Commissioner Quesada: And that is the other item I have on the agenda in relation to another Commission Aide. When I deal with our Aide, with Danette, I feel like she is being pulled in a thousand different directions. I ask her to be at a lot of meetings, I give her a lot of different work, a lot of different things to work on, and sometimes she tells me she is either doing something for another Commissioner or she is too busy to attend to whatever needs I have at that time. I think we are - I know Commissioner Lago uses her and Commissioner Keon, I hear that she works for the Vice Mayor as well. So are you guys having the same experience that she is being pulled in different directions? Do we need additional help? Commissioner Lago: I'll tell you from my experience. Just this Saturday and Sunday she was we were e-mailing back and forth with a constituent. The work that I give her in reference to constituent services, policymaking, is significant enough just for her to work for me fulltime. So I don't understand to this moment how she is capable to work for four individuals who are here mostly every day, when they are not here they are contacting her so that they can deal with City matters. So I think it's a matter of time that we bring forth another Commission Aide, along with the support of my colleagues, because I myself am in dire need of it, and if I'm going to do my best work to represent the residents of this community who elected me, I think we need to bring somebody in who can service them. Mayor Cason: Let me ask you a question. Commissioner Lago: Yes sir. Mayor Cason: Is your concept with this extra person to assign that person to two specific Commissioners so that they know who they work for and therefore….it seems to make more sense. Commissioner Quesada: Seems to make more sense and back-up to each other. Mayor Cason: And back up. Seems to make more sense because also in terms of inadvertent Sunshine issues to the extent that you have a person working for two, you know who to call and then that way you can differentiate in terms of your evaluations of two different individuals. Commissioner Lago: One hundred percent. Mayor Cason: I support that. Commissioner Lago: I spoke to the Interim City Manager in regards to this issue and again, I won't speak for her, but she made it clear that the funds are available from my understanding and I told her that this is something that I need done not in 2015, I need to provide some relief for Danette as soon as possible. So I know that you are bringing up this item, but I kind of feel that I'm also attached to this item. Mayor Cason: I don't have a problem. Commissioner Keon: Where does she sit or he sit? Where does this individual sit? Commissioner Lago: In the closet (laughter). We'll figure that out. Let's get the aide and then we'll figure it out. Mayor Cason: One of the things we've been discussing or I've been discussing with the City Manager past and present is something that's very needed and that's to find an office for every Commissioner and I think to the extent we can find that and I think there are some solutions in the works they'll be space I think. Commissioner Lago: Right now you have Danette, the Vice Mayor and Commissioner Quesada sitting in an office, an office that's the size of a closet. It doesn't make any sense. Mayor Cason: Is this by consensus? City Attorney Leen: Yes.
