WHEREAS, the Zoning Code requires an arcade or loggia to be no less than 10 feet in width; and WHEREAS, the Zoning Code does not currently have a requirement for a minimum clear distance for pedestrians to pass through an arcade or loggia that has outdoor seating and adjacent to a public sidewalk; and WHEREAS, a goal in the Comprehensive Plan is to promote the safe movement of pedestrian traffic; and WHEREAS, a member of the City Commission requested a Zoning Code text amendment to provide a 4-foot minimum clear distance for pedestrians to pass through an arcade or loggia that has outdoor seating and adjacent to a public sidewalk; and WHEREAS, new outdoor seating permit applications will be required to comply with a site plan illustrating the minimum 4-foot clearance; and WHEREAS, existing outdoor seating permits are not required to comply and are not required to resubmit site plans illustrating the minimum 4-foot clearance; and WHEREAS, after notice was duly published, a public hearing was held before the Planning and Zoning Board on September 21, 2023, at which hearing all interested parties were afforded the opportunity to be heard; and WHEREAS, the Planning and Zoning Board was presented with text amendments to the Official Zoning Code, and after due consideration, recommended approval ( vote: 6 to 1) of the text amendment; and WHEREAS, after notice was duly published, a public hearing was held before the City Commission for First Reading on October 10th, 2023, at which hearing all interested parties were afforded the opportunity to be heard; and WHEREAS, after notice was duly published, a public hearing for Second Reading was held before the City Commission at which hearing all interested parties were afforded the opportunity to be heard. ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES THAT: SECTION 1. The foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2.
