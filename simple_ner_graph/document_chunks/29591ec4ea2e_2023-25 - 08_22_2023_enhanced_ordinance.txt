WHEREAS, there are various communities located in the southern areas of the City of Coral Gables located along the waterways that are represented by homeowners' associations; and WHEREAS, the various communities and their respective homeowners' associations share common concerns and experiences that are unique to properties located along the waterways; and WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to establish a City advisory board comprised of representatives from the various homeowners' associations for communities located along the waterways in the southern areas of the City so that board members may provide City staff and the City Commission with their unique perspective regarding concerns common to such communities that include security, waterway use, and traffic and road issues; and WHEREAS, the City Commission intends for each member to be designated by each of the named homeowners' associations, subject to Commission approval; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BYTHE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OFCORALGABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. The foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That Chapter 2, Article III- Boards and Committees of the City Code of Coral Gables, Florida, Division 11 be created to read as follows: Sec.\_\_\_\_\_. Creation and Purpose. The purpose of the Coral Gables South Waterways Homeowners' Associations Advisory Board will be to furnish and relay citizen input to the City regarding the communities located along the waterways in the southern areas of the City of Coral Gables and to formulate recommendations regarding security, waterway use, landscaping, and traffic and road issues that are common to such communities. Sec.\_\_\_\_\_. Membership, Quorum, and Qualifications. The South Waterways Homeowners' Associations Advisory Board shall be comprised of \_\_ members, with each member to be confirmed by the City Commission. A quorum shall consist of a majority of members. Each member shall be a city resident living in one of the communities listed below that are located along the waterways in the southern areas of the City of Coral Gables and such member must be designated by the homeowners' association for the respective community in which the member resides. The following homeowners' associations shall designate one member for appointment to the board, with such designation to be evidence in a written communication from the president of such association: - 1) Cocoplum Homeowners' Association - 2) Deering Bay Master Property Owners' Association - 3) Gables- by-the- Sea Homeowners' Association - 4) Gables Estates Club - 5) Hammock Oaks Homeowners' Association - 6) Journeys End Homeowners' Association - 7) Kings Bay Homeowners' Association - 8) Old Cutler Bay Homeowners' Corp.
