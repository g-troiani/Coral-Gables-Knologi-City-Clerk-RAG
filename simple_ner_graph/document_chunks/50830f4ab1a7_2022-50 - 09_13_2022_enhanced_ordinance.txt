WHEREAS, in Resolution No. 2022- 121, the City Commission directed City staff to draft an Ordinance creating a Coral Gables Golf and Country Club (' Country Club') Advisory Board for the City Commission' s consideration; and WHEREAS, the Country Club is a unique historic landmark and City- owned property that was recently reacquired by the City from a private lessee; and WHEREAS, the Granada Golf Course is the first public nine-hole golf course in Coral Gables and a historic landmark that opened to the public in 1923, a year after the Country Club; and WHEREAS, presently the City has boards and committees in place that assist City staff in the oversight and maintenance of City infrastructure and specific City- owned properties; and WHEREAS, the Country Club is important to both the history and future of the City and the creation of a dedicated board will assist staff with the transition of the facility from a privately- operated club to a publicly- operated club; and WHEREAS, the City Commission intends for board members to offer a unique perspective to the City Commission and to support decisions in alignment with the needs of Country Club members and the community; and WHEREAS, due to the gradual reopening of the Country Club' s amenities, the City Commission desires that the first meeting of the Coral Gables Golf and Country Club Advisory Board take place after January 1, 2023; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OFCORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. The foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That Chapter 2, Article III- Boards and Committees of the City Code of Coral Gables, Florida, Division 9 be created to read as follows: Sec.\_\_\_\_\_. Creation and Purpose. The purpose of the Coral Gables Golf and Country Club Advisory Board will be to furnish and relay citizen input regarding the Coral Gables Golf and Country Club to the city, to formulate recommendations regarding the Coral Gables Golf and Country Club for the benefit of City staff and the City Commission, to create a forum for dialogue between Coral Gables Golf and Country Club members and the city; and to help preserve the harmony between the surrounding neighborhood and the Coral Gables Golf and Country Club. Sec.\_\_\_\_\_. Membership, Quorum, and Qualifications.
