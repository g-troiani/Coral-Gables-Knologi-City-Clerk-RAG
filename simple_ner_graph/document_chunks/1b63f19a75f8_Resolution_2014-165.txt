MONIES FOR LAW ENFORCEMENT TRAINING; THE CREATION OF A TRAINING VIDEO AS PART OF THE AUTISM WALLET CARD PROGRAM FOR UTILIZATION BY THE CORAL GABLES POLICE DEPARTMENT. WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Police Department participates with federal law enforcement agencies of the United States Departments of Justice, Treasury and/or Homeland Security in joint law enforcement operations, and the Coral Gables Police Department's participation in those joint investigations produces federally seized funds; and WHEREAS, at the discretion ofthe United States Attorney General under the relevant United States Statutes, 18 U.S.C. Section 981(e)( 2) and 21 U.S.C. Section 881(e)( 1)(a) and e )(3 )(b), the Coral Gables Police Department may lawfully retain at the conclusion ofthe legal proceedings the federally seized funds; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Chief ofPolice, Dennis L. Weiner, and the Coral Gables Finance Director, Diana Gomez, attest to have deposited the sum of $ 10,000 in said forfeited funds into the Coral Gables Forfeited Asset Fund; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Chief ofPolice requests to expend these funds for use by the Department for law enforcement training; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Chief ofPolice certifies that the request complies with all provisions of the appropriate F.A.F. statutory requirements as identified in the United States Justice Department's publication entitled " Guide to Equitable Sharing for State and Local Law Enforcement Agencies"; and WHEREAS, the F.A.F. Committee met on August 5, 2014, and followed the F.A.F. Committee operating procedures adopted on July 23, 2002; and WHEREAS, the F.A.F. Committee approved the Coral Gables Chief ofPolice's request to expend the available forfeited asset funds on the specific items listed below on this resolution; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of the Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Manager is hereby directed to authorize the Finance Director to expend $ 10,000 to the Coral Gables Police Department from Federal Forfeited Asset Fund monies for law enforcement training; the creation ofa training video as part ofthe Autism Wallet Card Program for utilization by the Coral Gables Police Department. herein. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY-SIXTH DAY OF AUGUST A.D., 2014 .
