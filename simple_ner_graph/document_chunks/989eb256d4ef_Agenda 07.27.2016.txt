EXECUTIVE SESSION ### C-1 - 16-5246 Attorney-Client executive session, closed to the public, for purposes of discussing the litigation styled Miami-Dade County et al. v. A certified Court Reporter will be present to ensure that the session is fully transcribed, and the transcripts will be made available to the public upon the conclusion of the litigation. At the conclusion of the attorney-client session, the public meeting will be reopened, and termination of the attorney-client session will be announced.
