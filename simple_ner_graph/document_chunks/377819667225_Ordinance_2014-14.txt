If any section, part of session, paragraph, clause, phrase or word ofthis Ordinance is declared invalid, the remaining provisions ofthis Ordinance shall not be affected. SECTION 6. This development permit by the City of Coral Gables does not in any way create any right on the part of an applicant to obtain a permit from a county, state or federal agency. Likewise, this development permit does not create any liability on the part of the City of Coral Gables for issuance ofthe permit ifthe applicant fails to obtain requisite approvals or fulfill the obligations imposed by a county, state or federal agency, or ifthe applicant undertakes actions that result in a violation ofcounty, state or federal law.
