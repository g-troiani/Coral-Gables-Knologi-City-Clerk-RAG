WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to have citizen participation in its anticrime and crime prevention efforts; and WHEREAS, the City- Wide Anti- Crime Committee can facilitate community involvement in public safety, thereby increasing citizen awareness; and WHEREAS, greater citizen awareness and coordination between the community and its public safety agencies will make Coral Gables a better place to live, work and play; and WHEREAS, this Resolution rescinds Resolution No. 2005- 119, thereby reinstating the City- Wide Anti- Crime Committee; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. Incorporation. That the foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2. Reinstatement and Purpose. That the City- Wide Anti-Crime Committee be reinstated 1) to assist and advise the Police Department in all matters involving crime and traffic issues within the areas they represent by gathering information to be delivered directly to the Command Staff of the Police Department, and 2) to assist the Department in the dissemination of initiatives and reports originating from the Department back to the areas they represent and to the Commission as a whole. SECTION 3. Qualifications. That the City- Wide Anti-Crime Committee shall have their primary place of residence within the boundaries of the City. SECTION 4. Membership. The Committee shall consist of thirteen ( 13) members with each member of the City Commission appointing two ( 2) members, the City Manager appointing two ( 2) members, and the Chief of Police appointing one ( 1) member, all ratified by the City Commission. Each member appointed by the Commission and City Manager shall represent, reside and/or work in one of the 12 zones represented in the Police Patrol Zone Map. The Chief of Police' s appointee may represent, reside and/ or work in any of the aforementioned zones. SECTION 5. Appointment and Removal. That the Committee members' terms of office shall commence October 1, 2014, and run for an initial term until April 30, 2015, or until a successor has been selected and qualified, and thereafter terms shall commence June 1 of odd numbered years and expire two ( 2) years thereafter on April 30, or until a successor has been selected and qualified. All appointments for replacement of any member shall be for the said member' s unexpired term. SECTION 6. Chairperson. That the Committee shall elect from among its membership, a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson who shall serve for a period of one ( 1) year, and may be named to successive terms if the Committee so chooses. SECTION 7. Administrative Staff Support. That the City Manager shall appoint a staff member to serve as a liaison with and Secretary for the City- Wide Anti- Crime Committee, and provide services as required by City Code, Article III, Division 1, Section 2-63, including, but not limited to, preparation of summary minutes. SECTION 8. Meetings.
