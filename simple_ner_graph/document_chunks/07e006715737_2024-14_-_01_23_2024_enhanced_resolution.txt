WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables being a Subrecipient with Traffic Technology Services, Inc. on behalf of the United States Department of Energy Advanced Research Projects Agency-Energy (ARPA-E) to install additional traffic sensors in the intersection of Alhambra and Ponde De Leon, contract an electrical engineer and data scientist for the project; and WHEREAS, the purpose of this program is to conduct engineering research and implement a small scale intelligent traffic network ( AutonomIA) that will leverage sensors, connected vehicles and artificial intelligence (AI) to optimize traffic in real time, improve safety, and lower carbon emissions; and WHEREAS, through this grant program, the City of Coral Gables is a subrecipient of allocated funds in the amount of $392,361 to support three-year operations also of in-kind use of our smart city infrastructure during the project; and WHEREAS, an amendment to the Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Annual Budget is required to recognize the $ 130,787 grant as revenue and to appropriate such funds to cover the grant expenditure; and WHEREAS, staff recommends the Commission authorizes annual budget amendments, as needed, for the life of the grant program; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA THAT: SECTION 1. That the foregoing “ WHEREAS” clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon the adoption hereof.
