--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-20 - 01_28_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 00:34:17 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 20 RESOLUTION ACCEPTING THE AFFILIATION AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES AND CITY COLLEGE TO ALLOW EMT/PARAMEDIC STUDENTS TO RIDE ON RESCUE UNITS. WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Fire Department has been mentoring EMT/Paramedic students by allowing them to " ride along" on their rescue units for over twenty years; and WHEREAS, students enrolled in the Emergency Medical Services curriculum are able to acquire clinical experience by riding with our Rescue personnel; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Fire Department has been using this standardized agreement for approximately 11 years; and WHEREAS, there is no cost to the City for this program; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City of Coral Gables is hereby authorized to enter into an agreement with City College to allow EMT/Paramedic students to ride on the Coral Gables Fire Department's rescue units. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein.
