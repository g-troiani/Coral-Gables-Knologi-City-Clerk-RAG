RENEWAL OPTION: Provided Tenant has not been in default during the Term of this Lease and is not in default under the terms of this Lease at the time of exercise or commencement of this option, Tenant shall have the option, exercisable at least six ( 6) months prior to the expiration of the initial Lease Term and the First Renewal Term, respectively to renew this Lease for a period of ten ( 10) years beginning on the 1st day following the last day of the initial Lease Term and ending on the last day of the tenth anniversary thereof ( the first such period " First Renewal Period" and a second such period the " Second Renewal Period"), upon the same terms and conditions herein except: the total Base Rent <!-- image --> payable during the First Renewal Period and the'Second Renewal Period shall be equal to ninety-five percent ( 95%) of Market Rate plus annual adjustments of two percent 2%) after the first Lease Year of the applicable Renewal Period, which is defined as the Rent that Landlord should reasonably be able to obtain for the Premises effective the first day of the First Renewal Period or Second Renewal Period, as applicable which in no event shall be less than one hundred and five percent (105%) of the Base Rent in effect for the last Lease Year of the initial Lease Term or First Renewal Period, as applicable. Said Market Rate shall be based on the prevailing market rate for a comparable use and other relevant factors including but not limited to size, location, and condition. Tenant's failure to notify Landlord at least six ( 6) months prior to the expiration of the initial Lease Term of its exercise of the First Renewal Period shall be considered forfeiture by Tenant of both options to renew, and failure to notify Landlord at least six (6) months prior to the expiration of the First Renewal Period of its exercise of the Second Renewal Period shall be considered forfeiture by Tenant of the second option to renew. Landlord shall provide Tenant with its determination of Market Rate after Tenant's notification that Tenant will renew. Pending determination of Market Rate, Tenant shall pay Base Rent for the beginning of the Renewal Period in the amount of one hundred and five percent ( 105%) of the Base Rent in effect for the last Lease Year ofthe initial Term. If Tenant objects to Landlord's determination of Market Rent, Tenant shall send written objection to Landlord within ten ( 10) days of receipt of Landlord's determination. Failure to object within such ten ( 10) day period shall be deemed acceptance of Landlord' s determination . If Tenant objects to Landlord' s determination of Market Rate, each party shall appoint within fifteen ( 15) days of Landlord's receipt of Tenant's objection, a real estate appraiser. Should either party fail to appoint an appraiser within this time period, the other party's appraiser's opinion shall be binding . The two ( 2) appraisers shall appoint a third appraiser within ten ( 10) days of their appointments. All appraisers shall have been active during the last live (5) years doing appraisals of retail properties in the area where the Premises is located .
