That the City Manager shall appoint a staff member to serve as a liaison with and Secretary for the City- Wide Anti- Crime Committee, and provide services as required by City Code, Article III, Division 1, Section 2-63, including, but not limited to, preparation of summary minutes. SECTION 8. Meetings. That the Committee shall hold regular meetings and special meetings at such times as the Committee may determine, at the call of the Chairman or the Secretary. If there is no business to conduct, a meeting need not be held, however, the Committee must meet at least semi- annually to remain active. SECTION 9. Quorum. That meetings shall be called to order when a quorum is present, which shall be at least seven ( 7) members, and a decision of a majority of the members present and voting at which a quorum is present shall be the decision of the Committee. SECTION 10. Compensation. That all members of the City- Wide Anti- Crime Committee shall serve without compensation. SECTION 11. Public Meetings. That all meetings of the Committee shall be open to the public, and a written summary of the proceedings shall be kept showing its action on each question considered, and such record shall be filed in the office of the Secretary and submitted to the City Commission. SECTION 12. Written Reports. That the City-Wide Anti-Crime Committee shall submit an annual report to the City Commission in accordance Division 1, Section 2-66. SECTION 13. Compliance with Board and Committee Rules. That the Committee shall be governed by any and all legislation enacted by the Commission; as well as any applicable administrative policies that govern the functions of City boards and committees; including, but not limited the provisions of the City Code City SECTION 14. Costs. That all costs incurred for notifications, andlor courtesy advertisements, and other items necessary to conduct the business of the Committee shall be borne by the City . SECTION 15. Effective Date. That this Resolution shall become effective as of October 14th, 2014. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FOURTEENTH DAY OF OCTOBER A.D., 2014.
