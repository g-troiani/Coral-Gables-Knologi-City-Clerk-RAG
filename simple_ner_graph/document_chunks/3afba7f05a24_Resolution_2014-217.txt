SPECIAL REVENUE FUND ENTITLED, " OFFICE OF THE CITY CLERK/ DOCUMENT FILING FEE"). WHEREAS, in April of 2005 the City of Coral Gables entered into an agreement with Florida International University ( F.LU.) and the Florida Center for Library Automation ( hereinafter referred to as FCLA), for the purpose of deployment of certain materials related to the City of Coral Gables as a digital library collection, entitled " Coral Gables Memory:" A Digital Collection from the City Beautiful, under the aegis of "Publication of Archival, Library and Museum Materials," ( hereinafter referred to as PALMM); and WHEREAS, to this end in furtherance ofthe Coral Gables Memory, the City of Coral Gables has jointly worked with the FlU Green Library in producing the following three digital projects: digitization of approximately 2,700 historical photographs covering the decade of the 1940s, a " Talking Book" project ( i.e. including audio narration), resulting in the digitization of 17 Coral Gables Legislative Minute Books ( approximately 4,700 pages) covering the time period between 1925 through 1942, which focuses on the early history of Coral Gables and is now serving as an interactive learning tool for students, the general public , and researchers alike that depicts those people, places and events that shaped our City in its early years; and finally the establishment of a " Virtual Historic City" project ( Phase I), in a geospatially enabling web environment by the Florida International University Library' s GIS Center and Digital Collection Center, along with Fill's Department ofLandscape Architecture, which resulted in the completion of the following tasks: - 1) Construction of a geo- spatial database and web publishing of a Virtual Historic Space; - 2) Creation of a Virtual Walking Tour ofthe City of Coral Gables; and - 3) Establishment ofa 3D simulation of significant historical buildings and landscape; WHEREAS, Coral Gables Virtual Historic City Project Phase II will include incorporation of a Bicycle Route Planner along the CulturallEco Tour of Coral Gables; the creation of a crowd- sourcing tool set to invite students and citizen participation in creation ofhistorical content; creation of an animated 3D display of how the City grew over time and finally, the creation of a data base of historical property and land records for public to contribute scanned documents, floor plans, etc. relating to private or public properties; as weB as the creation of a mobile function of the current Digital Collection interface to be displayed and used on mobile and iPAD devices . ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA THAT: SECTION 1. The foregoing " WHEREAS" clauses are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City of Coral Gables Commission does hereby approve the concept for a collaborative project entitled, " Coral Gables: A Virtual Historic City Phase II in a GeoSpatially Enabling Learning and Discovery Web Environment." SECTION 3. That the City Commission does hereby further allocate an amount of $ 51,000 per year, for year one ( FY 2014- 2015) and $ 51,000 per year, year two ( FY 2015- 2016) of the aforementioned project, and an amount of $ 52,000 in year three ( FY 2015- 2016), the final year of funding for same; said amounts to be taken from the Special Revenue Fund entitled, " Office of the City Clerk/ Document Filing Fee" ( Account Code No. 001-0601-511-9002). SECTION 4. That this resolution shall become effective immediately upon the date of its adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FOURTEENTH DAY OF OCTOBER, A.D., 2011.
