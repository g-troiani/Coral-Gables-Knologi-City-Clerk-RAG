--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-37 - 02_11_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:05:52 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 37 RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES SETTING POLICY AND DIRECTING STAFF TO UNDERTAKE ACTION TO EXTEND TROLLEY SERVICE TO THE MACFARLANE HOMESTEAD HISTORIC DISTRICT; FURTHER INSTRUCTING THE CITY MANAGER TO SCHEDULE THIS MATTER ON THE FIRST MEETING IN MAY, MORE SPECIFICALL Y, MAY 13,2014. WHEREAS, residents ofthe MacFarlane Homestead District have informed the City of a lack oftransit services available in that area and have requested that the City extend its trolley service; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has determined as a policy matter that it is in the best interests of the City of Coral Gables to extend its trolley service south of U.S.-1 to the MacFarlane Homestead Historic District; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a part ofthis Resolution hereof. SECTION 2. The City Commission hereby establishes a policy that trolley service should be extended to the MacFarlane Homestead District to meet the need for transit services in that area. SECTION 3. The City Commission does hereby direct staff to review the matter; conduct any necessary studies or analyses; bring the results ofthe studies or analyses back the first meeting in May, more specifically, May 13, 2014 for review by the Commission, and then undertake action to extend trolley service in the future to the MacFarlane Homestead Historic District by establishing at least one stop to serve the needs ofthe residents in that area. SECTION 4. This resolution becomes effective upon adoption. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS ELEVENTH DAY OF FEBRUARY, A.D., 2014 .
