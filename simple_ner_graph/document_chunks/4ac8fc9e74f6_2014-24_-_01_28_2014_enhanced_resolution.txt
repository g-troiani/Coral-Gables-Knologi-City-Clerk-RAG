WHEREAS, nine ( 9) qualifications statements were submitted in response to the RFQ by the following: Bermello Ajamil & Partners, Inc., Kimley- Rom and Associates, Inc., Curtis + Rodgers Design Studio, Inc., RVL Architecture and Design, Inc., Calvin, Giordano & Associates, Inc., CPR Corporation, ABCOM Technical Services, Inc., Geomantic Designs, Inc., and M.C. Harry & Associates; and WHEREAS, the Evaluation Committee met to evaluate the qualification statements, which resulted in six ( 6) firms being shortlisted as follows: Bermello Ajamil & Partners, Inc., Kimley- Hom and Associates, Inc., Curtis + Rodgers Design Studio, Inc., Calvin, Giordano & Associates, Inc., ABCOM Technical Services, Inc., and M.C. Harry & Associates, with this short listed group then asked to make a presentations to the Evaluation Committee; and WHEREAS, after the presentations, the Evaluation Committee selected three firms to recommend to the Commission in the following order of preference: ABCOM Technical Services, Coral Gables, FL; Curtis + Rodgers Design Studio, Inc., Miami, FL; and Kimley- Hom and Associates, Inc., Miami, FL; and WHEREAS, the Chief Procurement Officer recommends negotiations be authorized with the recommended list of firms in order of preference pursuant to Florida Statute 287.055 known as the " Consultants Competitive Negotiation Act" and Request for Qualifications ( RFQ) 2012. 05.17; and WHEREAS, Section 2-650 of the Procurement Code entitled " Authority" authorizes the Chief Procurement Officer to determine the lowest or highest responsive and responsible offeror and make recommendations; and WHEREAS, Section 2-650 of the Procurement Code authorizes the City Manager to delegate authority to the Chief Procurement Officer to administer and make recommendations on city contracts; Page 1 of 2 - Resolution No. 2014- 24 NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission accepts the recommendation of the Chief Procurement Officer to authorize contract negotiations with ABC OM Technical Services, Inc., and if necessary, with other short- listed consulting firms as the design consultant for the renovation of the Fred B. Hartnett Ponce Circle Park pursuant to Florida Statute 287. 055, known as the " Consultants Competitive Negotiation Act" and Request for Qualifications ( RFQ) 2012. 05.17. herein. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY -EIGHTH DAY OF JANUARY, 2014.
