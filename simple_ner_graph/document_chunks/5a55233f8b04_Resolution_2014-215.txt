--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-215 - 10_14_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:26:33 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 215 A RESOLUTION ACCEPTING A GRANT FOR FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE IN THE AMOUNT OF $ 200,000 FROM THE FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ( FDEP) FOR THE CITY'S WATERWAY BANK STABILIZATION PROJECT AND AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO EXECUTE AN AGREEMENT WITH DEP FOR SAID FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE UNDER FDEP AGREEMENT NO. 15DA2. WHEREAS, the need ofbank stabilization ofthe City'S waterway C-3 just downstream of flood control structure G-93 to mitigate erosion ofthe bank, which causes sedimentation that ultimately reduces effectiveness of the control structure and also causes severe scouring of bridge pilings; and WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables has requested financial assistance in order to implement this bank stabilization project; and WHEREAS, consequently, the Florida Department of Environmental Protection has agreed to provide financial assistance in the amount of $200,000, representing of the total project's cost; and WHEREAS, in order to receive said financial assistance, the City must enter into an agreement with the Florida Department ofEnvironmental Protection; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. The foregoing " WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption. SECTION 2. That authorization is hereby given to the City to execute an agreement with Florida Department of Environmental Protection for the purpose of bank stabilization with an estimated cost of $ 200,000. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FOURTEENTH DAY OF OCTOBER, A. D., 2014.
