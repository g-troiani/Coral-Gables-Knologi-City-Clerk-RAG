Driveways. Building sites less than one- hundred ( 100) feet of street frontage shall be limited to one (1) curb-cut. To accommodate street trees and minimal sidewalk disruption, driveways and curb- cuts shall not exceed eleven ( 11) feet in width within the public right-of-way. An existing condition may require the need to allow driveways and curb- cuts within the public right- of-way to exceed eleven 11) feet in width as determined by the Board of Architects but in no case shall it exceed eighteen ( 18) feet in width.
