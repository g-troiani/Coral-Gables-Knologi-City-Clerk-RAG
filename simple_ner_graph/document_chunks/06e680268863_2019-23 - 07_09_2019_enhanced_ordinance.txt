WHEREAS, the State legislature has enacted legislation that requires all candidates for state offices to file their Campaign Treasurer Reports electronically with the Department of State; and WHEREAS, the Miami - Dade County Board of County Commissioners is currently considering an Ordinance that would require all candidates for County office to file their Campaign Treasurer Reports electronically with the Supervisor of Elections in Miami- Dade County; and WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables has been committed to the use and implementation of technology to improve government efficiency, make government user- friendly, and whenever possible, reduce the demands on our resources; and WHEREAS, the City has the authority under Section 106. 07( 2)( a)( 2), Florida Statutes, to impose electronic filing requirements for campaign finance reports required to be filed by each person, candidate, political committee, electioneering communications organization or other individual or organization required to file such reports pursuant to Chapter 106, Florida Statutes; and WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that the implementation of a mandatory electronic filing system for Campaign Treasurer Reports would continue the commitment referenced above; and WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that such a system is consistent with applicable Florida laws; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That Chapter 18 of the Code of the City of Coral Gables is hereby amended by adding the following: ## Section 18- 9 - CAMPAIGN FINANCE REPORTS ## RI Electronic Reporting. Candidates for the Office of Mayor or City Commissioner shall file their Campaign Treasurer' s Reports electronically with the City Clerk. The City Clerk shall determine the required format for the Campaign Treasurer' s Reports and shall provide copies of the form upon request. For purposes of this Article, " Campaign Treasurer' s Reports" or " Reports" shall mean the reports required of the campaign treasurers of candidates pursuant to Section 106. 07, Florida Statutes. Reports filed pursuant to this section shall be completed and filed through the electronic filing system not later than midnight of the day designated. Reports not filed by midnight of the day designated are late filed and subject to the penalties below, and all other applicable penalties. ## Electronic Posting. Campaign Treasurer' s Reports submitted by candidates for the Office of Mayor or City Commissioner shall be posted on the City' s website by the City Clerk within three ( 3) business days of date of the electronic filing by such candidate.
