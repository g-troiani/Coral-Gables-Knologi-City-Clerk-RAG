That the City Commission does hereby authorize the City Manager to allocate an amount of $ 50,000 to Friends OfThe Underline in connection with the master planning ofThe Underline Bike Study that is a 10-mile urban trail and linear park for residents and visitors, as well as students and faculty running from the Miami River Brickell Station through Coral Gables to Dadeland South. This Bike Study is expected to be completed by June or July of2015. SECTION 2. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein.
