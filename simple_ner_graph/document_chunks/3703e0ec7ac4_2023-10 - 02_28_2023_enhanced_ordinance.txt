WHEREAS, Section 74-241 of the City Code currently allows the riding of motorized scooters upon all sidewalks located within the city, except where prohibited by official posting; and WHEREAS, Section 74-2 of the City Code prohibits skateboarding on the city' s public sidewalks where posted; and WHEREAS, currently Miracle Mile, Giralda Ave, and certain blocks of Ponce de Leon Boulevard have official City postings prohibiting the riding of bicycles, scooters, and skateboards; and WHEREAS, there has been an increase in concern regarding prohibited riding of bicycles, scooters, and skateboards and potential harm to pedestrians; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has recently adopted on first reading an ordinance increasing and amending the fines for bicycle riding where prohibited; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to amend the Code to mirror those fines for violations of Section 74-241 and 74-2 of the City Code; and WHEREAS, in order to increase deterrence, the City wishes to add specific increased penalties for violations of Section 74-241 and Section 74-2 and bring the penalties in line with similar violations; and WHEREAS, this ordinance amends Chapter 1 " General Provisions", Section 1-7 Penalties" of the official City Code in order to include penalties for violations of Section 74-241 and Section 74-2; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OFCORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. Chapter 1 ' General Provisions' Section 1-7 ' Penalties' is amended as follows: 1 | City Code § | Description of Violation | Penalty | |---------------|---------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------| | CC 74-2 | Prohibited skateboard use. $ | 35.00 fine for the first violation; 100.00 fine for the second violation; 200.00 fine for third or subsequent violations | | CC 74-241 | Motorized scooters on sidewalks | 35.00 fine for the first violation; 100.00 fine for the second violation; 200.00 fine for third or subsequent violations | SECTION 3. All ordinances or parts of ordinances inconsistent or in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. SECTION 4. If any section, part of section, paragraph, clause, phrase or word of this Ordinance is declared invalid, the remaining provisions of this Ordinance shall not be affected. SECTION 5.
