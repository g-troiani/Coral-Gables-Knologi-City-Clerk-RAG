Based on the backcasting process, goals will be developed for the six focus areas identified by the City in its RFO, including Energy Efficiency, Resource Conservation, Land Use and Development, Natural Area Preservation, Recycling and Waste Reduction, and Education and Outreach . Goals will be calibrated to accord with access to financial resources, staff time, techni cal knowledge, and the ability to leverage existing relationships .
