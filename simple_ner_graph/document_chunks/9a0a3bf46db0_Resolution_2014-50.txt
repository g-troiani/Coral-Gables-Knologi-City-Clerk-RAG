PAY GRADE 17E AT $ 22.7163 PER HOUR, OR 47,249. 90 PER YEAR). ## BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the City Commission does hereby instruct the City Manager to direct the Human Resources Director that a permanent position of Commission Aide be established for the City Commission within the Office of the City Clerk at Pay Grade 17E at $ 22.7163 per hour, or $47,249. 90 per year, shall be and it is hereby approved.
