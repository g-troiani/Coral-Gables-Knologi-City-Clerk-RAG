--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2024-43 - 02_27_2024.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-06-26 11:45:42 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2024- 43 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION DIRECTING THE CITY MANAGER TO WORK WITH CITY STAFF TO DESIGN AND IMPLEMENT GREEN AND WHITE BICYCLE LANE MARKINGS FOR BICYCLE LANES THROUGHOUT THE CITY. WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables is committed to fostering a safe, sustainable, and active transportation environment for its residents and visitors; and WHEREAS, the City recognizes the importance of bike lanes in promoting cycling as a viable and eco- friendly mode of transportation, contributing to the health and well- being of the community, and reducing traffic congestion and pollution; and WHEREAS, the enhancement of bike lane visibility is paramount to ensuring the safety of cyclists, pedestrians, and motorists alike, thereby encouraging more residents and visitors to choose cycling for their daily commutes and recreational activities; and WHEREAS, the standard practice of delineating bike lanes with white bicycle logos is effective, but the addition of a green rectangle background will increase the visibility of bike lanes, further enhancing safety and awareness among all road users, and is consistent with the plans for the bicycle lanes along the Underline; and WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables aims to align with best practices in urban design and transportation planning to create a more connected and accessible cityscape; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission hereby directs the City Manager to work with City staff to design and implement bike lanes within the City of Coral Gables. These bike lanes shall feature standard white bicycle logos painted on top of a green rectangle, consistent with the shade of green used by the Underline, to enhance their visibility and delineation. SECTION 3. That in designing these bike lanes, the City Manager and staff shall ensure that standard white bicycle logos are painted on top of a green rectangle s at regular intervals and at key points along the bike lanes, to improve visibility, delineate the lanes more clearly, and enhance safety for all road users. SECTION 4. This Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- SEVENTH DAY OF FEBRUARY, A.D., 2024.
