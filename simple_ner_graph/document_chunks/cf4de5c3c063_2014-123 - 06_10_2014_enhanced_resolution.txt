The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission does hereby approve and adopt the proposed City ofCoral Gables Bicycle and Pedestrian Master Plan. SECTION 3. That this resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TENTH DAY OF JUNE, A.D., 2014 .
