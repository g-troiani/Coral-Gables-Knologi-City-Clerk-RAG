TO SELL ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES FROM 12: 00 P.M. TO MIDNIGHT AT THE PLAZA LOCATED AT 60 MERRICK WAY FROM FRIDAY, MARCH 14, 2014 THROUGH SUNDAY, MARCH 17, 2014 DURING THE 2ND ANNUAL ST. PATRICK' S DAY WEEKEND EVENT TO BENEFIT BRANCHES, SUBJECT TO FLORIDA DEPARTMENT OF PROFESSIONAL REGULATION REQUIREMENTS. BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That Satchmo Blues Bar and Grill, Inc. be pennitted to sell alcoholic beverages at the Plaza located at 60 Merrick Way from 12: 00 p.m. to midnight on Friday, March 14, 2014, through Sunday, March 17, 2014, during the 2nd Annual St. Patrick' s Day Weekend event to benefit BRANCHES, subject to Florida Department of Professional Regulation Requirements. SECTION 2. The 2nd Annual St. Patrick' s Day Weekend event will take place from 12: 00 p.m. to midnight on Friday, March 14, 2014, through Sunday, March 17, 2014. There will be live music and perfonnances. Music played outside will end at 11 : 00 p.m. on Friday through Saturday, and will end at 8:00 p.m. on Sunday. SECTION 3. That this resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. 2014.
