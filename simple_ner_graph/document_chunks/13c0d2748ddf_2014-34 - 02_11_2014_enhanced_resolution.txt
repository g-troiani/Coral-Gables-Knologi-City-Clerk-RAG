MONIES FOR THE ACQUISITION OF ADDITIONAL LAW ENFORCEMENT EQUIPMENT; THE FIRST-YEAR SUBSCRIPTION TO SNAPTRENDS FOR UTILIZATION BYTHE CORAL GABLES POLICE DEPARTMENT. WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Police Department participates with federal law enforcement agencies of the United States Departments of Justice, Treasury and/or Homeland Security in joint law enforcement operations, and the Coral Gables Police Department's participation in those joint investigations produces federally seized funds; and WHEREAS, at the discretion of the United States Attorney General under United States Statute 18 U.S.C. Section 981(e)(2) and 21 U.S.C. Section 881(e)(I)( a) and ( e)(3)(b), the Coral Gables Police Department may lawfully retain at the conclusion of the legal proceedings the federally seized funds; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Chief of Police, Dennis L. Weiner, and the Coral Gables Finance Director, Diana Gomez, attest to have deposited the sum of $ 10,500 in said forfeited funds into the Coral Gables Forfeited Asset Fund; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Chief of Police requests to expend these funds for use by the Department to acquire additional law enforcement equipment; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Chief of Police certifies that the request complies with all provisions of the appropriate F.A.F. statutory requirements as identified in the United States Justice Department's publication entitled " Guide to Equitable Sharing for State and Local Law Enforcement Agencies"; and WHEREAS, the F.A.F. Committee met on January 29, 2014, and followed the F.A.F . Committee operating procedures adopted on July 23, 2002; and WHEREAS, the F.A.F. Committee approved the Coral Gables Chief of Police' s request to expend the available forfeited asset funds on the specific items listed below on this resolution; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The forgoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption herein; and SECTION 2. That the City Manager is hereby directed to authorize the Finance Director to expend $ 10,500 to the Coral Gables Police Department from Federal Forfeited Asset Fund monies to provide for the acquisition of additional law enforcement equipment; the first-year subscription to SnapTrends for utilization by the Coral Gables Police Department. herein . SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS ELEVENTH DAY OF FEBRUARY A.D., 2014.
