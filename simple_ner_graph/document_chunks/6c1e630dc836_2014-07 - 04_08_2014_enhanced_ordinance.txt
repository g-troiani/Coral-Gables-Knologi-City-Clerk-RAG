Any paid SECTION 2.15. VOLUNTARY ASSESSMENTS. A subdivision, neighborhood or other geographically defined area may voluntarily seek the imposition of an Assessment from the Commission through the following process: - A) Upon the receipt of a petition signed by at least 51 percent of the Owners of property within a geographically defined area seeking the creation of an Assessment Area and imposition of an Assessment to fund the Capital Cost or Project Cost of Local Improvements and Related Services which will have a special benefit to the property within such area, the Commission may adopt a resolution providing for the development of an Assessment program and the preparation of a non- binding ballot to be sent to all Owners within the area asking whether they support or oppose the imposition of the Assessment. If required by subsequent resolution of the Commission, the petition shall be accompanied by an appropriate fee to recover the costs associated with identifying the costs of the proposed project or Related Service, developing a budget and an apportionment methodology, and preparing and mailing the straw ballot. - 8) Subsequent to approval of the resolution required by the Commission, City staff or a qualified expert retained by the City shall develop the requested Assessment program, including the identification of costs associated with the Local Improvements or Related Service, and development of a budget and apportionment methodology allocating such costs to the Owners of property in the proposed Assessment Area. - C) Once the program has been developed, the City shall conduct a non-binding ballot election by mail. The ballot shall include a description of the proposed Local Improvements and Related Services, a statement that such Local Improvements and Related Services would be funded through imposition of an Assessment imposed on all Owners of property that will benefit from such Local Improvements and Related Services, the proposed amount of the assessment along- with the term of the assessment, and a deadline date for return of the ballot. At the end of the ballot, it shall include a question asking whether the Owner supports or opposes the imposition of the Assessment. Ballots shall be mailed to all property owners within the proposed Assessment Area that would be subject to the Assessment. Each tax parcel shall receive one ballot and be entitled to one vote. - D) After the deadline date for receipt of the ballots, the City shall count the votes, and if at least 51 percent of the Owners who respond support imposition of the Assessment, the City may, but is not required to, move forward with the imposition of the Assessment as provided herein. In the event the Commission proceeds with the imposition of the Assessment, the City shall follow the procedures outlined herein, including, but not limited to sections 2.02- 2.08. - E) In the Commission' s sole discretion, the Commission may approve the imposition of an Assessment for Local Improvements and Related Services even if the results of the ballot reveal that less than 51 percent of the Owners support imposition of the Assessment. - F) Under no circumstances shall this Section be construed to require that the Commission receive a petition or conduct a ballot in order to impose Assessments, generally. ## ARTICLE III ## COLLECTION OF ASSESSMENTS ## SECTION 3.01.
