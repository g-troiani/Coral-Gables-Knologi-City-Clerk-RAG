--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2015-28 - 10_27_2015.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:27:41 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## ORDINANCE NO. 2015-28 AN ORDINANCE RESCINDING PREVIOUSLY ADOPTED ORDINANCE NO. 2015-24, AND PROVIDING FOR THE LEVY OF TAXES FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING OCTOBER 1, 2015 AND ENDING SEPTEMBER 30,2016; FIXING THE RATE OF SUCH LEVY; PROVIDING FOR THE SEGREGATION AND THE APPLICATION OF THE PROCEEDS OF SUCH LEVY; FURTHER PROVIDING FOR THE SEPARABILITY OF THE PROVISIONS HEREOF; AND PROVIDING THAT THIS ORDINANCE SHALL BECOME EFFECTIVE OCTOBER 1, 2015. ( PASSED ON FIRST READING ON SEPTEMBER 8,2015). ## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That there is hereby levied upon all the real and personal property within the present corporate limits ofthe City of Coral Gables, which is assessed for taxation for the year 2015 and, which is subject to taxation under the Constitution and laws of the State of Florida as now written: A tax of 5.559 mills which is a 8.21 percent increase over the rolled- back rate; all proceeds ofthe collection ofthis levy to be recorded into the General Fund of the City, and shall be reserved therein and disbursed therefrom for the sole and express purpose of paying the necessary operating expenses of the City of Coral Gables, its departments, offices, and properties, for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2016. SECTION 2. That the Tax Collector of Miami-Dade County shall be and he is hereby authorized and instructed to proceed with the collection and enforcement of the taxes levied herein, as provided in the Charter ofthe City ofCoral Gables and the laws ofthe State ofFlorida which are or may be applicable thereto and to transmit such taxes collected for the City ofCoral Gables to the Finance Director ofthe City ofCoral Gables promptly upon their collection. SECTION 3. That ifany section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, word, or amount in this ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid by competent authority, then the remainder ofthe ordinance shall not be affected thereby, and shall remain in full force and effect. SECTION 4. That this ordinance shall become effective retroactive to October 1, 2015 as a re-adoption ofthis ordinance. 2015. ## PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY - SEVENTH DAY OF OCTOBER, A.D., Moved: Lago / Seconded: Quesada) Yeas: Keon, Lago, Quesada, Slesnick, Cason) Unanimous: 5-0 Vote) Agenda Item: 3) APPROVED: J SON ~ M R <!-- image --> APPROVED AS TO FORM AND LEGAL S ICIENCY: <!-- image --> ATTEST: <!-- image -->
