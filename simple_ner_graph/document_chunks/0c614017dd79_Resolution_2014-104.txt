--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-104 - 06_10_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:15:10 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014-104 RESOLUTION ACCEPTING THE RECOMMENDATION OF THE CHIEF PROCUREMENT OFFICER TO " PIGGYBACK" A CONTRACT FROM CLAY COUNTY, FLORIDA WITH MUSCO SPORTS LIGHTING, LLC, FOR LIGHTING IMPROVEMENTS FOR THE CORAL GABLES YOUTH CENTER BALL FIELDS PURSUANT TO SECTION 2978 OF THE PROCUREMENT CODE ENTITLED " USE OF OTHER GOVERNMENTAL UNIT CONTRACTS PIGGYBACK)". WHEREAS, on February 11, 2014, Clay County, FL awarded contracts to multiple companies to provide various equipment and amenities for County parks and playgrounds for three ( 3) years with an option to renew for two ( 2) additional years; and WHEREAS, one of the contracts awarded was to Musco Sports Lighting, LLC for sports lighting of athletic fields; and WHEREAS, the Public Works Department has requested the use of the Clay County, Florida contract to make timely lighting improvements for the Coral Gables Youth Center ball fields; and WHEREAS, the Chief Procurement Officer recommends this piggyback contract with Musco Sports Lighting, LLC for this lighting improvement project in the amount of $430, 000 pursuant to Section 2-978 of the Procurement Code entitled " Use of Other Governmental Unit Contracts ( Piggyback)"; and WHEREAS, Section 2-650 of the Procurement Code authorizes the City Manager to delegate authority to the Chief Procurement Officer to administer and make recommendations on City contracts; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission accepts the recommendation of the Chief Procurement Officer to " piggyback" a contract from Clay County, Florida with Musco Sports Lighting, LLC, for lighting improvements for the Coral Gables Youth Center ball fields pursuant to Section 2-978 of the Procurement Code entitled " Use of Other Governmental Unit Contracts Piggyback)", subject to final approval and execution of a contract by City staff. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TENTH DAY OF JUNE, A.D., 2014.
