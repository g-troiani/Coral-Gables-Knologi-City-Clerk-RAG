Commissioner Lago: First of all, thank you very much. This is a very exciting day in the City of Coral Gables. This is something that I know that we've been working on since I was fortunate enough to get elected to represent this community. I requested that we have the Green Task Force here because they've been really carrying this torch for some time now. Every day that passes sustainability no longer is an afterthought, it's truly the present, it's not the future. So as our City continues to grow, like what Commissioner Quesada stated before, we swell to close to 100,000 residents a day, energy, conservation, water conservation becomes an issue and I need to make sure that our City, which we are the leaders in so many different aspects that we continue to be the leaders in every aspect and that we do not forget about how important sustainability is.
