City Commission Meeting February 13, 2024 Mr. Garcia: Good morning, Mr. Mayor, Honorable Commission, City Administration. My name is Eloy Garcia and I'll probably be the most objective individual you' ll hear from today in regard to my support for your City Manager Peter Iglesias. I don' t live in the city. Unfortunately, I moved out of the city from the Craft Section many years ago and I regret it to this day, because you have a beautiful city. I don' t work for the city. I don' t conduct business with the city, unless you count when I come with my family to eat at your lovely restaurants which are amazing, but I met Mr. Iglesias in 2014. At that point, the City of Miami was going through the largest building boom ever. I became the City Fire Marshall. I inherited a fire prevention bureau in which more than half of them, over 40 individual divisions were retiring due to the end of their DROP. I needed to find someone I can rely on, someone with the knowledge, with the experience, and the trustworthiness that I needed at the time. And at that time, Mr. Iglesias was the Building Director and the Building Official, and we immediately bonded. We worked together; we accomplished many things. I was always able to reach out to him for assistance, and we worked together. There wasn' t one time that a developer would not go to Mr. Iglesias and request something, he said, what did Fire say. Or not one time that they would come to me and say, what did Building say? And I would reply the same thing. So, that was important because Mr. Iglesias was able to navigate what I call the three legs of this tripod. One is live safety, number one. Not only from the discipline of Fire, but from the discipline of Building that' s structural, electrical, mechanical, elevator, but he also knew the other leg which was the business community and how important it is for the revenues of the city; and lastly, politics. Yes, politics. And why do I say politics, because as they asked once, Collin Powell said is there politics in the military? He said, there' s politics in all endeavors. So, I will conclude today by telling you one story, because that' s what you do when you get older and you have gray hair, like I do, it' s called experience. We were in a meeting with an elected official and Mr. Iglesias said to that elected official, Mr. so and so, I'm going to work with you. I'll work with you, this is like a football field, from sideline to sideline, but what I won' t do is go out of bounds. When we left and I said, Peter, that was great, I'm going to steal that from you, and he goes to me, Eloy, after 30 years in this business whether in the public or in the private sector, I'm not going to end my career with egg on my face. That' s the individual you have. So, I support Mr. Iglesias. I hope you do too. However, and this is the most important statement, always after the however or but, if they choose not to support you, you' re always welcomed back. Thank you very much for your time and thank you very much for your service. Mayor Lago: Thank you, sir. City Clerk Urquia: Maria Cruz.
