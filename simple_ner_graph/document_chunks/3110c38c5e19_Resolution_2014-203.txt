--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-203 - 09_23_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:24:16 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 203 RESOLUTION DIRECTING THE CITY MANAGER TO PROCEED WITH THE DESIGN DEVELOPMENT OF A NEW TROLLEY MAINTENANCE AND STORAGE FACILITY LOCATED AT 525 SOUTH DIXIE HIGHWAY, CORAL GALBES, INCLUDING RENOVATIONS AND ADDTIONS TO FIRE STATION 2; AND AUTHORIZING THE UTILIZATION OF EXISTING CAPITAL IMPROVEMENT PROJECT FUNDS ALLOCATED TO FIRE STATION 2, FOR THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE DESIGN CRITERIA PACKAGE. WHEREAS, on August 4th, 2014 the City Commission approved a settlement agreement with Astor Trolley, LLC for the development of a project at 4111 Lejeune Road, and the agreement requires that the City vacate the current trolley maintenance and storage operations at this site in a time period that allows for the new development to be processed expediently; and WHEREAS, the current Fire Station 2 site located at 525 South Dixie Highway has been identified as the best available location for the new trolley facility; and WHEREAS, Fire Station 2 is in need ofupgrades, renovations, and expansion in order to most efficiently meet the current and future needs ofthe community; and WHEREAS, during this planning/design phase, a Title VI equity analysis is being requested and the eventual final decision on siting will be subject to that study; and WHEREAS, it has been determined that it is in the best interest of the City to simultaneously proceed with design development ofboth a new trolley maintenance and storage facility and upgrades and expansion ofFire Station 2; and WHEREAS, funds are included in the five-year capital improvement plan for the purpose ofrepairs and improvements ofFire Station 2; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. The foregoing " WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption. SECTION 2. That the City Commission directs the City Manager to proceed with the design development ofa new trolley maintenance and storage facility and renovations, upgrades, and expansion of existing Fire Station 2 facility, both located at 525 South Dixie Highway, and to utilize a portion of existing capital improvement project funds currently allocated to Fire Station 2 for this purpose. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY-THIRD DAY OF SEPTEMBER, A. D., 2014.
