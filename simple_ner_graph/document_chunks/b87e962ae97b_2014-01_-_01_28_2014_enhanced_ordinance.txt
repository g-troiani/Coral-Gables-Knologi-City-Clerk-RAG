For purposes of this article, the following words and phrases shall have the meanings stated below unless a different meaning is plainly required by the context: Average final compensation means: 1) The compensation paid to participants, other than police officers and firefighters, during the five years in which the participant's compensation was the highest preceding the earlier of the date ofretirement or separation from employment, divided by five.
