The following definitions and rules of construction shall apply to this Code and to all ordinances, unless the context requires otherwise: Generally. - 1) When provisions conflict, the specific shall prevail over the general and the more stringent provision shall always prevail. - 2) All provisions shall be liberally construed so that the intent of the city commission can be effectuated. - 3) Words and phrases shall be construed according to the common and approved usage of the language, but technical words, technical phrases and words and phrases that have acquired peculiar and appropriate meanings in law shall be construed according to such meanings. - 4) Grammatical errors shall not vitiate, and a transposition of words and clauses may be resorted to when the sentence or clause is without meaning as it stands. - 5) Scrivener's errors shall be ignored and may be corrected by city attorney pursuant to city attorney section of the Code.
