WHEREAS, the City Commission has established policies for the creation of boards and committees for the City of Coral Gables; and WHEREAS, certain boards, including the Code Enforcement Board, Construction Regulation Board, and Retirement Board are exempted from the term limit provision under Sec. 2- 53 ( b) of the City Code; and WHEREAS, it is the desire of the City Commission to include the Board of Architects as an exempted board under the Code; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. The foregoing " WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. Chapter 2, Article III, Section 2- 53 of the City Code of the City of Coral Gables entitled " Terms of members" is hereby amended to read as follows: I Sec. 2- 53. Terms of members. - a) Unless otherwise specifically provided in the Charter or the Code, the terms of office of the members of each board shall be for two years or until the appointing Commissioner leaves office, whichever occurs first. A board member shall serve until a successor has been selected and qualified. When the appointing Commissioner leaves office, the board member' s term shall expire. Vacancies on boards shall be filled by the appointing Commissioner or City Manager with confirmation by the City Commission for the unexpired term. - b) No person shall serve more than eight consecutive years on any one board, and no person shall serve concurrently on more than one board unless the second board is an ad hoc board. This provision shall not apply to members of the Code Enforcement Board, the Construction Regulation Board, the Board of Architects, or the Retirement Board. SECTION 3. All ordinances or parts of ordinances inconsistent or in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. SECTION 4.
