Upon seizing the motor vehicle, the police officer shall: - 1) Provide for the towing of the vehicle to a city -owned or city -controlled facility, or to a facility controlled by the city's towing agent; - 2) Provide written notices by hand delivery to the owner of the vehicle or the person in control of the vehicle that the vehicle has been seized and impounded by the city police department.
