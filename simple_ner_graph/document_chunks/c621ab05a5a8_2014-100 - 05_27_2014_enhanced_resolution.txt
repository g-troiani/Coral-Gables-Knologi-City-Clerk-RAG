WHEREAS, the Friends of the GreenLink (" GreenLink") have proposed an initiative to transform the land underneath MetroRail into a contiguous ten ( 10) mile walking, biking exercise path and linear park, from the Brickell Station to the Dadeland South Station; and WHEREAS, the GreenLink will provide a world-class mobility corridor with widened, straightened paths and improved crosswalks that protect the users; and WHEREAS, in similar cases, such as, the High Line in New York City, property values adjacent to the once abandoned trail greatly increased; and WHEREAS, the City Bicycle Pedestrian Master Plan recognizes the M-Path as a central amenity for City residents; and WHEREAS, the GreenLink proposes to transform the existing M-Path into a contiguous park space within the City limits; and WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables recognizes that numerous economic, social, health, and environmental benefits would ensue from realization ofthe GreenLink as a linear park and enthusiastically endorses the GreenLink proposal; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF MIAMI, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption hereof SECTION 2. The City Commission does hereby endorse the GreenLink proposal for creating a ten (10) mile linear park along the Metrorail Corridor. SECTION 3. The City Clerk is directed to transmit a copy of this Resolution to Governor Rick Scott, members of the Florida Legislature, Miami-Dade County Mayor Carlos Gimenez and the Florida Department ofTransportation. SECTION 4. This Resolution shall become effective immediately upon its adoption and signature ofthe Mayor PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY-SEVENTH DAY OF MAY, A.D., 2014. Moved: Quesada!
