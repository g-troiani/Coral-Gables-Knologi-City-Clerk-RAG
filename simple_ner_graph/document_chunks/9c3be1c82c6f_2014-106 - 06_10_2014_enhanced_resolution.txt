WHEREAS, Consulting Engineering &amp; Science, Inc. on behalf ofthe property Owner, Greater Miami Hillel Foundation, Inc. is requesting authorization for an outside sewer connection to the City Sanitary sewer district to serve 15,119 sq. ft. office space and two hotel residence units located at 5225 Ponce de Leon Boulevard, Coral Gables, Florida; and WHEREAS, The property is located within University of Miami private service area pump station basin 77-UM7H; and WHEREAS, As per Miami-Dade Department of Regulatory and Economic Resources, Pump Station 77-UM7H has sufficient capacity for the proposed project and all downstream pump stations including the treatment plant have capacity at this time; and WHEREAS, Flows from lift station UM #7 will discharge into pump station UM #1 and will ultimately connect to the 18" force main located along Ponce de Leon that flows in the City's manifold force main system.; and WHEREAS, The request consists of a sewer extension of 107 If of 8-inch gravity line to connect into an existing private manhole and sewer line along Stanford Drive discharging into pump station UM #7, owned and maintained bythe University ofMiami; and WHEREAS, The University of Miami has agreed to allow the property Owner to connect to the University's sanitary sewer collection / transmission system; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That authorization is hereby given for an outside sewer connection outside the City Sanitary sewer district to serve 15,119 sq. ft. office space and two hotel residence units located at 5225 Ponce de Leon Boulevard, Coral Gables, Florida, which shall be subject to the requirements ofthe Public Works Department, as set forth in Chapter 62 and 78 ofthe City Code, Ordinance No. 2007-29, and No. 2009-39 and Resolution No. 2008-07. SECTION 2. That upon approval of CES' Letter of Agreement, the property Owner will execute an agreement, agreeing to comply with the terms outlined in Ordinance No. 2007- 29, and No. 2009-39 and Resolution No. 2008-07. SECTION 3. That the property Owner pays a connection fee to the City of Coral Gables concurrently upon signing the agreement based on the estimated daily peak flow. The fee has been estimated to be $8,873. SECTION 4. That the property Owner shall provide a Maintenance or other Surety Bond in the amount of five percent ( 5%) of the construction cost to assure timely repairs of the Owner's facilities should a failure occurs. SECTION 5. That said Surety runs in perpetuity or until connection is no longer required. SECTION 6. That the property Owner shall provide Liability Insurance in the amounts required by Resolution No.
