The 100 feet shall be measured as the distance between the nearest property line for property zoned for residential use and either 1) the property line for typical construction projects ( i. e. single family homes, single building sites) or (2) the construction boundary ( quasi property line), as determined by the Buildi ng Official, for large sites or staged work. Further, no music or programming that exceeds 60 dB or 10 dB over ambient noise at the property line of a construction site is permitted - b) Dredging, land filling. Dredging and land filling operations shall not be carried out in the city between the hours of6:00 p.m. of one day and 7:30 a.m. of the next day. No dredging or land filling operations shall be carried out in the city's residential zones or within 100 feet ofthe city's residential zones on any weekend day or any holiday ( as set forth in subsection a)). However, ifupon investigation the city manager should find that the carrying out of such land filling and dredging operation will not interfere with or disturb nearby residents, the city manager is hereby given instructions and the authority in his/her discretion to waive this provision dealing with such dredging and land filling operations, but any such deviation from the terms hereof shall be evidenced by a special permit, in writing, issued and signed by the city manager or his/ her designee, pursuant to administrative regulation number 1.24. - c) Special permits authorized. The prohibitions of subsections ( a) and ( b) of this section shall not pertain to such cases where a special permit shall have been applied for and granted by the building and zoning department of the city, providing for special inspection by the proper department authorized by ordinance to inspect the parti cular work to be done. Provisions covering applications and permits are set forth in subsection ( d) ofthis section. - d) Procedure for special permits. Any person desiring a special permit for the purpose of doing construction, alterations or repair covered by the terms of this section shall make application to the building and zoning department ofthe city, giving such information as the building and zoning department requires to identify the locations of such work, the hours outside of the regular hours of inspection during which applicant proposes to work, and in addition thereto shall request and make arrangements with the building and zoning department for any special inspection of such work that may be necessary and shall compensate the building and zoning department at the usual rate of pay for such overtime inspection, in advance of issuance of such special permit. In addition to the special permit issued by the building and zoning department, any such deviation from the terms stated in subsections ( a) or (b) of this section shall be evidenced by a special permit, in writing, issued and signed by the city manager, pursuant to administrative regulation number 1.24. All special permits shall be issued upon a showing that the public peace will not be disturbed and that public safety will not be endangered. - e) Special permits generally not applicable to weekend days or holidays. Generall y, special permits, provided for in this section, shall not be issued for exterior construction on any Saturday in residential zones ofthe city or within 100 feet ofresidential zones ofthe city, or on any Sunday or any holiday as established by the federal government and on the date observed by the federal government and as established by section 1-2 of the City Code which includes December 24th, irrespective of when it is observed by the City). Any deviation from the terms hereof shall be evidenced by a special permit, in writing, issued and signed by the city manager, pursuant to administrative regulation number 1.24. - t) Exceptions. Any construction, alteration or repair, being performed by the City, whether interior or exterior, is expressly exempt from this section.
