Landscape Standards. - a. Purpose. The purpose of the Landscape Standards is to preserve and enhance the existing landscaped " garden district" character of Coral Gables, by requiring open lawns in the front yard, requiring the planting of trees in the front yard for new construction projects to provide a more comfortable pedestrian experience in the neighborhood, preserving existing specimen trees, and locating driveways, parking areas, and paved areas to the side and rear ofthe property. - b. Applicability. The Landscape Standards shall be mandatory for all properties developed pursuant to the Residential Infill Regulations. - c. Front Yards and Courtyards.
