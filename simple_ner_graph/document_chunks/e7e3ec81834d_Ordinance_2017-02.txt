--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2017-02 - 01_10_2017.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 00:31:05 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## ORDINANCE NO. 2017- 02 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION OF CORAL GABLES , FLORIDA, AMENDING SECTION 105-26 OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES CODE TO PROHIBIT CERTAIN EXTERIOR CONSTRUCTION ON SATURDAYS ( ALONG WITH SUNDAYS AND HOLIDAYS) IN RESIDENTIAL ZONES OR WITHIN 100 FEET OF RESIDENTIAL ZONES , PROVIDING FOR A REPEALER PROVISION, SEVERABILITY CLAUSE, CODIFICATION, ENFORCEABILITY AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. WHEREAS, Section 105-26 ofthe City ofCoral Gables Code currently permits construction on Saturdays between the hours of9 a.m. and 5 p.m.; and WHEREAS, Section 105-26 ofthe City Code prohibits all construction on Sundays and holidays, as established in section 1-2 ofthe City Code, in all areas ofthe City; and WHEREAS, exterior construction can produce significant noise and cause a disturbance to neighboring properties; and WHEREAS, residents ofthe City are entitled to the peaceful enjoyment oftheir homes, especially on the weekends ; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to amend Section 105-26 to prohibit certain exterior construction on Saturdays in residential zones or within 100 feet ofresident ial zones; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THECITY OFCORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. The foregoing " WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2 . Section 105-26 ofthe Code ofthe City ofCoral Gables , Florida, is hereby created to read as follows: Sec. l 05- 26. Construction and dredging, at night, on weekend days and holidays. - a) Construction prohibited. Construction ( i.e. pile driving operations, demolition, excavations, concrete pours, etc.) is prohibited on weekends and holidays as set forth in this section. No construction, alteration or general repair ofany building, structure, or other item for which a permit to construct, alter or repair is required by any ordinance of the city shall be done in the city before the hour of 7:30 a.m. or after 6:00 p.m. on any Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday or Friday. No construction work shall be performed on any Saturday before the hour of 9 : 00 a.m. and after the hour of 5:00 p . m. No exterior construction shall be performed on any Saturday, at any time, in the city's residential zones or within 100 feet of said zones, except: { l) work being performed by a property owner/ resident under an owner- builder permit, ( 2) work being performed by a property owner/ resident that does not require a permit, and ( 3) power/pressure cleaning of sidewalks, driveways or roofs. No construction, alteration or repair of any building, structure or other item shall be done on any holiday as established by the federal government and on the date observed by the federal government and as established by section 1-2 of the City Code ( which includes December 24th, irrespective ofwhen it is observed by the City). No construction, alteration or repair of any such item shall be done on any Sunday. The 100 feet shall be measured as the distance between the nearest property line for property zoned for residential use and either 1) the property line for typical construction projects ( i. e. single family homes, single building sites) or (2) the construction boundary ( quasi property line), as determined by the Buildi ng Official, for large sites or staged work. Any deviation from the terms hereof shall be evidenced by a special permit, in writing, issued and signed by the city manager, pursuant to administrative regulation number 1.24. Further, no music or programming that exceeds 60 dB or 10 dB over ambient noise at the property line of a construction site is permitted - b) Dredging, land filling. Dredging and land filling operations shall not be carried out in the city between the hours of6:00 p.m. of one day and 7:30 a.m. of the next day. No dredging or land filling operations shall be carried out in the city's residential zones or within 100 feet ofthe city's residential zones on any weekend day or any holiday ( as set forth in subsection a)).
