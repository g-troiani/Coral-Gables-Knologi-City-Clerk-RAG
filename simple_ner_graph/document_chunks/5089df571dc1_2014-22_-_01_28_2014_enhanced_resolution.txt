WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Museum Board of Directors has requested that the City Commission appoint Commissioner Vince Lago as an ex officio member ofits Board; and WHEREAS, the Museum Board believes that the appointment of Commissioner Lago would further the relationship between the City of Coral Gables and the Museum; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission does hereby authorize the appointment of City Commissioner Vince Lago to serve as an ex officio member of the Coral Gables Museum's Board of Directors, commencing March 2014. SECTION 3. That this resolution shall become effective upon signing. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- EIGHTH DAY OF JANUARY, A.D., 2014.
