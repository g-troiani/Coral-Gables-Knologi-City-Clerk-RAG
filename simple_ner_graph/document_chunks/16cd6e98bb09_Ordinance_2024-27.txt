WHEREAS, the City Commission established the South Waterways Homeowners' Associations Advisory Board in 2023 to facilitate communication between the board members and the City staff and the City Commission regarding common concerns including security, waterway use, and traffic and road issues; and WHEREAS, the City Code currently refers to the board as South Waterways Homeowners' Association Advisory Board; and WHEREAS, the South Waterways Homeowners' Associations Advisory Board wishes to change its name to Coral Gables Waterfront Communities Homeowners' Association Board to distinguish itself from the Waterways Advisory Board and the City Commission agrees that this change would be beneficial; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That Chapter 2, Article III- Boards and Committees of the City Code of Coral Gables, Florida, Division 11 be amended as follows1: ## Sec. 2-245.6. Creation and purpose. The purpose of the Coral Gables Waterfront Communities Homeowners' Association Board South Waterways Homeowners' Associations Advisory Board will be to furnish and relay citizen input to the city regarding the communities located along the waterways in the southern areas of the City of Coral Gables and to formulate recommendations regarding security, waterway use, landscaping, and traffic and road issues that are common to such communities. 1 Deletions are indicated by strikethrough. Insertions are indicated by underline. ## Sec. 2-245.7. Membership, quorum, and qualifications. The Waterfront Communities Homeowners' Association Board South Waterways Homeowners' Associations Advisory Board shall be comprised of members, with each member to be confirmed by the city commission. A quorum shall consist of a majority of members. Each member shall be a city resident living in one of the communities listed below that are located along the waterways in the southern areas of the City of Coral Gables and such member must be designated by the homeowners' association for the respective community in which the member resides. The following homeowners' associations shall designate one member for appointment to the board, with such designation to be evidence in a written communication from the president of such association: - 1) Cocoplum Homeowners' Association. - 2) Deering Bay Master Property Owners' Association. - 3) Gables-by-the-Sea Homeowners' Association. - 4) Gables Estates Club. - 5) Hammock Oaks Homeowners' Association. - 6) Journeys End Homeowners' Association. - 7) Kings Bay Homeowners' Association. - 8) Old Cutler Bay Homeowners' Corp. - 9) Snapper Creek Lakes Club. - 10) Sunrise Harbour Homeowners' Association. - 11) Tahiti Beach Homeowners' Association. From time to time, the city commission may amend by resolution the foregoing list of homeowners' associations to add or remove associations entitled to designate a member to the board and consequently change the number of members of the board. ## Sec. 2-245.8. Terms of office, appointment, and renewal. Terms of office shall begin on June 1 for a two-year period, ending May 31, in odd numbered years. An appointment for the replacement of any member shall be for the unexpired term. ## Sec. 2-245.9. Chairperson. The Waterfront Communities Homeowners' Association Board South Waterways Homeowners' Associations Advisory Board shall elect from among its membership, a chairperson and vice chairperson if applicable) who shall serve for a period of one year and may be named to successive terms if the board chooses, at the beginning of each new term. ## Sec. 2-245.10. Administrative support staff; written reports. The city manager shall assign a staff liaison for the purpose of providing administrative support to the board. The staff liaison shall serve as a liaison with, and secretary for the Waterfront Communities Homeowners' Association Board South Waterways Homeowners' Associations Advisory Board. Said support shall include, but will not be limited to, preparation of summary minutes and preparation of meeting agendas.
