WHEREAS, discussion was had regarding purchases made by the City on Amazon over the last few months; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing “ WHEREAS” clause is hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and is hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission hereby directs City staff to conduct an audit and review of all purchases made by the City on Amazon over the last twenty -four months. City staff has three months to complete this review. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein.
