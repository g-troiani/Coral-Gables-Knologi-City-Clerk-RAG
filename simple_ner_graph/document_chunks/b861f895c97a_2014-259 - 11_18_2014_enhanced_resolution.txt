WHEREAS, in November, 2012, the City issued an International Request for Qualifications for the commissioning of outdoor civic monuments incorporating a fountain feature, to be located in the traffic circles at the intersections of Segovia Street with Biltmore Way and Coral Way; and WHEREAS, a judging panel of five ( 5) citizens with expertise in the selection of public art reviewed a total of 181 applications from artists all over the world; and WHEREAS, on March 4, 2014, the panel voted to invite five semi-finalist artists to submit concepts designs to the City: Han Averbuch, Alice Aycock, Douglas Hollis, Brad Goldberg and Larry Kirkland. All five are recognized public artists and have shown in prestigious galleries and museums. Mr. Averbuch, born in Tel Aviv, Israel, currently works from his studio in Long Island, NY; Ms. Aycock is located in New York City, NY; Mr. Goldberg is in Dallas, TX; Mr. Hollis is in San Francisco, CA, and Mr. Kirkland is in Washington D.C.; and WHEREAS, the judging panel is composed of the following members, four of whom live in the neighborhood where the Segovia Circles are located: Silvia Karman Cubifia, executive director and chief curator of the Bass Museum of Art; Dr. Carol Damian, director and chief curator of the Patricia &amp; Phillip Frost Art Museum at Florida International University and member ofthe County's Art in Public Places Trust; Brian Dursum, former director and chief curator of the University of Miami' s Lowe Art Museum; Julio Grabiel, AlA, founder and principal designer of Grapa Studio and prior member of the County' s Art in Public Places Trust; and Nannette Zapata, Chief Operating Officer of Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden and curator of its outdoor sculpture program; and WHEREAS on September 10, 2014, the panel reviewed the Semi- fmalists' presentations and unanimously voted to recommend Ms. Aycock' s proposal to the City Commission for its consideration and approval; and WHEREAS, Ms. Aycock is a nationally renowned public artist whose sculptures are on permanent display at the Dulles International Airport, the San Francisco Public Library, the East River Park Pavilion in NYC and at JFK International Airport, among many sites; in the collections ofthe Museum ofModern Art (MoMA), NY; the Brooklyn Museum; the Whitney Museum ofAmerican Art; the LA County Museum and the National Gallery; and recently in March 2014, a series of her " Park Avenue Paper Chase" sculptures were installed on the Park Avenue Malls in New York City; and WHEREAS, The Artist's concept proposal calls for two sculptures -a large work at Biltmore Way with a water pool and misting feature and a smaller work at Coral Way both of which resonate with the environment and reinforce Coral Gables' identity as a " Garden City," drawing on the passion flower as inspiration;
