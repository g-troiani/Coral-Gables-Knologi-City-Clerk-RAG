--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-182 - 09_11_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:22:26 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014-182 A RESOLUTION GRANTING PERMISSION TO THE CITY MANAGER TO DONATE AN AIR SERVICE TRUCK AND AN LTI 100FT AERIAL PLATFORM FIRE TRUCK TO THE INTERNATIONAL AFFAIRS FOUNDATION WHICH WILL THEN DONATE IT TO THE DENTON PROGRAM, A FEDERAL PROGRAM DESIGNED TO TRANSPORT HUMANITARIAN GOODS TO COUNTRIES IN NEED. WHEREAS, the Denton Program allows private U.S. citizens and organizations to use space available on U.S. cargo planes to transport humanitarian goods to countries in need, including equipment, clothing, educational supplies, food, medical supplies and vehicles; and WHEREAS, the City intends to donate a 100FT Aerial Platform Fire Truck and an Air Service Truck, that are still operational, but are no longer used by the City, to the Denton Program through the International Affairs Foundation; and WHEREAS, both trucks may be ofservice to other countries; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission does hereby authorize the City Manager to donate an Air Service Truck and an LTI 100FT Aerial Platform Fire Truck to the International Affairs Foundation which will then donate it to the Denton Program, a federal program designed to transport humanitarian goods to countries in need. SECTION 3. That said resolution shall become effective upon the date ofits passage and adoption herein.
