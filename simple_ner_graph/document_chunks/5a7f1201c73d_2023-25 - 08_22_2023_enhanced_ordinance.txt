Sec.\_\_\_\_\_. Terms of Office, Appointment, and Renewal. Terms of office shall begin on June 1 for a two- year period, ending May 31st, in odd numbered years. An appointment for the replacement of any member shall be for the unexpired term. Sec.\_\_\_\_\_. Chairperson. The South Waterways Homeowners' Associations Advisory Board shall elect from among its membership, a Chairperson and Vice Chairperson ( if applicable) who shall serve for a period of one (1) year and may be named to successive terms if the board chooses, at the beginning of each new term. Sec.\_\_\_\_\_. Administrative Support Staff; Written Reports. The City Manager shall assign a staff liaison for the purpose of providing administrative support to the board. The staff liaison shall serve as a liaison with, and secretary for the South Waterways Homeowners' Associations Advisory Board. Said support shall include, but will not be limited to, preparation of summary minutes and preparation of meeting agendas. Additionally, the chairperson shall submit to the City Commission an annual report, approved by the board, in accordance with section 2-59 of the City Code. SECTION 3. All Ordinances or parts of Ordinances inconsistent or in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. SECTION 4. If any section, part of section, paragraph, clause, phrase or word of this Ordinance is declared invalid, the remaining provisions of this Ordinance shall not be affected. SECTION 5. It is the intention of the City Commission of the City of Coral Gables, Florida, that the provisions of this Ordinance shall become and be made part of the City of Coral Gables Code of Ordinances; and that the sections of this ' Ordinance' may be changed to section,' ' article,' or such other appropriate word or phrase in order to accomplish such intentions. SECTION 6. If the Official Code of the City of Coral Gables Table of Contents or other reference portions are affected by these provisions, then changes are approved as part of this Ordinance. SECTION 7. This Ordinance shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- SECOND DAY OF AUGUST, A.D., 2023.
