WHEREAS, the City recently entered into a collective bargaining agreement with the International Association of Firefighters, Local 1210; and WHEREAS, the collective bargaining agreement contains certain changes to retirement benefits of firefighters under the City of Coral Gables Retirement System Retirement System"); and WHEREAS, to implement the Retirement System changes, an ordinance amending the City Code provisions governing the Retirement System is required; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing recitals are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a part of this Ordinance upon adoption. SECTION 2. That Section 46- 25 of the " Code of the City of Coral Gables" pertaining to " Pensions" is hereby amended to read as followsl: ## Sec. 46- 25. Definitions. The following words, terms and phrases, when used in this article, shall have the meanings ascribed to them in this section, except where the context clearly indicates a different meaning: Words and figures underscored are additions to existing law; words and figures struck through are deletions. Average final compensation means: - 3) Effective September 30, 2013, the term " average final compensation" for firefighters means: - a. For participants who retire on or after October 1, 2012, and on or before September 30, 2013, the highest three-year average total earnings as ofthe date of retirement or the highest four- year average earnings, whichever is greater; - b. For participants who retire on or after October 1, 2013, and on or before September 30, 2014, the highest four- year average earnings as of the date of retirement September 30, 2013, or the highest five-year average earnings, whichever is greater; and - c. For participants who retire on or after October 1, 2014, the highest five- year average earnings. Effective September 30, 2013, retirement benefit calculations and employee contributions for firefighters shall be based on pensionable earnings and not total earnings.
