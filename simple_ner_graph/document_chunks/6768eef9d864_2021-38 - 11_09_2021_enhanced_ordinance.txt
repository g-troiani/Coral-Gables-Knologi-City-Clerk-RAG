At the end of each quarter, the board shall make non- binding policy recommendations and recommend maintenance care and improvement opportunities , including potential new landmarks, entrances, or entrance features, and parcels to acquire for such purposes, to the City Commission for items within its purview. The board shall hold one joint meeting annually with the Historic Preservation Board to discuss matters of mutual concern. Section 2-228. Membership and Quorum. The board shall consist of seven ( 7) members, five (5) of which shall be appointed by the members of the commission each appointing one member, one ( 1) member shall be appointed by the board- as-a-whole, and one ( 1) member shall be appointed by the City Manager. All appointed members should be confirmed by the city commission. A quorum shall consist of four (4) members. Section 2-229. Qualifications. Each of the members of the board shall be City residents residing in the City for a period of greater than five ( 5) years prior to their appointment, and have a demonstrated passion for preservation of Coral Gables landmarks. Section 2-230. Terms of Office, Appointment and Renewal. The Landmarks Advisory Board members' terms of office shall begin June 1 for a two-year period ending May 31st in odd numbered years. The initial board members terms will begin retroactive to June 1, 2021 and run through May 31, 2023 or until a successor has been selected and qualified. All appointments for replacement of any member shall be for the member' s unexpired term. Section 2-231. Chairperson. The board shall select among its members a chairperson and vice-chairperson who shall serve one-year terms. The chair and vice-chair may be named to successive terms. Section 2-232. Administrative Support Staff. The Historical Resources Director shall appoint a staff member from his or her department to serve as a liaison with, and secre tary for the Landmarks Advisory Board. Said support shall include, but will not be limited to, preparation of summary minutes and preparation of meeting agendas. Public works department staff shall attend board meetings as needed. Section 2-233. Written Reports. The Chairperson shall submit an annual report, approved by the board, in accordance with section 2-59 of the City Code. SECTION 3. All Ordinances or parts of Ordinances inconsistent or in conflict with the provisions of this Ordinance are hereby repealed. SECTION 4. If any section, part of section, paragraph, clause, phrase or word of this Ordinance is declared invalid, the remaining provisions of this Ordinance shall not be affected. SECTION 5. It is the intention of the Commission of the City of Coral Gables, Florida, that the provisions of this Ordinance shall become and be made part of the City of Coral Gables Code of Ordinances; and that the sections of this ' Ordinance' may be changed to ' section', ' article', or such other appropriate word or phrase in order to accomplish such intentions. SECTION 6. If the Official Code of the City of Coral Gables Tables of Contents or other reference portions is affected by these provisions, then changes are approved as a part of this Ordinance. SECTION 7. This Ordinance shall become effective immediately upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS NINTH DAY OF NOVEMBER, A.D., 2021.
