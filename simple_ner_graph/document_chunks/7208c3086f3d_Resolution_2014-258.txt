--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-258 - 11_18_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:29:44 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 258 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING AN AGREEMENT FOR LEGISLATIVE SERVICES AND WAIVING THE REQUIREMENTS OF THE PROCUREMENT CODE, PURSUANT TO SECTION 2-583( d). WHEREAS, Gomez Barker Associates, Inc. has been providing successful legislative services, primarily at the- State and Local levels since August 2002 and has developed a unique understanding ofthe needs ofthe City; and WHEREAS, the Procurement Code Section 2-583 provides the authority to the City Commission to waive any condition of the Procurement Code upon a four-fifths vote where it finds such waiver to be necessary in order to proceed with a contract, which is in the best interest ofthe City; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has waived the Procurement Code for previous contracts for legislative services awarded to Gomez Barker Associates, Inc.; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this resolution adopted hereof. SECTION 2. That the City COlnmission does hereby authorize an agreement with Gomez Barker Associates, Inc. for state and local legislative services for a one-year period in an amount not to exceed $ 6,250 per month, with two, one-year option periods that would potentially extend the term for an additional year per option, upon mutual agreement of both parties, further stating that this authorization is pursuant to Section 2-583(d) of the Procurement Code to obtain such professional services in the most expeditious manner, and the City Commission hereby finds that a waiver ofthe conditions ofthe Procurement Code is in the best interest of the City, so as to allow the City to continue with a firm that has provided quality service and is knowledgeable ofthe City and its operations. SECTION 3. That the agreements be subject to review and approval by the City Attorney. Page 1 of2 - Resolution No. 2014-258 SECTION 4. That this resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS EIGHTEENTH DAY OF NOVEMBER, A.D., 2014.
