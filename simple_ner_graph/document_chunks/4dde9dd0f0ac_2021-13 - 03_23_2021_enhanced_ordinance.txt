Zain/ Friedman Miracle Mile Downtown District Overlay ( DO). [ formerly 4203 ] - A. Purpose and applicability. - 1. The purpose of the Zain/ Friedman Miracle Mile Downtown District Overlay (DO) Distfiet is to promote the goals, objectives, and policies of the City' s Comprehensive Plan in accordance with a set of comprehensive standards to be approved within the Miracle Mile area.
