SECTION 1. That the City Commission does hereby approve the names of elected officials, namely, Mayor Jim Cason, Vice- Mayor William H. Kerdyk, Jr., Commissioner Frank Quesada, Pat Keon and Vince Lago, along with appointed officials Carmen Olazabal, Interim City Manager; Craig E. Leen, City Attorney; and Walter J. Foeman, City Clerk, to be placed on a plaque in Ingraham Park to commemorate completion of Beautification Project. SECTION 2. That in honor of the children of Coral Gables, who will enjoy the park for many years, the names of the officials' children will be included as well. SECTION 3. That City Administration is hereby instructed to notify residents regarding ceremony in Ingraham Park on Sunday, May 18,2014. SECTION 4. That this resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FIFTH DAY OF MAY, A.D., 2014.
