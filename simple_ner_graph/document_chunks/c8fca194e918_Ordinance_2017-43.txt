Unless it is apparent from the context that another meaning is intended, the following words when used in this article shall have the meanings attributed to them by this section: Alarm business means the business of any individual, partnership, corporation or other entity engaged in selling, leasing, maintaining servicing, repairing, altering, replacing, moving or installing any alarm system or causing any alarm system to be sold, leased, maintained, serviced, repaired, altered, replaced, moved or installed in or on any building, structure or facility. An alarm business shall be a burglar or fire alarm specialty electrical contractor which shall have, as a qualifying agent, a master alarm technician with a valid county or state certificate of competency. An alarm business shall have a municipal contractors occupational license issued by the county. Alarm permit means a permit issued by the city for commercial properties allowing the installation of an alarm system within the city.
