WHEREAS, the City Commission is concerned about incidents oftheft on construction sites; and WHEREAS, Florida Statutes make it a third degree felony to trespass on a construction site with a posted "no-trespass" sign; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to require the installation of a " no-trespass" sign on all construction sites in order to facility the arrest ofindividuals who trespass upon those sites; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OFCORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing recitals are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That Division I, Article II of Chapter 105 " Buildings and Building Regulation" of the Code of the City of Coral Gables, Florida, be hereby created to read as follows: ## CHAPTER 105. BUILDINGS AND BUILDING REGULATION * * * * * ## Article II. BUILDING STANDARDS Division 1. GENERALLY * * * * * ## Sec. 105- 29. Trespass Signs Required - a) Posting Required. The pennittee and/or property owner of a construction site shall post sign( s) stating: " THIS AREA IS A DESIGNATED CONSTRUCTION SITE, AND ANYONE WHO TRESPASSES ON THIS SITE COMMITS A FELONY," consistent with Sec. 810. 09. Said sign( s) shall contain the name and telephone number of the project manager as well as an alternate individual that may be contacted in order to detennine whether persons present at the site have the authority to be there. Said sign( s) shall be provided by the City and paid for by the pennittee when obtaining the pennit. - b) The sign( s) must be posted within 24 hours ofwhen the construction fence is installed at the site. The sign(s) must be displayed prominently at each fence/gate opening and must remain prominently displayed until the project is completed and all pennits are closed on the property. At sites where a fence is not required or provided, the sign(s) shall be placed at the opening( s) of the structure. - c) Penalty. Failure to post the " no trespass" sign(s) on a construction site constitutes a City Code violation for which the pennittee and/ or property owner may be cited and fined $ 100 per day the violation is present. ## SECTION 3. SEVERABILITY. If any section, sentence, clause, or phrase of this Ordinance is held to be invalid or unconstitutional by any court ofcompetent jurisdiction, then said holding shall in no way affect the validity of the remaining portions of this Ordinance. ## SECTION 4. REPEALER. All ordinances or parts ofordinances in conflict herewith, are hereby repealed. ## SECTION 5. CODIFICATION.
