Having considered the facts in this matter as well as the City Attorney's advice, the City Commission has determined that it is necessary and appropriate to bring legal action against The Nyhart Company and Stanley, Holcombe &amp; Associates to recover the damages incurred by the City and Retirement Board related to The Nyhart Company' s and Stanley, Holcombe &amp; Associates' actions relating to the application of the maximum limits on retirement benefits contained in section 415 of the Internal Revenue Code. Accordingly, the City Commission authorizes and directs the City Attorney to bring legal action against The Nyhart Company and Stanley, Holcombe &amp; Associates, as well as any other related parties that the City Attorney determines should be named to effectuate full relief, to recover damages that resulted from The Nyhart Company' s and Stanley, Holcombe &amp; Associates' actions relating to the application of the maximum limits on retirement benefits contained in section 415 of the Internal Revenue Code, as well as any other claims that the City possesses against these parties. The City Commission authorizes the City Attorney to engage outside legal counsel to assist in taking such necessary legal action to recover any and all damages incurred by the City and retirement Board.
