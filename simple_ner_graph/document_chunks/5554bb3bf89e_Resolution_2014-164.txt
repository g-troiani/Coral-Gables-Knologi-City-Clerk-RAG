--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-164 - 08_26_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-06-26 11:45:42 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 164 RESOLUTION AMENDING PREVIOUS RESOLUTION NO. 2003- 176, APPROVING SALARY INCREASE IN THE AMOUNT OF $ 20,000, AS SET FORTH HEREIN. WHEREAS, the City Commission has determined that Resolution 2003- 176 should be amended as provided herein; and WHEREAS, the City Commission may set or amend the City Clerk' s compensation; and WHEREAS, the City Clerk amassed a distinguished 20- year tenure with the City of Miami, as well as an equally accomplished 10 year tenure here with the City of Coral Gables; and WHEREAS, the City Clerk has implemented numerous innovative administrative initiatives, such as receiving approval from the Bureau of Consular Affairs/ Department of State to become a Passport Acceptance Facility, an initiative that has generated $ 1,095, 238 in revenue since its inception in 2006; and WHEREAS, the City Clerk has also established a funding mechanism, ( i.e. Special Revenue Fund) for the following projects: Passport Acceptance Facility, Document Filing Fee, Lobbyist Registration and Digital Recordings said projects, particularly the Document Filing Fee has brought forth creative departmental collaborations Citywide, such as an automated file code indexing system to standardize file arrangement, equipment and supplies as an organizational efficiency, to date which the City Clerk' s Office, Office of Sustainability and Public Works are now benefiting from; along with other Citywide initiatives such as enabling e-recording services, which have resulted in recordation of over 1,500 documents; providing training support Citywide in furtherance of utilization of our Legistar Legislative Workflow and Document Management System) and TRIM ( Enterprise Content Management System) to conduct public records request searches, along with purchases for designated departments of certain Records Management technology hardware for those who have shown a need for same; and WHEREAS, cutting edge historic preservation initiatives, such as the " Talking Book", which chronicles peoples, places and events covering the time period of 1925 to 1942, with an audio narration along with a plethora of secondary resource materials; as well as the first of its kind Virtual Historic City project that changes the path by which history is told from a linear progression over time, to conveying history over a temporal scale using G.I.S. and Google Earth mapping technology with a time space bar to highlight certain landmarks in 3D; and WHEREAS, the City Clerk through his office has distinguished himself in bringing forth numerous innovations in technology and maintaining efficiency, including but not limited to a Dashboard Management System, complete with metrics which pull from data sources in various functional areas such as Passport Services, Lobbyist Registration and our Employee Application Reporting System ( EARS), which tracks each increment of functional activity performed by staff and assigns a time value for same; and WHEREAS, also the establishment of a legislative initiative in the form of a document filing fee legislation ( i.e. which will exceed $ 200,000 in revenue this Fiscal Year) has become a standard by which other Municipal Clerks have sought to emulate throughout the state, which addresses meeting an unfunded mandate as established in our Public Records Law, to produce sound and economical records management systems; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. The City Commission does hereby amend previous Resolution No. 2003- 176, to provide City Clerk Walter Foeman a salary increase from his present salary in the amount of $ 20,000 per annum, effective October 1, 2014. SECTION 3. That Mr. Foeman shall be afforded severance compensation equal to twenty 20) weeks of salary if Mr. Foeman is terminated for any reason other than misconduct as defined in Section 443.036, Florida Statutes. SECTION 4. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- SIXTH DAY OF AUGUST, A.D., 2014.
