WHEREAS, in July 2012, the City Commission was made aware of several issues concerning certain actions and advice of the City retirement system actuary, The Nyhart Company (" Nyhart") and its predecessor, Stanley, Holcombe &amp; Associates (" Stanley"), regarding the application of Section 415 ofthe Internal Revenue Code to the retirement benefits of certain current and former City employees; and &amp; WHEREAS, in July 2012, the City Commission was advised that the City retirement benefits payable to certain former City employees were significantly reduced by application of the limitations on retirement benefits contained in Section 415 ofthe Internal Revenue Code; and WHEREAS, questions were raised as to whether the Retirement Board and members of the City of Coral Gables Retirement System had been adequately informed about the effect the Section 415 limitations would have on the retirement benefits ofplan members; and WHEREAS, the City Commission directed the City Attorney to conduct a comprehensive review of the actions taken by Nyhart and Stanley related to the application of the maximum limits on retirements benefits contained in Section 415; and WHEREAS, the City Attorney has provided the City Commission a report of the comprehensive review of the actions taken by Nyhart and Stanley related to the application of the maximum limits on retirements benefits contained in Section 415 of the Internal Revenue Code, and the City Commission agreed with the findings in that report; and WHEREAS, as a result ofthe actions ofNyhart and Stanley relating to the Section 415 benefit limits, the City has incurred damages in the form of professional fees paid for legal and actuarial services in connection with a review and analysis of the Section 415 issues caused by Nyhart's and Stanley'S performance; for remediating the damages caused by Nyhart's and Stanley'S actions through settlement agreements with former City employees; and in the creation and funding ofthe Preservation ofBenefits Plan; and WHEREAS, the City Attorney, with the assistance of outside pension counsel, has concluded that the City and Retirement Board have a number ofpotential claims against Nyhart and Stanley in connection with the application ofthe Section 415 limits; and WHEREAS, the City Commission desires to take legal action to recover damages that the City and Retirement Board have suffered; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION. 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2.
