--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-228 - 10_28_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:27:52 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 228 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE PLACEMENT OF A TEMPORARY BANNER AT THE FIRST CHURCH OF CHRIST, SCIENTIST LOCATED AT 410 ANDALUSIA AVENUE, CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA. WHEREAS, the First Church of Christ, Scientist located at 410 Andalusia Avenue, Coral Gables, Florida has requested permission to display a temporary banner welcoming the public to Thanksgiving service; and WHEREAS, this request has been granted in the past without incident; and WHEREAS, the temporary banner will be removed on Friday, November 28, 2014; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission does hereby authorize the placement of a temporary banner ( 24' x 115') across the church pillars along Andalusia Avenue, from November 26, 2014 to November 28, 2014 welcoming the public to Thanksgiving Day service. SECTION 3. That said resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- EIGHTH 2014 .
