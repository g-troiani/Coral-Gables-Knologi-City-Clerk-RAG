--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2021-38 - 11_09_2021.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:28:33 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## ORDINANCE NO. 2021-38 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY COMMISSION AMENDING THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES CODE BY CREATING CHAPTER 2 ' ADMINISTRATION', ARTICLE III ' BOARDS AND COMMITTEES', DIVISION 7 ' LANDMARKS ADVISORY BOARD' PROVIDING FOR A REPEALER PROVISION, SEVERABILITY CLAUSE, CODIFICATION, AND AN EFFECTIVE DATE. WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables is home to various entrances, landmarks and historic features; and WHEREAS, presently the City has boards and committees in place which assist City staff in the oversight and maintenance of City infrastructure; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has identified a gap in City board and committee oversight as it relates to the City owned and/or maintained entrances, plazas, fountains, historically significant poles and lights, historic markers, and other historically significant landmarks or items; and WHEREAS, the City Commission believes it would be helpful to have a City board dedicated to identifying and proposing new landmarks, entrances, and entrance features to the City, and identifying parcels to be acquired for same; and WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that the addition of a Landmarks Advisory Board would provide resident oversight of the extensive landmarks throughout the City and allow a structure for residents and board members to propose new landmarks and entrances, identify parcels for acquisition, review the condition of entryways and landmarks and bring maintenance issues to the City' s attention in a timely manner to avoid potential deterioration; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That Chapter 2, Article III- Boards and Committees of the City Code of Coral Gables, Florida, Division 7 ' Landmarks Advisory Board' be created as indicated below. ## Chapter 2Administration, Article IIIBoards and Committees, Division 7 Landmarks Advisory Board' Section 2-227. Creation and Purpose. That a Landmarks Advisory Board is hereby created for the purpose of identifying and recommending improvement opportunities to City staff with respect to the City owned and/or maintained entrances, plazas, fountains, historically significant poles and lights, historic markers, and other historically designa ted landmarks or items. The board shall establish a collection of written records and photographic inventory of each item within its purview and document its state of preservation. The board shall also be responsible for locating and identifying potential new landmarks, entrances, or entrance features to the City, and identifying parcels to acquire for such purposes. The board shall meet quarterly, with special meetings scheduled as needed. At the end of each quarter, the board shall make non- binding policy recommendations and recommend maintenance care and improvement opportunities , including potential new landmarks, entrances, or entrance features, and parcels to acquire for such purposes, to the City Commission for items within its purview. The board shall hold one joint meeting annually with the Historic Preservation Board to discuss matters of mutual concern. Section 2-228. Membership and Quorum. The board shall consist of seven ( 7) members, five (5) of which shall be appointed by the members of the commission each appointing one member, one ( 1) member shall be appointed by the board- as-a-whole, and one ( 1) member shall be appointed by the City Manager. All appointed members should be confirmed by the city commission. A quorum shall consist of four (4) members. Section 2-229. Qualifications. Each of the members of the board shall be City residents residing in the City for a period of greater than five ( 5) years prior to their appointment, and have a demonstrated passion for preservation of Coral Gables landmarks. Section 2-230. Terms of Office, Appointment and Renewal. The Landmarks Advisory Board members' terms of office shall begin June 1 for a two-year period ending May 31st in odd numbered years. The initial board members terms will begin retroactive to June 1, 2021 and run through May 31, 2023 or until a successor has been selected and qualified. All appointments for replacement of any member shall be for the member' s unexpired term. Section 2-231. Chairperson. The board shall select among its members a chairperson and vice-chairperson who shall serve one-year terms. The chair and vice-chair may be named to successive terms. Section 2-232.
