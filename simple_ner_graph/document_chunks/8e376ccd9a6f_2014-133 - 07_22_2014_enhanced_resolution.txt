That the City Commission accepts the recommendation of the Chief Procurement Officer to award the Biltmore Golf Course Remediation Phase II Project to Cherokee Enterprises, Inc., pursuant to Section 2-828 of the Procurement Code entitled " Contract Award" and Invitation for Bid ( IFB) 2014. 04.22, subject to final approval and execution of a contract by City staff.
