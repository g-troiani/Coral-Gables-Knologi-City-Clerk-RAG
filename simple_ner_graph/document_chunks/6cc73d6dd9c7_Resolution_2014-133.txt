WHEREAS, on April 25, 2014, the Procurement Division fonnally advertised, issued and distributed Invitation For Bid ( lFB) 2014 .04.22 Biltmore Golf Course Environmental Remediation Phase II project; and WHEREAS, on May 1,2014, a non- mandatory pre- bid conference was held with sixteen 16) prospective bidders attending out of a total of thirty- three ( 34) prospective bidders who obtained the bid package from the Procurement Division; and WHEREAS, on May 22, 2014, three responsive ( 3) bids were submitted by: Florida Construction &amp; Engineering, Inc., SFM Services, Inc., and Cherokee Enterprises, Inc.; and WHEREAS, the lowest responsive and responsible bid was submitted by Cherokee Enterprises, Inc., in the amount of $ 243, 178.50; and WHEREAS, Section 2-650 of the Procurement Code authorizes the City Manager to delegate authority to the Chief Procurement Officer to administer and make recommendations on City contracts; and WHEREAS, the Chief Procurement Officer pursuant to Section 2-828 of the Procurement Code entitled " Contract Award" and Invitation for Bid ( IFB) 2014.04.22 recommends that Cherokee Enterprises, Inc., be awarded the Biltmore Golf Club Environmental Remediation Phase II project; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confinned as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission accepts the recommendation of the Chief Procurement Officer to award the Biltmore Golf Course Remediation Phase II Project to Cherokee Enterprises, Inc., pursuant to Section 2-828 of the Procurement Code entitled " Contract Award" and Invitation for Bid ( IFB) 2014. 04.22, subject to final approval and execution of a contract by City staff. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- SECOND DAY OF JULY, A.D., 2014.
