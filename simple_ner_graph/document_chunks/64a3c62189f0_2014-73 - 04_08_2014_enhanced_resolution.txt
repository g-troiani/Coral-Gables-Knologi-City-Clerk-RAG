WHEREAS, the City Commission approved Resolution No. 2011- 46 appointing Craig E. Leen as City Attorney and further approving salary and benefit package; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has determined that Resolution No. 2011- 46 should be amended as provided herein; and WHEREAS, the City Commission may set or amend the City Attorney' s compensation pursuant to section 2-201 ( c) of the City Code; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION. 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION. 2. That Section 4 of Resolution No. 2011- 46 is amended to read in its entirety as follows: That in addition to Mr. Leen' s compensation and benefits stated herein, and in lieu of Mr. Leen joining the City' S retirement pension plan, the City will contribute an amount per annum equivalent to 25% of Mr. Leen' s yearly base salary to Mr. Leen' s 401( a) plan in installments every two weeks consistent with the City' S payroll process. SECTION 3. That Section 6 of Resolution No. 2011- 46 is amended to read in its entirety as follows : That Mr. Leen shall be afforded severance compensation equal to twenty ( 20) weeks of salary if Mr. Leen is terminated for any reason other than misconduct as defined in Section 443. 036, Florida Statutes. SECTION 4. That all other provisions of Resolution No. 2011- 46 shall remain the same. Page I of2 - Resolution No. 2014- 73 PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS EIGHTH DAY OF APRIL, A.D., 2014 .
