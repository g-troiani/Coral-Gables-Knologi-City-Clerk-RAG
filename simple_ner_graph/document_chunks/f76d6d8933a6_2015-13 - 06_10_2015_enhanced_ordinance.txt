Traffic Monitoring. At the Applicant' s expense, the City shall perform annual traffic monitoring for a period of five years commencing six months from the issuance of the first Temporary Certificate of Occupancy. The traffic monitoring shall include a study of the number of external vehicular trips generated by the Project during the morning and afternoon peak periods. Should the actual number of external vehicular trips during either of these periods result in a 10% increase in the number of external vehicular trips generated by the development above that which was projected during the original traffic impact analysis, the City will conduct traffic impact studies, at the Applicant' s expense, to determine appropriate mitigation.
