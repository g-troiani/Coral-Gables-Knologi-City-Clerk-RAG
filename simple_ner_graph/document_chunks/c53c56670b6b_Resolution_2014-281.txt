In accordance with the provisions of Sections 8 and 9 of the City Charter ( Chapter 10847, Laws of Florida, 1925, as amended,) a General Biennial Election is directed to be held in the City of Coral Gables, Florida, at the polling places designated in Exhibit No. 1, attached and incorporated, on Tuesday, April 14, 2015, from 7:00 a.m. until 7:00 p.m., for the purpose of electing candidates for the Offices of Mayor and City Commissioners for Groups I and IV and V of the City of Coral Gables, each to be voted upon at the General Biennial Election, which is to be held, as provided in Article" ( Mayor and City Commissioners); Section 8 of the City Charter, wherein the candidates for each group ( Mayor and City Commissioners), who receives the greatest number of votes for the prospective office for which he or she is a candidate, in which case he or she shall be elected to said office. SECTION 2. A General Biennial Election is directed to be held in the City of Coral Gables, Florida at the polling places designated in said Exhibit No. 1, from 7:00 a.m. until 7:00 p.nl., on Tuesday, April 14,2015, for the purpose of electing a Mayor and two Commissioners of the City of Coral Gables. SECTION 3. The candidate who shall receive the greatest number of votes for Group One ( I), for Mayor, shall serve as Mayor of the City of Coral Gables for a period of two ( 2) years from 12:00 noon, after the canvass of the vote and the declaration of the result of said General Biennial Election. The candidates who shall receive the greatest nunlber of votes for Group Numbers Four ( IV) and Five ( V) s.hall serve as Commissioner of the City of Coral Gables for a period of four ( 4) years from 12:00 noon, after the canvass of the vote and the declaration of the result of said General Biennial Election. SECTION 4. The Precinct Election Clerks and Inspectors to serve at said polling places on said date shall be designated by the Supervisor of Elections of Miami- Dade County, Florida, for such purposes in accordance with the General Laws of the State. SECTION 5. The elections shall be held at the voting places in the precincts designated on said Exhibit No. 1, or as may be designated by the Supervisor of Elections of Miami- Dade County, in conformity with the provisions of the General Laws of the State. A description of the registration books and records which pertain to election precincts wholly or partly within the City and which the City is adopting and desires to use for holding such elections is as follows: All registration cards, books, records and certificates pertaining to electors of the City and established and maintained as official by the Supervisor of Elections of MiamiDade County, in conformity with the provisions of the General Laws of the State, are adopted and declared to be, and shall hereafter be recognized and accepted as, official registration cards, books, records and certificates of the City. SECTION 6. The ballots to be used in said elections shall be so prepared that the names of the qualified candidates will appear on said ballots in alphabetical order according to surname for each Group provided, however, that the ballots and their preparation shall comply with the Laws of the State of Florida, with respect to absentee ballots, and to the use of mechanical voting machines or the computer Election System (" CES").
