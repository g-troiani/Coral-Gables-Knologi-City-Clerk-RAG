Final Comprehensive Ontology for City Governance
This ontology provides a definitive, unified schema for structuring data from diverse city government documents. By integrating detailed feedback, this model adopts an action-centric approach, enhancing its capacity for procedural and temporal analysis.
🏛️ Entity Types
These are the primary classes of objects, or "nouns," in the city governance domain.
👤 Person
Definition: An individual involved in or referenced by government activities, such as an official, staff member, applicant, or member of the public. Examples: "Mayor Jane Smith," "Council Member John Doe," "City Manager Alice Johnson," "Commissioner Smith," "Smith" Attributes:
personID: A unique identifier for the individual.
name: The full name of the person.
title: The official title (e.g., "The Honorable," "Dr.").
affiliation: The primary Organization the person is associated with.
contactInfo: Email, phone number, or other contact details.
🏢 Organization
Definition: A formal group, institution, government body, legislative council, department, committee, or external agency. Examples: "City Council," "Department of Transportation," "Planning and Zoning Committee," "Planning Department," "ABC Corporation" Attributes:
orgID: A unique identifier for the organization.
name: The official name of the organization.
type: The category of the organization (e.g., "Department," "Committee," "Agency," "Legislative Body").
jurisdiction: The geographical or administrative area of authority.
address: The primary physical address of the organization.
📄 Document
Definition: An official record, report, piece of correspondence, or meeting minutes. This is the central source of information and evidence. Examples: "Meeting Minutes of 09-10-2025," "Staff Report on Traffic," "Annual Budget Report," "Staff Report SR-2024-123," "Meeting Minutes 01-09-2024," "Public Hearing Notice PH-2024-45" Attributes:
documentID: A unique identifier for the document.
title: The official title of the document.
type: The category of the document (e.g., "Minutes," "Report," "Memo").
status: The current state (e.g., "Draft," "Final," "Archived").
issueDate: The date the document was published or filed.
version: A version number or revision date.
summary: A brief description of the document's content.
sourceURL: A link to the original document file.
hyperlinks: Array of {url, text, page} objects for links within the document.
meeting_date: The date of the associated meeting (for meeting-related documents).
document_classification: Standardized classification category.
Source_File_Name: Original filename of the document.
Source_File_Path: Full path to the source file.
⚖️ Policy
Definition: A formal rule, law, ordinance, resolution, or regulation that is proposed, enacted, or referenced. It represents the legal/regulatory content. For ordinances and regulations, a separate Document node should also be created to represent the physical document. Examples: "Ordinance 2025-12," "Resolution R-2025-45," "City Zoning Code Section 5.1," "Ord. 2024-01," "Res. R-23-456," "Emergency Ordinance E-2024-12," "Ordinance 2023-45," "Resolution R-22-123" Attributes:
policyID: A unique identifier for the policy (e.g., "policy_ordinance_2024_01").
ordinanceNumber: The official ordinance number if applicable.
title: The official title of the legislation.
status: The current legislative state (e.g., "Proposed," "Enacted," "Repealed," "Amended").
effectiveDate: The date the policy's rules come into force.
expirationDate: The date the policy is no longer in effect.
legalReferences: An array of citations to other laws or codes.
documentRepresentation: Reference to the associated Document node (for ordinances/regulations).
🗓️ Event
Definition: A specific, planned occurrence in time, such as a meeting, public hearing, vote, or workshop. Examples: "City Commission Regular Meeting," "Public Hearing on Budget," "Public Hearing on January 23, 2024" Attributes:
eventID: A unique identifier for the event.
name: The official name or title of the event.
type: The category of the event (e.g., "Regular Meeting," "Public Hearing," "Workshop").
dateTime: The specific date and time the event occurs.
status: The event's state (e.g., "Scheduled," "Completed," "Canceled").
outcome: The result of the event (e.g., "Adjourned," "Motions Passed").
doc_id: Document identifier associated with the event.
Source_File_Name: Name of the source file containing event information.
🎬 Action
Definition: A specific, discrete procedural step or activity performed by a Person or Organization. This entity makes processes explicit. Examples: "Vote on Ordinance 2025-12," "Approval of contract," "Amendment to Zoning Code," "approved," "deferred," "amended" Attributes:
actionID: A unique identifier for the action instance.
type: The category of the action (e.g., "Vote," "Approval," "Proposal," "Amendment," "Repeal").
dateTime: The timestamp when the action was performed.
outcome: The result of the action (e.g., "Passed 5-2," "Approved," "Denied").
details: A textual description of the action (e.g., "Motion by Smith, second by Doe").
💎 Asset
Definition: A physical, financial, or other resource of value to the city. This replaces Financial Entity to be more inclusive. Examples: "2025 Parks Budget," "Fleet Vehicle 12," "Central Park Property," "State Infrastructure Grant," "$150,000 Parks Improvement Fund," "$2.5 million Infrastructure Bond," "Contract value: $75,000" Attributes:
assetID: A unique identifier (e.g., Parcel Number, Account Number).
name: A human-readable name for the asset.
type: The kind of asset (e.g., "Fund," "Grant," "Property," "Vehicle").
value: A financial or assessed value.
currency: The currency used (e.g., "USD").
status: The current state (e.g., "In Service," "Under Construction," "Depleted").
fiscalYear: The fiscal year associated with a financial asset.
🚧 Project
Definition: A specific, planned initiative or undertaking by the city, often with a defined scope, budget, and timeline. Examples: "Riverside Greenway Development," "Downtown Revitalization Initiative," "Main Street Repaving" Attributes:
projectID: A unique identifier for the project.
name: The common name of the initiative.
description: A brief summary of the project's goals.
status: The current state (e.g., "Planned," "Ongoing," "Completed").
startDate: The official start date of the project.
endDate: The official or projected end date of the project.
📍 Location
Definition: A physical place, geographical area, or administrative district relevant to government actions. Examples: "City Hall Commission Chambers," "District 5," "123 Main Street," "405 Biltmore Way, Coral Gables, FL 33134," "Miracle Mile between Ponce de Leon and LeJeune," "City Commission Chambers, 3rd Floor," "City Hall," "Miracle Mile," "33134" Attributes:
locationID: A unique identifier for the location.
name: The common name of the location.
type: The category of the location (e.g., "Building," "District," "Property").
address: The full street address.
coordinates: Geographic latitude and longitude (e.g., 25.8202, -80.1226).
🏷️ Role
Definition: The specific function, title, or position held by a Person within an Organization or Event. Examples: "Mayor," "Committee Chair," "Sponsor," "Applicant," "Voter" Attributes:
roleID: A unique identifier for the role title.
title: The name of the role (e.g., "Chairperson").
startDate: The date the role assignment began.
endDate: The date the role assignment ended.
🌐 Topic
Definition: The subject matter, issue, or abstract concept being discussed or addressed. Examples: "Affordable Housing," "Traffic Congestion," "Public Safety," "Zoning" Attributes:
topicID: A unique identifier for the topic.
name: The name of the topic.
category: A broader classification for the topic (e.g., "Transportation," "Housing").
description: A brief explanation of the topic.
🏛️ Meeting
Definition: A formal gathering of government officials, typically a city council meeting or committee meeting. Examples: "City Council Regular Meeting 2024-01-09," "Planning Committee Meeting 2024-02-15" Attributes:
meetingID: A unique identifier for the meeting.
date: The date of the meeting.
doc_id: Document identifier associated with the meeting.
Source_File_Name: Name of the source file containing meeting information.
sourceURL: URL link to the meeting document or video.
📋 AgendaDocument
Definition: The formal agenda document for a specific meeting, containing all agenda sections and items. Examples: "Agenda for City Council Meeting 2024-01-09," "Planning Committee Agenda 2024-02-15" Attributes:
agendaDocID: A unique identifier for the agenda document.
meeting_date: The date of the associated meeting.
Source_File_Name: Name of the source agenda file.
Source_File_Path: Full path to the source agenda file.
parent_meeting_id: ID of the associated meeting.
sourceURL: URL link to the agenda document.
📑 Section
Definition: A section or category within an agenda document that groups related agenda items. Examples: "Regular Business," "Public Hearings," "Consent Agenda," "New Business" Attributes:
sectionID: A unique identifier for the section.
code: The section name or code (e.g., "E", "F", "G").
order: The order of this section within the agenda.
meeting_date: The date of the associated meeting.
parent_agenda_doc_id: ID of the parent agenda document.
section_type: Standardized type (PRESENTATIONS, CONSENT, PUBLIC_COMMENT, REGULAR_BUSINESS, NEW_BUSINESS, EMERGENCY).
📋 AgendaItem
Definition: A specific item on a meeting agenda, typically with an alphanumeric identifier. Examples: "E-1," "F-10," "R-2024-123" Attributes:
itemID: The agenda item identifier.
title: Brief description of the item.
type: Category (e.g., "Resolution," "Ordinance," "Discussion").
presenter: Person presenting the item.
estimatedDuration: Expected discussion time.
meeting_date: The date of the meeting this item belongs to.
document_reference: Reference to associated document (e.g., "Ordinance 2024-01").
order: Numeric order within the section.
document_type: Type of document being discussed.
document_classification: Classification of the associated document.
parent_section_id: ID of the parent Section.
sourceURLs: Array of related URLs.
hyperlinks: Array of {url, text, page} objects for richer link metadata.
📋 AgendaDocument
Definition: The formal agenda document for a specific meeting, containing all agenda sections and items. This is a specialized Document that structures the meeting's business.
Examples: "Agenda for City Council Meeting 2024-01-09", "Planning Committee Agenda 2024-02-15"
Attributes:
    agendaDocID: A unique identifier for the agenda document.
    title: The title of the agenda document.
    type: Fixed as "agenda".
    status: The current state (e.g., "Draft", "Final").
    issueDate: The date the agenda was published.
    meeting_date: The date of the associated meeting.
    parent_meeting_id: ID of the associated Event (meeting).
    Source_File_Name: Name of the source agenda file.
    Source_File_Path: Full path to the source agenda file.
    sourceURL: URL link to the agenda document.

📑 Section
Definition: A logical grouping within an agenda document that organizes related agenda items by category or purpose.
Examples: "CONSENT AGENDA", "PUBLIC COMMENTS", "REGULAR BUSINESS", "PRESENTATIONS AND PROTOCOL DOCUMENTS"
Attributes:
    sectionID: A unique identifier for the section.
    name: Human-readable section name.
    code: Section code (e.g., "A", "B", "C").
    section_type: Standardized category (PRESENTATIONS, CONSENT, PUBLIC_COMMENT, REGULAR_BUSINESS, NEW_BUSINESS, EMERGENCY).
    order: The order of this section within the agenda.
    parent_agenda_doc_id: ID of the parent AgendaDocument.
    meeting_date: The date of the associated meeting.
📃 Contract
Definition: A formal agreement between the city and another party. Examples: "Contract No. 2024-15," "RFP-2023-456," "Bid Award #789," "Contract No. 2024-15" Attributes:
contractID: Unique contract identifier.
title: Contract description.
vendor: Organization awarded the contract.
amount: Total contract value.
startDate: Contract effective date.
endDate: Contract expiration date.
status: Current state (e.g., "Active," "Pending," "Expired").
💻 Technology
Definition: Software, platform, or technical system used by the city. Examples: "Microsoft Teams," "Granicus," "Accela," "Tyler Munis" Attributes:
techID: Unique identifier.
name: Product name.
vendor: Company providing the technology.
purpose: Primary use case.
licenseType: Licensing model.
🗳️ VoteOutcome
Definition: Detailed record of a voting action with specific tallies. Examples:
{"id": "outcome_E-1_2024-01-09", "type": "vote_outcome", "status": "passed", "yes_votes": 5, "no_votes": 2, "details": "Motion passed with Commissioner X abstaining"}
"Vote on Ord. 2024-01: Passed 5-2"
"Motion to defer E-1: Failed 3-4" Attributes:
outcomeID: Unique identifier (e.g., "outcome_E-1_2024-01-09").
agendaItemID: Related agenda item (was "agendaItem").
result: The outcome (alternative to "status").
status: Result (e.g., "passed", "failed", "tabled", "deferred").
yesVotes: Number of affirmative votes.
noVotes: Number of negative votes.
abstentions: Number of abstentions.
voteDetails: Array of [Person, vote] pairs.
🔗 Relationship Types
These connections define how entities interact. This model is action-centric, using the Action entity to explicitly represent processes.
Relationship
Connects (Source → Target)
Description & Example
isMemberOf
Person → Organization
Links an individual to a group they belong to. Ex: "Jane Smith" isMemberOf "City Council."
isPartOf
Organization → Organization
Defines institutional hierarchy. Ex: "Planning Committee" isPartOf "Department of Development."
holdsRole
Person → Role
Assigns a specific function or title to a person. Ex: "Jane Smith" holdsRole "Mayor."
participatesIn
Person, Organization → Event
Shows attendance or active involvement in an event. Ex: "Mayor Doe" participatesIn "Budget Hearing."
authoredBy
Document, Policy → Person, Organization
Identifies the creator or official issuer of a document. Ex: "Staff Report" authoredBy "Dept. of Transport."
sponsors
Person, Organization → Policy, Project
Signifies formal sponsorship or primary advocacy for an initiative. Ex: "Council Member Doe" sponsors "Resolution R-2025-45."
performsAction
Person, Organization → Action
The core relationship linking an agent to a procedural step. Ex: "City Council" performsAction "Action #789" (Vote on Ordinance).
targetOf
Action → Document, Policy, Project, Asset
Connects an Action to the entity it affects. Ex: "Action #789" is targetOf "Ordinance 2025-12."
recordedIn
Action → Document
EXPANDED: Can also be Event → Document (for transcripts)
Indicates where an action or event is officially recorded.
Ex: "Action #789" recordedIn "Meeting Minutes"
Ex: "City Council Meeting" recordedIn "Verbatim Transcript 2024-01-09"
isLocatedAt
Organization, Project → Location
Specifies the primary, static physical location of an entity. Ex: "The Dept. of Parks" isLocatedAt "City Hall Annex."
occursAt
Event → Location
Specifies the location where a dynamic event takes place. Ex: "The Public Hearing" occursAt "City Hall Council Chambers."
references
Document, Policy → Document, Policy, Topic
Indicates that one document cites or mentions another entity. Ex: "Ordinance 2025-12" references "City Charter §5.1."
amends
Policy → Policy
(Separated) Shows that one legal instrument officially modifies another. Ex: "Ordinance 2025-45" amends "Ordinance 2015-23."
repeals
Policy → Policy
(Separated) Shows that one legal instrument officially nullifies another. Ex: "Ordinance 2025-99" repeals "Ordinance 2011-05."
owns
Person, Organization → Asset
Denotes legal ownership or stewardship of an asset. Ex: "The City" owns "Central Park Property."
funds
Asset → Project, Organization
Specifies the source of funding (Asset of type 'Financial') for an initiative. Ex: "State Grant" funds "Park Renovation Project."
addressesTopic
Document, Event, Project → Topic
Connects a resource or discussion to its primary subject matter. Ex: "Meeting on Housing" addressesTopic "Affordable Housing."
discusses
Event → AgendaItem, Policy, Document, Topic
Links an event to specific agenda items covered. CLARIFICATION: Can also be Event → Policy, Event → Document, Event → Topic. An event can discuss multiple types of entities. Ex: "City Commission Meeting" discusses "E-1."
hasAgenda
Event → AgendaDocument
Links a meeting event to its formal agenda document. Ex: "City Council Meeting 2024-01-09" hasAgenda "Agenda Document for 2024-01-09."
hasSection
AgendaDocument → Section
Links an agenda document to its constituent sections. Ex: "Agenda Document" hasSection "Regular Business Section."
hasAgendaItem
Section → AgendaItem
Links a section to the specific agenda items it contains. Ex: "Regular Business Section" hasAgendaItem "E-1."
precedes
AgendaItem → AgendaItem
Shows the temporal order of agenda items within a meeting. Ex: "E-1" precedes "E-2."
resultsIn
AgendaItem → VoteOutcome
Connects an agenda item to its voting result. Ex: "E-1" resultsIn "outcome_E-1_2024-01-09."
governedBy
Contract → Policy
Links a contract to its authorizing legislation. Ex: "Contract No. 2024-15" governedBy "Ordinance 2024-01."
uses
Organization → Technology
Shows technology adoption by an organization. Ex: "City Clerk's Office" uses "Granicus."
votedOn
VoteOutcome → Policy, Contract, Project
Links a vote outcome to what was voted on. Ex: "outcome_E-1_2024-01-09" votedOn "Ordinance 2024-01."
presents
Person → AgendaItem
Shows who presents an agenda item. Ex: "Commissioner Jane Doe" presents "E-1."
awards
Organization → Contract
Shows which organization awards a contract. Ex: "City Council" awards "Contract No. 2024-15."
awardedTo
Contract → Organization
Shows the recipient of a contract. Ex: "Contract No. 2024-15" awardedTo "ABC Corporation."
implementedBy
Policy → Document
CLARIFICATION: This is the passive direction. A Policy is implementedBy its Document representation. The active form "implements" (Document/AgendaItem implements Policy) is the inverse. Both directions may be needed depending on context. Links a legal rule/regulation to its physical document representation. Ex: "Ordinance 2024-01" implementedBy "Document: Ordinance 2024-01 PDF."
embodies
Document → Policy
Links a legal document to the policy/rule it contains. Ex: "Document: Ordinance 2024-01 PDF" embodies "Ordinance 2024-01."
implements
AgendaItem → Policy, Document
An agenda item implements or enacts a policy or document. Ex: "Agenda Item E-1" implements "Ordinance 2024-01." Note: This is the active direction (item implements policy), complementary to "implementedBy" (policy implementedBy document).

📋 AgendaDocument Relationships
AgendaDocument inherits all Document relationships PLUS:

authoredBy
AgendaDocument → Person, Organization
AgendaDocument can be created by individuals or organizations. Ex: "Agenda Document 2024-01-09" authoredBy "City Clerk's Office."

references
AgendaDocument → Document, Policy, Topic
AgendaDocument can reference other documents, policies, or topics. Ex: "Agenda Document" references "Budget Policy 2024."

recordedIn
Action → AgendaDocument
Actions can be recorded in agenda documents. Ex: "Meeting Action #123" recordedIn "Agenda Document 2024-01-09."

hasAgenda
Event → AgendaDocument
Links a meeting event to its formal agenda document. Ex: "City Council Meeting 2024-01-09" hasAgenda "Agenda Document for 2024-01-09."

hasSection
AgendaDocument → Section
Links an agenda document to its constituent sections. Ex: "Agenda Document" hasSection "Regular Business Section."

📑 Section Relationships

hasSection
AgendaDocument → Section
Part of hasSection relationship from AgendaDocument. Ex: "Agenda Document" hasSection "Consent Agenda Section."

hasAgendaItem
Section → AgendaItem
Links a section to the specific agenda items it contains. Ex: "Regular Business Section" hasAgendaItem "E-1."

addressesTopic
Section → Topic
Section can address specific topics. Ex: "Budget Section" addressesTopic "Municipal Finance."

precedesSection
Section → Section
Shows the temporal order of sections within an agenda. Ex: "Consent Agenda" precedesSection "Regular Business."

📐 Relationship Direction Guidelines

Some relationships have both active and passive forms:
- implements (active) vs. implementedBy (passive)
- authors (active) vs. authoredBy (passive)  
- awards (active) vs. awardedTo (passive)

When both forms exist:
- Use the form that makes the query most natural
- The system should understand both directions
- Storage may use canonical direction and infer the inverse

For agenda item voting flows:
AgendaItem → resultsIn → VoteOutcome
VoteOutcome → votedOn → Policy/Contract/Project

For document relationships:
Document → embodies → Policy (document contains the policy text)
Policy → implementedBy → Document (policy is implemented by the document)
AgendaItem → implements → Policy (agenda item enacts the policy)

📏 Attribute Naming Conventions

For consistency across the system:
- Use camelCase for all attributes (e.g., meetingDate, not meeting_date)
- ID fields should be {entityType}ID (e.g., personID, documentID)
- Date fields should include "Date" or "DateTime" suffix
- Status fields should be named "status" (not "state" or "result")
- Source tracking: Source_File_Name, Source_File_Path (exceptions to camelCase for legacy compatibility)
