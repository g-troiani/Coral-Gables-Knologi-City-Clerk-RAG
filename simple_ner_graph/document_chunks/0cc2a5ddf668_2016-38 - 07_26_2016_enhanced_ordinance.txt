For single special events or nonrecurring parades, an application for a permit shall be filed with the events committee, with copies to the chief of police and fire chief, at least 30 and not more than 180 days before the special event or parade is proposed to commence. The events committee may waive the minimum 30-day filing period and accept an application filed within a shorter period if, after due consideration of the date, time, place, and nature of the special event or parade, the anticipated number of participants, and the city services required in connection with the event, the events committee determines that the waiver will not present a hazard to public safety. Where the events committee authorizes a single special event or nonrecurring parade to be held less than 30 days following the submission of the permit application, the applicant shall be required to pay two times the standard application fee and all actual costs incurred by the city in expediting said request, including overtime costs. Said costs are in addition to any and all other costs and charges contained in this article. - 2) Timing ojapplication Jor reoccurring special events or parades.
