--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Meeting Date: 02.13.2024 - Source File: 2024-30 - 02_13_2024.pdf **ENTITIES IN THIS DOCUMENT:** - AGENDA_ITEM: G-3 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2024- 30 A RESOLUTION OF THE CITY COMMISSION AUTHORIZING CITY STAFF TO NEGOTIATE AND EXECUTE A CONTRACT WITH FREEBEE TO PROVIDE ON-DEMAND PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION SERVICES FOR ONE ( 1) YEAR WITH TWO ( 2) ONE YEAR OPTIONS, AT AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $ 520,785 ANNUALLY PURSUANT TO SECTION 2-607(17) OF THE PROCUREMENT CODE ENTITLED ' EXEMPTIONS ( PURCHASE OF PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION SERVICES)'. WHEREAS, the City Commission has demonstrated an ongoing commitment to providing high quality on demand transportation services to support our central business district; and WHEREAS, the Point to Point transportation service provided by Freebee that started during the Streetscape Project and continued over the last six years has been a big success with over 325,000 passengers; and WHEREAS, the City wishes to continue providing an ongoing free, ' last-mile' public transportation service within the Downtown area; and WHEREAS, FDOT has agreed to support the City's efforts to enhance transit services and expand transportation choices within Coral Gables by providing a matching Service Development Grant; and WHEREAS, the City was notified by FDOT on October 9, 2023 that it was awarded a grant (Award FM# 453981-1-84-01) in the amount of $253,000; and WHEREAS, the City accepted the FDOT grant on December 12, 2023 pursuant to Resolution 2023-364; and WHEREAS, the Grant will provide the City funding for up to 50% of the cost of providing Freebee On-Demand Transit Service via Electric Vehicles; and WHEREAS, Freebee will be converting the existing fleet of 5 GEM electric vehicles to Tesla Model X electric vehicles; and WHEREAS, the City and Freebee will modify the existing schedule ( 10am-10pm) to extend operating hours during peak days/times such as Friday and Saturday night; and WHEREAS, the revised schedule will be Monday-Thursday 10am-9pm, Friday and Saturday 10am-11:59pm and Sunday 10am-7pm; and WHEREAS, Freebee has agreed to provide the new fleet vehicles in advance of the start of the FDOT grant and will honor the current monthly rate until the grant commences; and WHEREAS, Freebee will provide a advertising revenue guarantee in the amount of 24,000 that Freebee will keep and instead provide an upfront discount on the operation cost; and WHEREAS, under Section 2-607(17) of the Procurement Code, entitled ' Exemptions Purchase of Public Transportation Services),' transportation services that serve the general public are exempt from the Procurement Code; and WHEREAS, the City is interested in enhancing first and last mile transit options for residents, employees and visitors to Coral Gables; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission authorizes City Staff to negotiate and execute a contract with Freebee to provide on-demand public transportation services for one (1) year with two (2) one-year options, subject to receipt of State funding, at an amount not to exceed $520,785 annually pursuant to Section 2-607(17) of the Procurement Code entitled ' Exemptions (Purchase of Public Transportation Services)'. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. ## PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS THIRTEENTH DAY OF FEBRUARY, A.D., 2024.
