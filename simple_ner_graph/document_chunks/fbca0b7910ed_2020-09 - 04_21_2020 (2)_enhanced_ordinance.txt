( FOR RATIFICATION) WHEREAS, on March, 12, 2020, the Mayor of the City of Coral Gables, Florida declared a state of emergency for the City of Coral Gables as authorized by Chapter 252.38( 3)( a), Florida Statutes, and Section 27- 2( c)( 6) of the City Code in response to the Novel Coronavirus Disease2019 ( COVID- 19); and WHEREAS, as the Novel Coronavirus Disease ( COVID- 19) continues to spread in the area and the City, County, and State remain in a state of emergency; and WHEREAS, the current City Code provisions regarding emergency situations and emergency powers do not contemplate such prolonged and ongoing states of emergency; and WHEREAS, given the communicable nature of the virus, the City has suspended all advisory board meetings until future notice, and has suspended all quasi-judicial board meetings for the month of April; and - WHEREAS, the indefinite nature of this state of emergency, along with the CDC recommended social distancing guidelines will continue to prohibit regularly scheduled in - person board and commission meetings for an extended period; and WHEREAS, though the City of Coral Gables is following and enforcing all guidelines and executive/ emergency orders, including the Governor' s Executive Orders 20- 91 and 20- 92, Miami Dade County Emergency Order 12- 20, and the City' s own " Safer at Home" Declaration, residents and businesses in Coral Gables continue to access City services and require City staff to review and approve applications, permits, and respond to resident inquiries; and WHEREAS, granting the City Manager the authority to expand the types of administrative reviews to balance the prolonged nature ofthis current emergency and with the property rights and interests that residents and businesses have in continuing to conduct businesses with and within the City; and WHEREAS, the position of Director of Public Safety no longer exists, and the City Code should be updated to reflect that the Fire Chief oversees the Emergency Management Division and serves as head of the Emergency Operations Center; and WHEREAS, during local states of emergency the City Manager should be authorized, consistent with his or her authority in the City Charter and City Code, to delegate certain tasks and responsibilities to department directors as well as assistant city managers; and WHEREAS, should a local business continue to operate in violation of any city, county, or statewide emergency orders, the City Manager should have the authority to revoke that business' s certificate of use in order to ensure the public health, safety, and welfare;
