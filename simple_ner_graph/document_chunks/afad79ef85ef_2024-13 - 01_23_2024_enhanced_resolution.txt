COMMISSION OF THE CITY OFCORAL GABLES AS FOLLOWS: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2. The City Commission does hereby approve support of the public art installation ' The Water Below Us', contingent upon approval of the art design through the Historical Resources and Cultural Arts Department. SECTION 3. The City Commission authorizes an amendment to the Fiscal Year 20232024 Annual Budget to recognize the $50,000 transfer from the Art In Public Places reserve as revenue and to appropriate such funds to be used towards the project. SECTION 4. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY-THIRD DAY OF JANUARY, A.D., 2024.
