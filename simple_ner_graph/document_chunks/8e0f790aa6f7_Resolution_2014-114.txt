The subject matter of paragraphs ( a), ( b), ( c) and ( d) of section 166.021(3), Florida Statutes, are not relevant to imposition ofAssessments related to the Miracle Mile Improvement Project within the City. - C) The City Commission has enacted the Code to provide for the creation of Assessment Areas and authorize the imposition of Assessments to fund the cost of Local Improvements and Related Services that benefit the property located therein.
