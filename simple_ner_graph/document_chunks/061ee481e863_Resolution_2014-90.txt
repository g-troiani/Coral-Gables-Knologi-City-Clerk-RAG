--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-90 - 05_05_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:12:35 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014-90 ## RESOLUTION APPROVING AGREEMENT WITH INTERIM CITY MANAGER WHEREAS, the City Commission appointed Cannen Olazabal to be the Interim City Manager ofthe City ofCoral Gables, effective as ofApril 19, 2014; and WHEREAS, Carmen Olazabal was previously the Assistant City Manager and would like to return to being Assistant City Manager once her tenure as Interim City Manager is complete; and WHEREAS, the responsibilities of being Interim City Manager are significant, and exceed the responsibilities and time commitment ofbeing Assistant City Manager; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. Carmen Olazabal will serve as Interim City Manager until the City Commission appoints another permanent or Interim City Manager, or until she relinquishes the position. SECTION 3. As Interim City Manager, Ms. Olazabal will continue to be entitled to all ofthe pay and benefits she received as Assistant City Manager, except her salary will increase by the standard ten ( 10) percent for working outside of her job classification while she serves as Interim City Manager to account for the increased responsibilities and time commitment ofthe position. Likewise, her pension contribution will be five (5) percent, consistent with section 5034 of the City Code. These changes to Ms. Olazabal's salary and pension contribution are effective as ofApril 19, 2014. SECTION 4. Upon appointment of a permanent City Manager or another Interim City Manager, Ms. Olazabal will revert to holding her former position ofAssistant City Manager, and her pension contribution will revert to the rate that the Assistant City Manager and other exempt employees would pay. SECTION 5. This agreement does not vest Ms. Olazabal with any additional rights that she would not otherwise have to continued employment with the City, including in the position of Assistant City Manager. Ms. Olazabal may be moved from that position in the discretion of her successor, subject to her rights under section 22 of the City Charter. The City Commission also retains its full rights pursuant to the City Charter, including its rights under sections 20 and 23 ofthe City Charter. SECTION 6. That Ms. Olazabal shall be afforded severance compensation equal to twenty ( 20) weeks of her then present salary if Ms. Olazabal's employment with the City is terminated for any reason other than misconduct as defined in Section 443.036, Florida Statutes, but only in the event that such termination of employment occurs while she is Interim City Manager or within six ( 6) months after Ms. Olazabal's tenure as Interim City Manager is completed. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FIFTH DAY OF MAY, A.D., 2014.
