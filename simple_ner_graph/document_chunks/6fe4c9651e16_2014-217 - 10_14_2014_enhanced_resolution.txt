Establishment ofa 3D simulation of significant historical buildings and landscape; WHEREAS, Coral Gables Virtual Historic City Project Phase II will include incorporation of a Bicycle Route Planner along the CulturallEco Tour of Coral Gables; the creation of a crowd- sourcing tool set to invite students and citizen participation in creation ofhistorical content; creation of an animated 3D display of how the City grew over time and finally, the creation of a data base of historical property and land records for public to contribute scanned documents, floor plans, etc. relating to private or public properties; as weB as the creation of a mobile function of the current Digital Collection interface to be displayed and used on mobile and iPAD devices . ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA THAT: SECTION 1. The foregoing " WHEREAS" clauses are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City of Coral Gables Commission does hereby approve the concept for a collaborative project entitled, " Coral Gables: A Virtual Historic City Phase II in a GeoSpatially Enabling Learning and Discovery Web Environment." SECTION 3. That the City Commission does hereby further allocate an amount of $ 51,000 per year, for year one ( FY 2014- 2015) and $ 51,000 per year, year two ( FY 2015- 2016) of the aforementioned project, and an amount of $ 52,000 in year three ( FY 2015- 2016), the final year of funding for same; said amounts to be taken from the Special Revenue Fund entitled, " Office of the City Clerk/ Document Filing Fee" ( Account Code No. 001-0601-511-9002). SECTION 4. That this resolution shall become effective immediately upon the date of its adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FOURTEENTH DAY OF OCTOBER, A.D., 2011.
