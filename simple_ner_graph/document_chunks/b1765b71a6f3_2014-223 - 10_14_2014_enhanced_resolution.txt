In accordance with the provisions of Section 218.385, Florida Statutes, the Borrower hereby determines that a negotiated sale is in the best interest of the Borrower and hereby approves the negotiation of the Loans with the Commission. Negotiation of the Loans will allow the City to access markets not otherwise accessible to the Borrower for borrowings less than $25,000, 000 at total costs and rates favorable to the Borrower. It is not reasonably expected that the total amount of debt to be incurred by the Borrower to reimburse itself for expenditures paid with respect to the Project will exceed $ 25,000,000.
