Code Section 2-201(e)(9), Chapter 38, Article II, should be deleted from the Code. City City, City Specifically, Chapter 38, Article II, was expressly repealed through the passage of Chapter enacted through Ordinance Number 2006-04 (the &lt;Ordinance'), passed and adopted on the twenty-eighth of March 2006. Indeed, the Ordinance; in Section 2, stated that &lt;the Code of the City of Coral Gables, Chapter 16, Article V, entitled 'Noises,' is hereby repealed and a new ordinance is created. Therefore; Chapter 38, which was Chapter 16 at the time the Ordinance passed, was expressly repealed and should, therefore; be deleted from the City Code. 34, day Moreover, Chapter 38, Article II, should be deleted because it was superseded by the Ordinance. Indeed, Chapter 38, Article II was encoded in 1958 while Chapter 34, Article V was encoded in 2006. Additionally; Chapter Article V, fully incorporated the provisions of Chapter 38, and, therefore; rendered Chapter 38 superseded. 34, Historically; Chapter 38, Article II, was encoded under Chapter 16, as evidenced by the editorial notes contained in the Code. City Accordingly, based on the foregoing, and pursuant to your authority under City Code Section 2201( e)( 9), which expressly grants the City Attorney the authority to " to delete provisions that have been superseded," it is my opinion that Chapter 38, Article II, " Noise" should be deleted from the City Code.
