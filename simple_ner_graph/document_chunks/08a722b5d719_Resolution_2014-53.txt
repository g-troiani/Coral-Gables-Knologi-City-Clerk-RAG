That the City Commission does hereby accept the City Attorney 's interpretation of the Zoning Code regarding the Transfer of Development Rights ( TDR) , which is attached to this resolution; further stating that the maximum amount of the development rights that a donor site is able to provide for calculation purposes is 3 . 5 Floor Area Ratio ( FAR); further stipulating that said interpretation be accompanied by the Ordinance No. 29730, and that those owners ofhistoric properties who would receive additional FAR be so notified. SECTION 2. That said resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein.
