WHEREAS, the Mayor is the ceremonial head of the City and serves as the City's representative in negotiations, meetings and functions; and WHEREAS, the Mayor serves as the Chair ofthe City Commission; and WHEREAS, Section 6 ofthe City Charter, provides that the Mayor presides at meetings ofthe City Commission and performs duties consistent with his or her office and the Charter as maybeimposed bythe Commission; and WHEREAS, Section 6 of the City Charter also provides that the Mayor shall be recognized as the official head of the city for all ceremonial purposes, by the courts for the purpose ofsef\;'.ing civil processes and by the Governor for military purposes; and WHEREAS, the Mayor has customarily exercised the authority ofthe Office ofMayor, but it would be useful to codify this authority to provide clarity on the particular scope of that authority; ## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That Section 2-28 of the Code of the City of Coral Gables, Florida, is hereby created to read as follows: ## Section 2-28. Mayor; Duties and Powers - a) Function. Section 6 of the City Charter vests the Mayor with the authority to preside over meetings of the Commission and to generally perform duties consistent with his or her office. This ordinance specifies the Mayor's duties and powers. - b) Duties. The Mayor shall have the following authority: - 1) To serve as Chair of the City Commission including the authority to procedurally manage and administer commission meetings and ruling - on points of order which are final unless overridden by the City Commission. - 2) To establish time limits for speakers. - 3) To represent the City Commission m negotiations, meetings, and functions. - 4) To designate other Commissioners to represent the City Commission in negotiations, meetings, and functions except as otherwise designated bythe City Commission. - 5) To have the Chairpersons of Boards or Committees report to him or her on matters ofprocedure. - 6) To issue procedural orders in quasi-judicial matters. - 7) To rule on procedural motions byparties to quasi-judicial matters. - 8) To approve requests for time-certain matters. - 9) To appoint the Vice Mayor. To call special meetings of the City Commission consistent with Section 2-26 ofthe City Code. - 10) To exercise all other authority otherwise provided in the City Charter and/or City Code. - c) The Mayor's authority relating to procedural matters is subject to review by the City Commission.
