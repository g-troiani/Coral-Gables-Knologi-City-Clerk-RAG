That the Finance Director shall have the authority to include encumbrances outstanding as of September 30, 2018 and prior year one-time budgeted items ( capital and/ or operating) as a re - appropriation of funds in the 2018- 2019 fiscal year for the amount of the encumbrances and/ or prior year budget. SECTION 5. That the Finance Director shall have the authority to reallocate the appropriation for expenditures contained herein within funds or between funds, or as may be hereafter adjusted by the City Commission, among the various line item accounts so long as the total appropriation for expenditures is not exceeded except as amended per Sections 6 and 7 below. SECTION 6. That the Finance Director shall have the authority to transfer unappropriated funds between operating, capital, proprietary and internal service funds as necessary and appropriate during the prior fiscal year-end closing process, as well as subsequent to the adoption of the Fiscal Year 2018-2019 Annual Budget for the continuity of City operations so long as the total appropriation for expenditures is not exceeded except as amended per Sections 6 and 7 below. SECTION 7. That the Finance Director shall be authorized to amend the budget to recognize and appropriate revenue and expenditure items e. g., donations, interest earnings from capital project debt proceeds, temporary fronting of future fiscal year early purchases, immediate replacement of totaled vehicles, etc., necessary for the continuity of City Operations. SECTION 8. That all other budget amendments to recognize increased revenue and expense appropriations occurring subsequent to the adoption of the Fiscal Year 2018- 2019 Annual Budget must be approved by resolution of the City Commission. SECTION 9. That\_ the City Manager has the full authority to utilize salary surplus generated by vacancies existing on October 1, 2018 as well as surplus generated by vacancies occurring subsequent to October 1, 2018 to cover the cost of temporary staff hired to support or enhance the recruitment process or to aid in the continuity of City Operations. SECTION 10. That if any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, word, or amount of this ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid by competent authority, then the remainder of this ordinance shall not be affected thereby and shall remain in full force and effect. SECTION 11, That this Ordinance shall become effective October 1, 2018. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- FIFTH DAY OF SEPTEMBER A.D., 2018.
