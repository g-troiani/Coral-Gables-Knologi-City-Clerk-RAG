The City Commission may approve a waiver pursuant to this subsection B.11. upon finding that the waiver will neither ( A) harm the public interest nor B) create parking problems in the area surrounding the applicant' s project site. - b. Requirements that may be waived. If the Director of Development Services reviews and rejects a remote parking application on the basis of any of the following requirements, then an applicant may request that the City Commission review its application for remote parking and, following a public hearing, approve a waiver of one or more of these requirements, and may impose any conditions it deems necessary on such waiver: - i. the 1,000 foot maximum distance between the remote parking spaces and the applicant' s project site; and - ii. the requirement that the remote parking be located in the CBD; and - iii. the requirement that the land containing the use seeking to utilize remote parking be located in the CBD. - c. Effect of waiver. All of the remaining requirements of section 5-140S.B., that have not been waived by the City Commission, must be satisfied. - 12. Appeals. The applicant may appeal any determinations made by the Development Services Director under this subsection through the process set forth in Article 3, Division 6 of the Zoning Code. ## 5-1409.
