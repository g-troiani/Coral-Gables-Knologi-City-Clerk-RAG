--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-130 - 07_22_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:18:01 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014-130 RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE USE OF CITY HALL, AND WAIVING THE SPECIAL EVENTS APPLICATION PERMIT FEE AND PARKING FEES FOR THE ADJACENT PARKING LOT FOR THE HISTORIC PRESERVATION ASSOCIATION OF CORAL GABLES FUNDRAISER ON SATURDAY, OCTOBER 18, 2014. WHEREAS, the Historic Preservation Association of Coral Gables ( the " Association"), a Florida non- profit founded in 1991, is dedicated to the preservation, restoration and use of historic properties in Coral Gables; and WHEREAS, the City has provided support in the past, and the Association is requesting the use of City Hall on Saturday, October 18, 2014 for a fundraiser from 6 p.m. to 10 p.m.; and WHEREAS, the Association is also requesting waiving the Special Events Application Permit fee, and parking fees for public spaces in the surface lot adjacent to City Hall for event attendees; and WHEREAS, the Special Events Application Permit fee is $ 809. 00 and the parking fees are estimated to total $ 335. 00 for a total fee waiver of$I,144. 00; and WHEREAS, the Association shall be responsible for providing the appropriate security loff-duty officers and insurance; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission hereby authorizes the use of City Hall, and waives the Special Events Application Permit fee and parking fees for October 18,2014, from 6 p.m. to 10 p.m. for the Historic Preservation Association of Coral Gables. SECTION 3. That the Association will meet all other requirements of the special events application and permit including security and insurance. SECTION 4. That this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- SECOND DAY OF JULY, A.D., 2014.
