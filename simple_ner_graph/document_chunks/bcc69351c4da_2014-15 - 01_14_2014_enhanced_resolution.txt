WHEREAS, the Palace Management Group, LLC ("Palace") approached the City with a letter ofintent (" Letter ofIntent") proposing the sale ofspace ofapproximately 9,741 square feet ( the " Center Space") with the parking garage of their facility at 83 Andalusia Avenue, Coral Gables, Florida; and WHEREAS, the City plans to use the Center Space as a community center for senior citizens; and WHEREAS, if an agreement is reached, the Center Space would be released from that Parking Garage Lease and Development Agreement between the Palace and the City dated July 14, 2000, as amended so that the City would own the space free and clear ofthe Palace's long-term leasehold interest; and prior to transfer of the Center Space, the Palace would: bump out the walls as shown on the plan ( the " Plan") attached to the Letter of Intent; install building standard exterior doors and windows; install air handlers and compressors sufficient to accommodate the HVAC system in the Center Space; install a fire sprinkler system with the exception of sprinkler heads; bring electricity to the Center Space; install sanitary sewer and water lines to and through the Center Space; and pour concrete flooring; and WHEREAS, the City has obtained two appraisals for the Center Space: on December 31,2013, Blazejack &amp; Co. appraised the Center Space as modified at $ 3,122,581 and on January 6, 2014, Waronker &amp; Rosen, Inc. appraised the Center Space as modified at $ 3 million; and WHEREAS, the City would pay to the Palace $ 3 Million on delivery of the Center Space with all the aforementioned work completed, which price is supported by both appraisals; and WHEREAS, in addition to releasing the Center Space to the City and as part of their consideration for the transaction, the Palace would provide the patrons of the City Senior Center, subject to reasonable rules and guidelines, access to the gymnasium, the movement center and the pool at least 2 times per week for a period of2 hours for each ofthe amenities, and in addition, the Palace will provide the City with three assigned parking spaces for staff; 35 unassigned parking spaces for patrons; and the following parking for events: up to 100 spaces per month for the monthly luncheon; up to 250 spaces per year for the annual luncheon; up to 150 spaces per year for the annual senior art show; and up to 100 spaces per 3 year for the annual holiday party.
