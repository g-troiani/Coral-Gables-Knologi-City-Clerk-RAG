Building Official and Code Enforcement Division; Powers. - a) During a state ofemergency, the Building Official has full jurisdiction over any construction site and can give any orders deemed appropriate which can be enforced by the City Attorney or Code Enforcement. - b) Hurricane Inspections - 1. During a state ofemergency or once the five-day forecast cone from the National Hurricane Center predicts that the City will be impacted by a tropical storm or hurricane, whichever comes first, best efforts shall be used by owners and/or any occupants ofreal property to secure all furniture, display racks, materials and similar loose objects in exposed outdoor locations, including loose materials or equipment at construction sites that must be secured or otherwise appropriately braced to rigid construction or stored in buildings to the extent practicable, given the conditions. The City shall give oral or written orders to any person on the premises most logically responsible for maintenance and such orders shall be carried out before winds of tropical storm velocity begin. In the event ofconstruction, the City shall also attempt to give notice to the contractor, ifpracticable. - 2. Failure to comply with the requirements in (1) will subject the property owner and/or contractor to the following fines: - 1.
