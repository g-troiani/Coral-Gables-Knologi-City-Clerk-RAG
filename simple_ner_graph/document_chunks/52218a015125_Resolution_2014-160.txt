WHEREAS, portions of State of Florida designated roadways ( State Road S.R.) 5/South Dixie Highway, S.R. 976/Bird Road, and S.R. 959/Red Road) are located within the City ofCoral Gables; and WHEREAS, these roadways contain median and roadside areas that require maintenance; and WHEREAS, the State of Florida and the City recognize the need for entering into an agreement designating and setting forth the responsibilities of each party with regard to the maintenance ofthe landscaping associated with these areas; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing " WHEREAS" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Manager is hereby authorized on behalf of the City to execute a Turf and Landscape Maintenance Joint Participation Agreement with the Florida Department of Transportation, Financial Project # 252311-2-78-02, for the maintenance ofState Road (S.R.) 5/South Dixie Highway, S.R. 976IBird Road, and S.R. 959IRed Road within Coral Gables. SECTION 3. That term ofthe Agreement shall be for one ( 1) year and may be renewed twice and further extended for an additional period not to exceed 6 months. SECTION 4. That the executed agreement shall be made a part of this resolution and kept on file in the office ofthe City Clerk. SECTION 5. That this resolution shall become effective upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY- SIXTH DAY OF AUGUST, A.D., 2014.
