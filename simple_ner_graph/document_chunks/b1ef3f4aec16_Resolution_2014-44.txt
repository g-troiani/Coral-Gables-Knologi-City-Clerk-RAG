--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-44 - 03_11_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:08:12 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014-44 RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES TO REQUEST AN AMENDMENT TO THE 2012 URBAN AREAS SECURITY INITIATIVE (UASI) PROJECTS LIST. WHEREAS, Resolution No. 2013-291, passed and adopted by the City Commission on December 10, 2013, approved the Memorandum of Agreement entered between the City of Miami and the City of Coral Gables for the purpose of carrying out Fiscal Year 2012 Urban Areas Security Initiatives (UASI) program projects; and WHEREAS, Resolution No. 2013-291 identified approved UASI projects submitted by the City of Coral Gables Fire and Police Departments; and WHEREAS, these projects included Medical and First Responder training patient decontamination, medical stabilization, and scene management); and WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables Fire and Police Departments support and seek to amend the 2012 Urban Areas Security Initiative ( UASI) Projects List to fund Mass Casualty kits and ballistic equipment for Active Shooter emergencies in the amount of $ 63,499.60 in order to enhance the City of Coral Gables' ability to respond to a terrorist threat or act; and WHEREAS, this Resolution supports the Fire and Police Departments' efforts to amend the 2012 UASI Project Update Worksheet; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1: The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2: That the City of Coral Gables, including its Fire and Police Departments, is hereby authorized to request an amendment to the 2012 Urban Areas Security Initiative ( UASI) Projects List to fund Mass Casualty kits and ballistic equipment for Active Shooter emergencies projects in the amount of $ 63,499.60 in order to enhance the City ofCoral Gables' ability to respond to a terrorist threat or act. SECTION 3: That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS ELEVENTH DAY OF MARCH, A.D., 2014.
