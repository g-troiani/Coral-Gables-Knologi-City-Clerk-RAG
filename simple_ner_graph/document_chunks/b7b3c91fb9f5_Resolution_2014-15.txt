--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-15 - 01_14_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 00:32:07 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014- 15 RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY MANAGER TO ENTER INTO NEGOTIATIONS WITH THE PALACE MANAGEMENT GROUP, LLC REGARDING PURCHASE OF THE GROUND FLOOR SQUARE FOOTAGE AT THE PARKING GARAGE AT 83 ANDALUSIA AVENUE. WHEREAS, the Palace Management Group, LLC ("Palace") approached the City with a letter ofintent (" Letter ofIntent") proposing the sale ofspace ofapproximately 9,741 square feet ( the " Center Space") with the parking garage of their facility at 83 Andalusia Avenue, Coral Gables, Florida; and WHEREAS, the City plans to use the Center Space as a community center for senior citizens; and WHEREAS, if an agreement is reached, the Center Space would be released from that Parking Garage Lease and Development Agreement between the Palace and the City dated July 14, 2000, as amended so that the City would own the space free and clear ofthe Palace's long-term leasehold interest; and prior to transfer of the Center Space, the Palace would: bump out the walls as shown on the plan ( the " Plan") attached to the Letter of Intent; install building standard exterior doors and windows; install air handlers and compressors sufficient to accommodate the HVAC system in the Center Space; install a fire sprinkler system with the exception of sprinkler heads; bring electricity to the Center Space; install sanitary sewer and water lines to and through the Center Space; and pour concrete flooring; and WHEREAS, the City has obtained two appraisals for the Center Space: on December 31,2013, Blazejack &amp; Co. appraised the Center Space as modified at $ 3,122,581 and on January 6, 2014, Waronker &amp; Rosen, Inc. appraised the Center Space as modified at $ 3 million; and WHEREAS, the City would pay to the Palace $ 3 Million on delivery of the Center Space with all the aforementioned work completed, which price is supported by both appraisals; and WHEREAS, in addition to releasing the Center Space to the City and as part of their consideration for the transaction, the Palace would provide the patrons of the City Senior Center, subject to reasonable rules and guidelines, access to the gymnasium, the movement center and the pool at least 2 times per week for a period of2 hours for each ofthe amenities, and in addition, the Palace will provide the City with three assigned parking spaces for staff; 35 unassigned parking spaces for patrons; and the following parking for events: up to 100 spaces per month for the monthly luncheon; up to 250 spaces per year for the annual luncheon; up to 150 spaces per year for the annual senior art show; and up to 100 spaces per 3 year for the annual holiday party. At the City's option, parking may be by valet provided by the Palace rather than self-parking; and WHEREAS, the City will not permit persons engaged in development, operation or management of senior living facilities other than the Palace to participate in the operation or management ofthe Center Space; and WHEREAS, by taking action, the Commission is authorizing the City to negotiate an agreement with the Palace, but there is no legally binding agreement on either party until any proposed agreement complies with the City's Procurement process, and the City Commission votes to accept the final agreement, ifit chooses to do so; and WHEREAS, each party will bear its own professional fees; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That the foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Manager is hereby authorized to enter into negotiations with the Palace for the Center Space, with such negotiations to be non-binding unless and until a final document is presented to the City Commission and is approved. The City may discontinue negotiations at any time without any obligation or penalty prior to a fully executed agreement. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective immediately upon the date of its passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FOURTEENTH DAY OF JANUARY, A.D., 2014.
