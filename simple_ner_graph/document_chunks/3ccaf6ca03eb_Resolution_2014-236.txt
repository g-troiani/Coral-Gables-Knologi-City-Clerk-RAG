--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2014-236 - 10_28_2014.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:28:00 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## RESOLUTION NO. 2014-236 A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING EXPENDITURES IN THE AMOUNT OF 36,170, FROM FEDERAL FORFEITED ASSET FUND ( F.A.F.) MONIES FOR THE ACQUISITION OF ADDITIONAL LAW ENFORCEMENT EQUIPMENT; THE VERIPIC DIGITAL EVIDENCE MANAGER AND ASSOCIATED HARDWARE FOR UTILIZATION BY THE CORAL GABLES POLICE DEPARTMENT. WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Police Department participates with federal law enforcement agencies of the United States Departments of Justice, Treasury and/or Homeland Security in joint law enforcement operations, and the Coral Gables Police Department' s participation in those joint investigations produces federally seized funds; and WHEREAS, at the discretion ofthe United States Attorney General under United States Statute 18 U.S.C. Section 981(e)(2) and 21 U.S.C. Section 881(e)(l)( a) and ( e)(3)(b), the Coral Gables Police Department may lawfully retain at the conclusion of the legal proceedings the federally seized funds; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Interim Chief of Police, Edward J. Hudak, Jr., and the Coral Gables Finance Director, Diana Gomez, attest to having deposited the sum of $ 36,170 in said forfeited funds into the Coral Gables Forfeited Asset Fund; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Interim ChiefofPolice requests to expend these funds for use by the Department, to acquire additional law enforcement equipment; and WHEREAS, the Coral Gables Interim Chief ofPolice certifies that the request complies with all provisions of the appropriate F.A.F. statutory requirements as identified in the United States Justice Department's publication entitled " Guide to Equitable Sharing for State and Local Law Enforcement Agencies"; and WHEREAS, the F.A.F. Committee met on October 15, 2014, and followed the F.A.F. Committee operating procedures adopted on July 23, 2002; and WHEREAS, the F . A.F. Committee approved the Coral Gables Interim Chief ofPolice's request to expend the available forfeited asset funds on the specific items listed below in this resolution; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Manager is hereby directed to authorize the Finance Director to expend $ 36,170 to the Coral Gables Police Department from Federal Forfeited Asset Fund monies to provide for the acquisition of additional law enforcement equipment; the VeriPic Digital Evidence Manager and associated hardware for utilization by the Coral Gables Police Department. herein. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption 2014.
