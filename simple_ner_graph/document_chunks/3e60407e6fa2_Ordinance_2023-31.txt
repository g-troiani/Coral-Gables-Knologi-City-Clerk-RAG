That for the fiscal year beginning October 1, 2023, the annual compensation inclusive of the annual CPI adjustment) of the five members of the City Commission shall be $69,000 for the mayor, $ 67,000 for the vice-mayor, and $ 65,000 for each of the three commissioners; the monthly local expense allowance shall be adjusted to $ 800 monthly for each member of the City Commission); and a monthly car allowance shall be provided for each member of the City Commission in the amount of $703.84. SECTION 11. That the City Manager has the full authority to utilize salary surplus generated by vacancies existing on October 1, 2023 as well as surplus generated by vacancies occurring subsequent to October 1, 2023 to cover the cost of temporary staff hired to support or enhance the recruitment process or to aid in the continuity of City Operations. SECTION 12. That if any section, subsection, sentence, clause, phrase, word, or amount of this ordinance shall be declared unconstitutional or invalid by competent authority, then the remainder of this ordinance shall not be affected thereby and shall remain in full force and effect. SECTION 13. That this Ordinance shall be effective October 1, 2023. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY-SIXTH DAY OF SEPTEMBER A.D., 2023.
