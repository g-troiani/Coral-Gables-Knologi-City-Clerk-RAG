WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables has over forty miles of public, navigable waterways and coastal areas, which present a host ofunique issues not found in other parts ofthe City; and WHEREAS, presently the City has Boards or Committees in place which assist City staffin the oversight and maintenance ofCity infrastructure; and WHEREAS, the City Commission has recognized a gap in City infrastructure with respect to the City's waterways and wishes to enact a Committee tasked with addressing the unique concerns of the City waterways and coastal areas, the residents who live along the waterways and coastal areas, and those who regularly use the waterways and coastal areas; and WHEREAS, the City Commission, given the City's unique positioning in the State, believes it can have a positive impact on sustainability practices within the waterways and coastal areas through the enactment ofthis Committee; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE CITY COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. INCORPORATION. That the foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2. CREATION AND PURPOSE. That the Waterway Advisory Board is hereby created for the purpose of identifying and recommending improvement opportunities to City staff with respect to the Coral Gables' public, navigable waterways and coastal areas. The Board shall make nonbinding policy recommendations specifically related to the health, safety, maintenance and sustainability ofthe Coral Gables' waterways. Moreover, the Board shall seek to ensure that the waterways and coastal areas remain accessible for recreational use and enjoyment ofall. SECTION 3. MEMBERSHIP. The Board shall consist ofnine (9) Members, five (5) of which shall be appointed by the members ofthe Commission each appointing one (1) member, two ( 2) members shall be appointed by the Commission-as-a-whole, one ( 1) member shall be appointed by the Board-as-a-whole, and the remaining one (1) member shall be appointed by the City Manager. All appointed members shall be confirmed bythe City Commission. In appointing members, the City Commission and City Manager shall ensure that each waterway or coastal area throughout the City is represented by a Board member. SECTION 4. QUALIFICATIONS. That eight of the the Members ofthe Board shall be City residents residing along a City waterway for a period greater than six (6) months prior to their appointment. Of the eight Members ofthe Board, one Member shall reside along each of the following waterways: ( 1) Mahi; ( 2) Coral Gables; ( 3) Entrada Channel; ( 4) Gables Estates; 5) Matheson Hammocks; ( 6) Snapper Creek; ( 7) Gables by the Sea; and ( 8) Deering Bay.
