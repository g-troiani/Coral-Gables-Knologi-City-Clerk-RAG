WHEREAS, during the April 16, 2014, Special City Commission Meeting, the City Commission requested of the City Administration to expedite the search for a firm to provide professional executive search services for the position ofCity Manager; and WHEREAS, on May, 5, 2014, the City Commission reviewed three top executive recruitment firms, namely, Colin Baenziger &amp; Associates, Mercer and Slavin, and WHEREAS, the executive search firm of Colin Baenziger &amp; Associates was selected to undertake the city's recruitment process for a City Manager; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission does hereby select the recruitment firm ofColin Baenziger &amp; Associates, to conduct the City'S recruitment process for a City Manager. SECTION 3. That this resolution shall become effective immediately upon the date ofits passage and adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FIFTH DAY OF MAY, A.D., 2014.
