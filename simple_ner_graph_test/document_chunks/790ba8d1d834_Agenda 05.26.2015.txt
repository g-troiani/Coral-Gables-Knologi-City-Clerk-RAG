Commercial Land Uses,' pursuant to expedited State review procedures (S.163.3184, Florida Statutes) and Zoning Code Article 3, 'Development Review,' Division 15, 'Comprehensive Plan Text and Map Amendments;' amending the 'Commercial High-Rise Intensity,' 'Commercial Mid-Rise Intensity,' and 'Commercial Low-Rise Intensity' Land Use Classifications to provide that (A) residential use shall be permitted and (B) intensity shall be controlled by a Planned Area Development plan instead of by Floor Area Ratio, in a project developed in accordance with the Mediterranean Village Form-Based Planned Area Development; and further amending the 'Commercial High-Rise Intensity' and 'Commercial Mid-Rise Intensity' Land Use Classifications to provide that, in such a Mediterranean Village project, additional height may be granted for specified uses or architectural embellishment; providing for a repealer provision, severability clause and providing for an effective date.
