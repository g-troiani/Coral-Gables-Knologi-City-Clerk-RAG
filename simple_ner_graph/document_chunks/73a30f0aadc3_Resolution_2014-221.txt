11. Public Meetings. That all meetings of the Committee shall be open to the public, and a written summary of the proceedings shall be kept showing its action on each question considered, and such record shall be filed in the office of the Secretary and submitted to the City Commission. SECTION 12. Written Reports. That the City-Wide Anti-Crime Committee shall submit an annual report to the City Commission in accordance Division 1, Section 2-66. SECTION 13. Compliance with Board and Committee Rules. That the Committee shall be governed by any and all legislation enacted by the Commission; as well as any applicable administrative policies that govern the functions of City boards and committees; including, but not limited the provisions of the City Code City SECTION 14. Costs. That all costs incurred for notifications, andlor courtesy advertisements, and other items necessary to conduct the business of the Committee shall be borne by the City . SECTION 15. Effective Date. That this Resolution shall become effective as of October 14th, 2014. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS FOURTEENTH DAY OF OCTOBER A.D., 2014.
