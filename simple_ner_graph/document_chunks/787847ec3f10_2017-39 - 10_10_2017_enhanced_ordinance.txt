WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 2017-30, enumerating the duties and powers of the Office ofMayor was adopted bythe City Commission on July 11, 2017; and WHEREAS, it is equally useful and appropriate to adopt an Ordinance setting out the duties and powers of each member of the City Commission ( which includes the Mayor and Vice Mayor); and WHEREAS, Commissioners have customarily exercised the authority of the Office ofCommissioner, but it would be useful to codify this authority to provide clarity on the particular scope ofthat authority and to add additional powers where appropriate; ## BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. That Section 2-29 of the Code of the City of Coral Gables, Florida, is hereby created to read as follows: ## Section 2-29. Commissioners; Duties and Powers - a) Function. This ordinance specifies the duties and powers of each member of the City Commission, which includes the Office of the Mayor and Vice Mayor and which will be referred to as " City Commissioner" herein. - b) Duties. Each City Commissioner shall have the following authority: - 1) To sponsor and place items on the City Commission meeting agenda, including proposed Ordinances and Resolutions; - 2) To inquire ofand receive information from City staff; - 3) To request Sunshine meetings consistent with established procedure; - 4) To use the City seal and his/her official title in the performance ofCity business; - 5) To have an office budget in an amount determined in the City budget; - 6) To serve in his/her official capacity as a member of other boards and/or committees, upon which the City has a seat, when appointed by the Mayor or City Commission; - 7) To request and rely upon Ethics Opinions issued by the City Attorney' s Office; - 8) To appear before City Boards and Committees; - 9) To propose to fellow Commissioners, at a City Commission meeting, the initiation of Chapter 164, F.S.
