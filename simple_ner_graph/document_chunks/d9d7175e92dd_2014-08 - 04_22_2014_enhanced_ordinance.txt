WHEREAS, City Staff is requesting Zoning Code text amendments to Article III Development Review" of the Zoning Code to modify existing procedures and create new procedures to facilitate the resolution of disputes involving the City and other governmental agencies that impact the City's Zoning Code; and WHEREAS, the City's application of its zoning and land development regulations and related rules, policies, and practices must also be consistent with constantly evolving requirements involving the rights of applicants and affected parties under the Civil Rights Act of 1964, the Religious Land Use and Institutionalized Persons Act (" RLUIPA"), the First, Fifth, and Fourteenth Amendments to the U.S. Constitution, the Fair Housing Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act, and other applicable state and federal laws and regulations relating to the protection ofthe public health, safety and welfare; and WHEREAS, the City Commission intends to amend the Zoning Code in order to enable the City to resolve claims related to possible violations of state and federal laws and regulations, and to avoid or settle costly litigation or administrative proceedings, while maintaining consistency with and providing appropriate relief under the City's Zoning Code; and WHEREAS, the City Commission finds that it is in the public interest to establish separate standards and procedures within the Zoning Code in order to facilitate the resolution ofthese claims; and WHEREAS, the codification of these standards and procedures will permit the City and qualifying individuals or entities, where warranted based on the facts and law, to avoid or settle costly judicial or administrative litigation, ensure compliance with applicable law, and provide appropriate relief under the City'S Zoning Code; and WHEREAS, the zoning relief procedure is consistent with applicable case law and the legislative history of the subject laws and legal precedent, which encourage local governments to provide an accommodation procedure; and WHEREAS, the City Commission determines that the adoption ofthis Ordinance is in the best interests ofthe health, safety and welfare ofits residents and citizens; and WHEREAS, after notice being duly published, a public hearing was held before the Planning and Zoning Board on March 12, 2014, at which hearing all interested persons were afforded the opportunity to be heard;
