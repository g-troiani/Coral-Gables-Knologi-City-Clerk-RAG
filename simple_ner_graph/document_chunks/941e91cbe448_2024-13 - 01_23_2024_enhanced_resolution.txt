WHEREAS, in partnership with the City of Coral Gables' Sustainability Division and Economic Development Department, the Historical Resources and Cultural Arts Department accepted a proposal form Blue Missions to develop and install a temporary public art installation opening on World Water Day, and continuing through Earth Day into April 2024; and WHEREAS, founded in 2010, BLUE Missions is a 501c3 nonprofit organization that is dedicated to ensuring clearwater access through international sustainable development projects and local educational outreach; and WHEREAS, 'The Water Below Us' is a public awareness campaign using public art to draw attention toward the protection of our local water sources; and WHEREAS, designed by local artist collective, Vaco Studios and The Sunken Blimp, the installation will illustrate the interconnection of water and all that lives through visual art and an augmented reality (AR) experience; and WHEREAS, design continues and approval of this resolution is contingent upon approval of the art design through the Historical Resources and Cultural Arts Department; and WHEREAS, funding of $50,000 for the temporary public art activation is proposed through the art fund; and WHEREAS, an amendment to the Fiscal Year 2023-2024 Annual Budget is required to recognize the $50,000 transfer from the Art In Public Places reserve as revenue and to appropriate such funds to be used towards the project; and WHEREAS, the Cultural Development Board voted unanimously ( 6-0) recommending support of the public art installation ' The Water Below Us' in partnership with Blue Missions at their meeting on December 19, 2023; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OFCORAL GABLES AS FOLLOWS: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Resolution upon the adoption hereof. SECTION 2. The City Commission does hereby approve support of the public art installation ' The Water Below Us', contingent upon approval of the art design through the Historical Resources and Cultural Arts Department.
