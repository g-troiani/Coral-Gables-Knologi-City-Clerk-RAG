SECTION 1. That the City Commission does hereby approve one more position to the City's employee head count, in furtherance ofthe establishment of an additional Commission Aide position. SECTION 2. That the City Clerk will conduct the interview and selection process for the Commission Aide position and provide two ( 2) to three ( 3) finalists that the affected four ( 4) City Commissioners may possibly meet with, ifdesired. SECTION 3. That the City Manager and City Clerk move quickly in connection with the recruitment effort for said position and with identifying any available space options. SECTION 4. That is resolution shall become effective upon the date ofits adoption herein. PASSED AND ADOPTED THIS TWENTY -SIXTH DAY OF AUGUST, A.D., 2014.
