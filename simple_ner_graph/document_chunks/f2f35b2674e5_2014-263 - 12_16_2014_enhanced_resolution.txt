WHEREAS, the City's current on-site vehicle and equipment parts and supply services contract with Genuine Parts Company d/b/a NAPA Auto Parts will expire December 22, 2014, which is a piggyback contract based on a NAPA contract with the City ofMiramar, FL; and WHEREAS, to replace this contract, Procurement is recommending participation in a cooperative purchasing agreement through the National Joint Powers Alliance NJPA) under Section 2-980 ofthe Procurement Code; and WHEREAS, under the proposed NJPA Cooperative contract, the pricing method to provide vehicle and equipment parts and supplies will be Cost + Expenses + 10%, which is the same pricing method used under the current NAPA piggyback contract, and is based upon NAPA's actual acquisition cost, that is defined as the parts price NAPA charges from their distribution warehouse to their outlying stores; and WHEREAS, under the above methodology, the total sale price can be calculated and verified by comparing the base acquisitions price plus a 10% profit; and WHEREAS, NJPA is a public purchasing cooperative set-up by the State of Minnesota with over 50,000 member agencies nationwide, which the City of Coral Gables is also a member; and WHEREAS, the NJPA contract with J~APA was competitively solicited by the National Joint Power Alliance under RFP No. 031212, which complies with the solicitation requirements contained with Section 2-978 ofthe Procurement Code; and WHEREAS, Section 2-650 of the Procurement Code authorizes the City Manager to delegate authority to the Chief Procurement Officer to administer and make recommendations on City contracts; ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES: SECTION 1. The foregoing " Whereas" clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part ofthis Resolution upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That the City Commission does hereby accept the recommendation ofthe Chief Procurement Officer to authorize a " Piggyback" contract with Genuine Parts Company d/b/a NAPA Auto Parts from the National Joint Powers Alliance ( NJPA) to provide on- site vehicle and equipment parts and supply services pursuant to Section 2980 of the Procuren1ent Code for a period running through April, 12, 2016, with an option to renew for one ( 1) additional year, subject to final approval and execution of a contract by City staff. SECTION 3. That this Resolution shall become effective upon the date of its adoption herein. 2014.
