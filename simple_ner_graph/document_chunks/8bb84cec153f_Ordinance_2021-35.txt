--- DOCUMENT METADATA AND CONTEXT ============================= **DOCUMENT IDENTIFICATION:** - Document Type: DOCUMENT - Source File: 2021-35 - 10_12_2021.pdf - Processed Stage: Stage 1 (OCR Extraction) - Conversion Date: 2025-07-05 01:26:11 **DOCUMENT CONTENT:** ==================== ## CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA ## ORDINANCE NO. 2021-35 AN ORDINANCE OF THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES, FLORIDA, AMENDING THE CITY OF CORAL GABLES CODE BY CREATING CHAPTER 2 ' ADMINISTRATION', ARTICLE III ' BOARDS AND COMMITTEES', DIVISION FEMA COMMUNITY RATING SYSTEM PROGRAM ACTIVITY 330 PROGRAM FOR PUBLIC INFORMATION COMMITTEE' PROVIDING FOR A REPEALER PROVISION, SEVERABILITY CLAUSE, CODIFICATION, AND AN EFFECTIVE DATE. WHEREAS, the City of Coral Gables lies along various waterways and Biscayne Bay and is vulnerable to tropical weather events and flood hazards that can damage public and private property; and WHEREAS, the City participates in FEMA's Community Rating System ( CRS) Program, which offers flood insurance policy premium discounts in communities like the City of Coral Gables that have developed and implemented extra measures beyond minimum floodplain management requirements to provide its residences protection from floods; and WHEREAS, the City Commission wishes to establish a ' FEMA Community Rating System Program Activity 330 Program for Public Information Committee' ( The PPI Committee) in accordance with FEMA' s CRS Program Activity 330 Outreach Projects and policies and applicable laws and regulations; and WHEREAS, the FEMA Program for Public Information is an ongoing public information campaign that allows a community through a committee to design and transmit messages that the committee determines are most important to its flood safety and the protection of floodplains' natural functions; and WHEREAS, the City Commission believes that the addition of a PPI Committee will provide valuable flood outreach materials to property owners within the City and to help lower the cost of flood insurance for policy holders as part of the City' s participation in FEMA' s CRS program; and WHEREAS, section 2-57 of the City Code provides that the second reading of an ordinance creating a new board shall be held no earlier than 15 days after passage on the first reading of the ordinance creating said board, however, requirements from the Community Rating System Program and the important of the board require the City Commission to hear this Ordinance on second reading 14 days from its initial passage, as such the City Commission is explicitly waiving the relevant requirement in Section 2-57 of the City Code. ## NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE COMMISSION OF THE CITY OFCORAL GABLES, FLORIDA: SECTION 1. That the foregoing ' WHEREAS' clauses are hereby ratified and confirmed as being true and correct and are hereby made a specific part of this Ordinance upon adoption hereof. SECTION 2. That Chapter 2, Article III- Boards and Committees of the City Code of Coral Gables, Florida, Division 8 ' FEMA Community Rating System Program Activity 330 Program for Public Information Committee' be created as indicated below. ## Chapter 2- Administration, Article III- Boards and Committees, Division 8 ' FEMA Community Rating System Program Activity 330 Program for Public Information Committee' Section 2-234. Creation and Purpose. That a FEMA community rating system program activity 330 program for public information committee ( hereinafter ' the PPI Committee') is hereby created in order to assist the City in assessing the City' s public information needs as it relates to floodplain management and to assist the City with all other tasks as indicated in those regulations and policies promulgated by FEMA' s CRS Program as may be amended from time to time. The PPI committee will meet at least once each calendar year. Section 2-235. Membership and Quorum. The PPI committee shall consist of five ( 5) members, all which shall be appointed by the City Manager. All appointed members should be confirmed by the city commission. A quorum shall consist of three (3) members. One (1) of the members shall be the City's public information officer; one ( 1) shall be the City's floodplain manager. Section 2-236. Qualifications. The members of the PPI committee appointed from outside the local government and local staff as cited in Section 2-235, shall represent each of the following areas of experience: real estate, banking, and property insurance. Section 2-237. Terms of Office, Appointment and Renewal. The PPI committee members' terms of office shall begin June 1 for a two-year period ending May 31st in odd numbered years. The initial committee members terms will begin retroactive to June 1, 2021 and run through May 31, 2023 or until a successor has been selected. and qualified. All appointments for replacement of any member shall be for the member's unexpired term.
