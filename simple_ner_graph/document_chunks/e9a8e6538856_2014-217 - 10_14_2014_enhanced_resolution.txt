WHEREAS, in April of 2005 the City of Coral Gables entered into an agreement with Florida International University ( F.LU.) and the Florida Center for Library Automation ( hereinafter referred to as FCLA), for the purpose of deployment of certain materials related to the City of Coral Gables as a digital library collection, entitled " Coral Gables Memory:" A Digital Collection from the City Beautiful, under the aegis of "Publication of Archival, Library and Museum Materials," ( hereinafter referred to as PALMM); and WHEREAS, to this end in furtherance ofthe Coral Gables Memory, the City of Coral Gables has jointly worked with the FlU Green Library in producing the following three digital projects: digitization of approximately 2,700 historical photographs covering the decade of the 1940s, a " Talking Book" project ( i.e. including audio narration), resulting in the digitization of 17 Coral Gables Legislative Minute Books ( approximately 4,700 pages) covering the time period between 1925 through 1942, which focuses on the early history of Coral Gables and is now serving as an interactive learning tool for students, the general public , and researchers alike that depicts those people, places and events that shaped our City in its early years; and finally the establishment of a " Virtual Historic City" project ( Phase I), in a geospatially enabling web environment by the Florida International University Library' s GIS Center and Digital Collection Center, along with Fill's Department ofLandscape Architecture, which resulted in the completion of the following tasks: - 1) Construction of a geo- spatial database and web publishing of a Virtual Historic Space;
